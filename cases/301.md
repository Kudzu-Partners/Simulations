## Abstract

InduMed S.R.L. was, in the case universe, a 19-year-old Bogotá medical-device distributor importing orthopaedic, cardiovascular and laparoscopic devices from three European principals and reselling into 340 Colombian clinic and hospital accounts across four coverage territories — year-to-date 2026 revenue on a COP 62bn run-rate, 24 direct field reps, year-to-date attainment 76 % against a plan that assumed 94 %, voluntary rep turnover 29 % in the preceding twelve months against a Colombian medical-device industry benchmark of 18 %, a commission-heavy compensation structure unchanged since 2007, and a pipeline in which 12 of the 340 accounts drove 74 % of revenue while receiving the same weekly-visit routine as the other 328. On Thursday, April 23, 2026, at 4:18 p.m., **Mariana Restrepo-Ocampo**, a 41-year-old National Sales Director, re-read the three-round workbook her commercial-operations lead had closed at 11:47 p.m. the previous night and prepared for the 7:00 p.m. executive-committee commitment. The case opens at that desk.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply the Churchill-Ford-Walker multiplicative sales-performance model — Performance = Role × Aptitude × Skill × Motivation × Environment — to diagnose why InduMed's 76 % attainment cannot be repaired by any single-dimension intervention and articulate which two dimensions the commission-structure change, the training allocation, the quota calibration and the territory redesign each move.
2. Calibrate the Round 1 quota curve against the documented 110 %-burnout-ceiling, 95-to-105 % sweet-spot and below-80 % complacency finding, and articulate why the ratchet-trap — setting conservative in year one to look generous and then re-anchoring to realised performance in year two — produces systematic sandbagging that degrades performance by the second year even under an unchanged compensation plan.
3. Design the Round 2 commission-plus-retention package for **Andrés Felipe Reyes** — the 132 %-attainment top performer MedSupply is pursuing — and articulate why the documented team-retention option (team-based bonus tied to regional growth) often dominates a purely individual-salary match on two-year-retention panels.
4. Trace the Round 3 account-tiering decision (12 strategic A-tier accounts driving 74 % of revenue, 52 B-tier accounts driving another 20 %, 276 C-tier accounts driving the residual 6 %) through the Zoltners-Sinha-Lorimer key-account-management framework, commit to two dedicated key-account managers pulled out of territories, and articulate why the Pareto-shape of the book forces a structural change the 2007 configuration never needed.

On the afternoon of **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 4:18 p.m., **Mariana Restrepo-Ocampo** closed the tab where she had been reviewing Andrés Felipe Reyes's pipeline — 132 % year-to-date, eleven A-tier oncology accounts, the MedSupply headhunter's LinkedIn message on his phone that the rep had forwarded to her two hours earlier with the one-line *"Directora, cuando pueda hablamos"* — and opened the GranTemporada workbook her commercial-operations lead had finalised at 11:47 p.m. the previous night. Past the window of her eighth-floor office in the Zona Empresarial del Chicó she could see the afternoon traffic stacking on Carrera Once. On the pin-board above the workbook sat the Ley 1474 anti-kickback summary, the commission-plan comparator her compensation consultant had drafted in January, the Colombian-Association-of-Medical-Device-Distributors 2025 benchmark report showing InduMed's turnover at 29 % against industry 18, and the MedSupply-poach pattern memo from the chief commercial officer tracking the three offer approaches over the previous thirty days. The 7:00 p.m. executive-committee commitment was two hours and forty-two minutes away.

---

## The Protagonist

Restrepo-Ocampo, 41, had been National Sales Director since January 2024, had completed an Administración de Empresas at the Universidad de los Andes in 2006 and an MBA at the IESE Business School Barcelona in 2013, had spent eight years at Johnson & Johnson Medical Devices in Bogotá and Buenos Aires commercial roles before returning to Colombia in 2021 as Regional Sales Director at a multinational orthopaedic peer, and had joined InduMed in January 2024 with an explicit mandate from the founder-CEO to "profesionalizar la fuerza de ventas sin romperla." Her sales-management instincts had been shaped by three teachings: the 2012–2013 IESE exposure to the Zoltners-Sinha-Lorimer *Accelerating Sales Force Performance* canon; the Johnson & Johnson regional discipline around Churchill-Ford-Walker diagnostic models and 95-to-105 % quota calibration; and a 2022 failed commission redesign at her previous employer where a purely individual-match response to a competitor poach attempt had cost the firm the top three reps within eleven months despite above-market cash offers.

---

## The Decision

Restrepo-Ocampo had to commit, by the 7:00 p.m. executive-committee call, the three-round sales-force package.

1. **Round 1 — Design.** Reallocate the 24 reps across the four coverage territories. Set quota per territory at 80-to-120 % of theoretical potential. Select one of six compensation-plan variants (ranging from 100 % fixed through 60/40 base-plus-commission with accelerators at quota attainment). Allocate up to COP 400M training budget across product knowledge, consultative-selling, CRM discipline and clinical-language capability. Name two-to-eight hunters out of the twenty-four.
2. **Round 2 — Performance Management.** Rule on Andrés Felipe Reyes retention against the MedSupply approach. Rule on the Costa Atlántica territory under 61 % attainment for three consecutive quarters — performance-improvement plan, reassignment or termination. Rule on the Hospital Universitario de Bogotá enterprise opportunity — dedicated key-account manager or existing rep. Rule on two compliance-audit expense-claim exceedances above the COP 150,000 anti-kickback ceiling — warn, fine or terminate each.
3. **Round 3 — Strategic Account Growth.** Tier the 340-account book into A, B and C. Pull two reps out of territories and dedicate them as key-account managers. Commit the MedSupply-poach response across the top three reps — match-individually, team-retain or accept-and-replace. Reset quotas for 2027 without ratcheting on 2026 realised performance.

Commitments: the Round 1 design package; the Round 2 four rulings; the Round 3 tiering, KAM, poach response and quota reset; and a one-page executive-committee memo that frames the three-round commitment as a sales-system redesign rather than a commission tweak.

## Sectoral Background

The 2018–2025 Colombian medical-device distribution category had, in the case universe, grown from approximately COP 8.4bn to COP 14.2bn in aggregate B2B revenue, concentrated in three operator tiers (multinational distributors like Johnson & Johnson, Medtronic and Stryker direct arms; mid-market import-and-distribute players like InduMed; smaller regional-specialty distributors). The 340-clinic-and-hospital Colombian addressable market had absorbed a documented two-point-four-percentage-point year-on-year shift toward specialty-differentiated field-rep coverage during 2022–2025, concentrated in the top-quartile hospital segment where the Ley 1474 compliance ceiling and the documented clinical-language capability gap had reshaped the rep-to-clinician interaction. The 2023–2025 MedSupply Andina competitor entry — a Medellín-based distributor backed by a Bogotá private-equity partner — had matched a documented rep-poach pattern across the Colombian commercial-specialty market, with three approaches in the preceding thirty days at InduMed alone.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1990–2025 sales-force-management literature produced three findings the simulation had to respect. First, the Churchill-Ford-Walker multiplicative performance model — Performance = Role × Aptitude × Skill × Motivation × Environment — operationalised in the 1990 meta-analysis and replicated in multiple Latin American panels, documented that a rep at perfect aptitude and zero motivation produced zero; a miscalibrated quota killed the motivation term and the equation collapsed regardless of training investment. Second, the Zoltners-Sinha-Lorimer *Accelerating Sales Force Performance* quota-calibration finding — the 95-to-105 % sweet spot where attainment probability peaks, the 110 %-plus burnout ceiling where top-performer turnover rises, and the below-80 % complacency zone where the best reps leave regardless of compensation — was robust across B2B-medical-device panels. Third, the Pareto-tier-and-dedicate key-account-management literature documented that a commercial book where 12 accounts drove 74 % of revenue but received the same service intensity as the remaining 328 was operating with a structural mismatch the 2007 same-service-for-all configuration could not resolve without dedicating reps as key-account managers.

The 2000–2025 commission-economics literature added two findings. The documented two-year-retention panel showed that team-based retention packages tied to regional growth dominated purely individual-cash matches in medical-device B2B contexts by approximately 1.9×, particularly when the poaching competitor's offer was cash-front-loaded without a ramp-period clawback. And the Colombian Ley 1474 anti-kickback enforcement curve had compressed per-clinical-client expense limits to COP 150,000 since the 2021 Resolución update, producing a three-case-per-year audit density at mid-market distributors InduMed's size.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The 2007-vintage commission plan; the 2024 Restrepo-Ocampo mandate; the 2025 attainment slide to 76 %; the January–March 2026 commission-consultant diagnostic; the MedSupply-poach approaches of the preceding thirty days; and the April 23 commitment the executive committee had scheduled at the March review were the sequence.

### What Restrepo-Ocampo knew

The Churchill-Ford-Walker diagnostic and the 95-to-105 % sweet-spot curve; the six compensation-plan variants and their expected two-year-retention and attainment trade-offs; the 12-account Pareto shape of the book and the service-intensity mismatch; the three MedSupply approaches and the team-retention-vs-individual-match dominance pattern; and the executive committee's appetite for a specific three-round commitment rather than a narrative plan.

### What she did not know

Whether Andrés Felipe Reyes would accept a team-retention package or had already decided; whether the Costa Atlántica territory's 61 %-attainment problem was rep-driven, territory-potential-driven or competitor-driven; whether Hospital Universitario de Bogotá would respond to a dedicated key-account manager within the ninety-day window or would stall; whether the 2027 quota-reset commitment would hold under the CFO's pressure to show forward growth in the April-May budget process; and whether the Ley 1474 compliance audit outcomes would hold at two-cases-per-year or would trend toward the three-case peer median as the company grew.

### What others were saying

**The founder-CEO (60, serving on the board since 2007)**: *"Mariana, los reps son gente. No son celdas en un Excel. Redisñalo con la cabeza, pero explícalo con el corazón."*

**The Chief Commercial Officer (predecessor, now Chief Revenue Officer, 58)**: *"El plan del 2007 funcionó porque en 2007 teníamos doce reps y ocho cuentas que mandaban. Hoy son veinticuatro reps y trescientas cuarenta cuentas. Es otro animal."*

**Andrés Felipe Reyes (top-performer, 34, eight years at InduMed)**: *"No me voy por la plata. Me voy si siento que la empresa no ve lo que pasa en mi territorio."*

**The Costa Atlántica Regional Manager (52, sixteen years at InduMed)**: *"El problema no son mis reps. Es que la Costa es la Costa. Bajo los mismos parámetros que Bogotá nunca voy a llegar."*

**The compliance officer (external, retained since Ley 1474)**: *"Dos expedientes en el escritorio. Uno claramente intencional. Otro probablemente no. Las dos caen dentro de la misma resolución."*

**The MedSupply Andina sales director (recently public remarks)**: *"Estamos construyendo el mejor equipo de ventas de dispositivos médicos del país. Contratamos a quien sepa vender."*

## Options Under Consideration

Restrepo-Ocampo had narrowed the redesign space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — System redesign (territory rebalance + 95-to-105 % quota calibration + 70/30 base/commission plan with accelerators + COP 320M training across consultative-selling and clinical-language + four hunters + team-retention for top three reps + A/B/C tiering with two dedicated KAMs + 2027 reset without ratchet) (Recommended).** Respects Churchill-Ford-Walker; respects the 95-to-105 % finding; respects the team-retention-dominates-individual-match panel; respects the Pareto book shape; respects the Ley 1474 compliance boundary. Projected 2027 attainment 91-94 %, turnover 18-21 %.
- **Option B — Commission-tweak-only (current territories + lift quota to 115 % + shift to 50/50 base/commission with steeper accelerators + minimal training + no tiering + individual-cash match for top three reps + 2027 ratchet on realised).** Rejected — the commission-tweak-only reading treats the 76 % attainment as a motivation problem rather than a system problem; the 115 % quota lands above the burnout ceiling; the individual match dominates on month-six retention and loses on month-twenty-four retention; the ratchet creates the sandbagging trap the consultant flagged.
- **Option C — Aggressive disruption (reassign all 24 reps across territories + 120 % quota + 60/40 with uncapped accelerators + COP 400M training + eight hunters + terminate Costa manager + tier and dedicate three KAMs + accept-and-replace MedSupply poach + 2027 reset).** Defensible on capability-building grounds but loses too many reps to the reassignment shock; the 120 % quota exceeds the burnout ceiling by a decile; the eight-hunter configuration compresses farming coverage in the B-and-C tiers; the accept-and-replace posture forfeits thirty-two percentage points of institutional account-knowledge.

Restrepo-Ocampo's private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the single decision most correlated with the 2027 outcome was the quota calibration, because the Churchill-Ford-Walker equation collapsed on the motivation term under miscalibration and because the documented Colombian-medical-device-rep panel had shown attainment probability peaking at 95-to-105 % with a sharp burnout boundary above 110. The *Territorio Tuyo* frame sits at the LATAM-Spanish-medical-device-B2B-sales-management tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from NexaCore Sales Force (sim 023, US-SaaS B2B-software-sales command system): sim 301 emphasises the commission-heavy, clinic-hospital-clinician-interaction, Ley-1474-compliance context in Spanish; sim 023 emphasises the SaaS-ARR-pipeline-velocity quarterly-commit context in English.
