## Abstract

EnerStar Andina S.A.S. was, in the case universe, a 47-year-old Bogotá-headquartered family-owned third-generation lead-acid battery manufacturer founded in 1979 by the Restrepo-Samper family serving automotive OEM (GM Colombia, Renault, Mazda assembly contracts), automotive aftermarket (Mercado Libre, Exito distribution), telecom-backup (Claro, Tigo, Movistar infrastructure) and industrial-UPS (data-center and hospital segments) across the Andean-Venezuelan corridor — approximately COP 182bn 2025 revenue, 620 employees, EBITDA margin 18.2%, customer base satisfied and profitable, a rising Asian-and-European li-ion threat on the 4-year horizon for automotive-start-stop segments and the 2-year horizon for industrial-UPS segments, and a Chilean Volta startup raising USD 45m for a solid-state-battery development program that the EnerStar engineering team had flagged in the December 2025 technology-watch memo. On **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 8:22 a.m., **Sra. María-Fernanda Restrepo-Samper**, a 47-year-old incoming Chief Executive Officer and third-generation family-member arriving on her one-hundred-eighth day in role, opened the four-technology S-curve workbook her chief technology officer had refreshed at 11:52 p.m. the previous evening and sat down to commit the four-round package for the 3:00 p.m. family-council-and-board session.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply Christensen's 1997 Innovator's-Dilemma framework to EnerStar's lead-acid-versus-li-ion configuration, articulate why disruptive technologies enter underperforming on metrics customers value, serve marginal segments customers aren't in, improve faster than customer needs rise, and why mainstream customers give no warning.
2. Apply Foster's 1986 technology-S-curve framework to the four chemistries (lead-acid near top at 1.5%/yr, li-ion in steep middle at 8–10%/yr, LFP at 9%/yr, solid-state at 15%/yr from low baseline), compute the crossing points at 4 years for automotive-start-stop and 2 years for industrial-UPS, and articulate why the S-curve reading dominates the customer-satisfaction reading as the strategic signal.
3. Apply Christensen-Raynor-McDonald's 2015 sustaining-versus-disruptive framework to the AGM-for-start-stop and VRLA-for-telecom sustaining-innovations inside EnerStar's core customer base versus the li-ion consumer-electronics new-market-disruption and LFP-low-end-disruption at the fleet-operator segment, and articulate why the sustaining lens dominates inside the core and the disruptive lens dominates outside.
4. Apply Trigeorgis's 1996 real-options framework to the R&D-budget allocation (COP 8bn sustaining NPV-positive versus COP 28bn Year-1 plus COP 18bn Years-2-3 disruptive NPV-negative at WACC 14% but probability-weighted option value COP 54bn), articulate the asymmetric cost-of-non-participation at minus COP 180bn, and articulate why DCF analysis is structurally wrong for disruption and real-options analysis replaces it.
5. Commit the Round-3 O'Reilly-Tushman 2004 ambidextrous-organisation autonomous-subsidiary architecture for the li-ion unit and the Round-4 Christensen-Ulwick Jobs-to-be-Done non-consumer-entry framing with the Year-8 exit-position commitment, and articulate why the innovation-strategy frame sim 318 occupies is distinct from Nexum (sim 020), InnoLab (sim 118) and InnoParadox (sim 168) — 318 emphasises the LATAM-family-firm S-curve-and-real-options-and-ambidextrous synthesis inside the classic Christensen canon.

## The Protagonist

Restrepo-Samper, 47, had been CEO at EnerStar Andina since January 5, 2026 — one hundred eight days into role at the case opening — had completed an Ingeniería Química at the Universidad de los Andes in 2001 and an MBA at the Harvard Business School in 2009, had spent seven years at a US-based energy-storage venture between 2009 and 2016, had led the innovation portfolio of a multinational automotive-parts manufacturer in Stuttgart between 2016 and 2022, and had returned to Bogotá in 2023 as heir-apparent of the Restrepo-Samper family firm. Her innovation-strategy instincts had been shaped by three formative exposures: the 2008 Harvard-Business-School Christensen-Raynor Innovator's Dilemma elective; the 2013 US energy-storage venture pivot from grid-scale to behind-the-meter that taught her the low-end-disruption pattern at close range; and the 2019 Stuttgart ambidextrous-organisation architecture for a li-ion unit that had delivered 2.8× the NPV projection under autonomous-subsidiary architecture against the in-line-business-unit baseline.

---

## The Decision

Restrepo-Samper had to commit, by the 3:00 p.m. family-council-and-board session, the four-round package.

1. **Round 1 — Innovator's Dilemma and S-curves.** Accept the S-curve reading. Commit the disruptive-plus-sustaining dual-track portfolio. Acknowledge that the current customer base is the most reliable guide to where EnerStar will not find the future.
2. **Round 2 — Real-options R&D allocation.** Commit the COP 8bn sustaining-lead-acid budget. Commit the COP 28bn Year-1 plus COP 18bn Years-2-3 li-ion entry budget under real-options framing. Package the commitment as a call-option, not a DCF project.
3. **Round 3 — Ambidextrous subsidiary.** Commit the autonomous-subsidiary architecture for li-ion. Separate P&L, separate management team, consumer-electronics customers, iterative-experimentation culture. Connect to EnerStar only through capital and senior attention.
4. **Round 4 — Jobs-to-be-Done and 8-year exit.** Commit the Jobs-to-be-Done non-consumer-entry thesis. Commit the Year-8 exit-position target (40% revenue from li-ion-and-LFP platforms, 60% from sustaining-lead-acid-plus-AGM-and-VRLA).

Commitments: the Round 1 dual-track disruption-response; the Round 2 real-options R&D allocation; the Round 3 ambidextrous-organisation architecture; the Round 4 Jobs-to-be-Done Year-8 exit; and a one-page family-council-and-board memo that frames the four-round commitment as a single 8-year Innovator's-Dilemma response.

## Sectoral Background

The 1990–2025 Latin-American stationary-and-automotive-batteries category had, in the case universe, registered approximately USD 6.4bn in aggregate annual revenue across the 87 registered manufacturers, concentrated in three operator tiers (multinational lead-acid manufacturers like Exide and East Penn; regional family-owned manufacturers like EnerStar and MAC; specialty stationary-UPS converters). The 2018–2025 li-ion-import panel had documented that Asian-and-European li-ion imports to Latin America grew at 38% CAGR while lead-acid grew at 1.2% CAGR, with documented segment-crossing readings showing li-ion captured 18% of Andean industrial-UPS demand by 2025 from a near-zero 2019 baseline. The 1995–2025 disruption-pattern panel had documented that approximately 72% of incumbent manufacturers that fell to li-ion disruption did so with customer-base-satisfaction readings above 90% in the twelve months preceding the S-curve crossing.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1986–2025 innovation-strategy literature produced three findings the simulation had to respect. First, Christensen's 1997 Innovator's-Dilemma framework, combined with Foster's 1986 S-curve canon and Christensen-Raynor-McDonald's 2015 sustaining-versus-disruptive refinement, had produced documented disruption-response readings across approximately 2,100 panel-match incumbent-firms showing that S-curve-plus-disruptive-lens interventions correlated at 0.68 with 10-year-market-share-preservation readings and at 0.72 with 10-year-profitability preservation. Second, Trigeorgis's 1996 real-options framework had documented disruptive-R&D-NPV-reversal rates approximately 2.8× higher under real-options logic than under DCF-only logic on panel-match cases, and cost-of-non-participation readings averaging 3.2× the direct-DCF project cost. Third, O'Reilly and Tushman's 2004 ambidextrous-organisation framework had documented disruptive-unit-survival rates approximately 4.2× higher under autonomous-subsidiary architecture than under in-line-business-unit architecture on panel-match disruption-response cases.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The 1979 Restrepo-Samper founding; the 1992 first industrial-UPS contract; the 2001 telecom-backup framework with Claro; the 2015 GM Colombia OEM contract; the 2019 first Asian li-ion import at large scale; the 2024 first li-ion capture of Andean industrial-UPS demand at 18%; the December 2025 Volta solid-state memo; the January 5, 2026 Restrepo-Samper appointment; the 108-day diagnostic phase; and the April 23 family-council-and-board session were the sequence.

### What Restrepo-Samper knew

The S-curve reading across the four chemistries; the Trigeorgis real-options value at COP 54bn; the cost-of-non-participation at minus COP 180bn; the O'Reilly-Tushman ambidextrous-architecture blueprint; the Jobs-to-be-Done non-consumer-entry framing; the family-council and board's risk-appetite readings; the 47-year customer-base loyalty across GM Colombia, Claro, Tigo and Movistar; and the COP 182bn revenue base with 18.2% EBITDA margin that would fund the disruption response.

### What she did not know

Whether the family council would absorb the COP 28bn Year-1 li-ion budget under real-options framing or would reject the NPV-negative project; whether the GM Colombia OEM contract team would accept the sustaining-track AGM investments as sufficient commitment to start-stop while the li-ion autonomous-subsidiary matured; whether the Volta Chilean startup would enter the Andean industrial-UPS segment first via a partnership or first via an acquisition; whether the consumer-electronics li-ion segment was genuinely non-consumer for EnerStar or would produce a within-core-customer-cannibalisation; and whether the Year-8 exit-position was achievable at 40/60 li-ion-LFP / lead-acid-AGM-VRLA split or would regress to 25/75 under execution drift.

### What others were saying

**The family-council senior (grandfather of the protagonist, 78)**: *"María-Fernanda, cuarenta y siete años de plomo-ácido. No me apagues el negocio que tu abuelo levantó en el setenta y nueve. Pero no me lo dejes atrapado en el dos mil treinta y cinco."*

**The CTO (56, 22 years at EnerStar)**: *"La curva S no miente. La madurez del plomo-ácido es terminal. El litio tiene ocho a diez por ciento anual de pendiente. Si no entramos, alguien entrará por nosotros."*

**The GM Colombia OEM relationship lead (51)**: *"El AGM para start-stop lo necesitamos en dos mil veintiocho. Si EnerStar no lo tiene, vamos a Exide o a East Penn."*

**The Claro telecom-backup procurement lead (44)**: *"Ya estamos probando litio de proveedor coreano. Si EnerStar tiene litio en veintiocho, lo compramos. Si no, ya no."*

**The Volta (Chilean startup) founder (37)**: *"Estado sólido para industrial-UPS en treinta y uno. Podríamos sentarnos en treinta a hablar."*

**The 620 EnerStar employees (aggregate signal via the 2025 engagement survey)**: *"El plomo-ácido es nuestra historia. El litio es una pregunta que no habíamos hecho."*

## Options Under Consideration

Restrepo-Samper had narrowed the innovation-strategy space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — Dual-track sustaining-plus-disruptive + real-options R&D budget + ambidextrous subsidiary + Jobs-to-be-Done non-consumer-entry + Year-8 40/60 exit (Recommended).** Respects Christensen, Foster, Trigeorgis, O'Reilly-Tushman and Ulwick; preserves the lead-acid customer base through AGM-and-VRLA sustaining; enters li-ion consumer-electronics as the non-consumer-entry beachhead; creates the autonomous subsidiary; commits the 40/60 Year-8 position. Projected 10-year market-share preservation at 72% against a 31% no-disruption-response baseline.
- **Option B — Sustaining-only, defend the core, do not enter li-ion.** Rejected — reproduces the Kodak-Nokia-Blockbuster-Encarta pattern; accumulates the documented 72%-incumbent-fall rate against 18% under dual-track response; NPV positive for 4 years, terminal thereafter.
- **Option C — Abandon lead-acid, pivot aggressively to li-ion.** Defensible on disruption-embrace grounds but risks family-council rupture in year forty-eight, customer-base alienation in the automotive-OEM and telecom-backup segments, and the 620-employee lead-acid-manufacturing workforce transition without a managed-distress interlude.

Restrepo-Samper's private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the single decision most correlated with the 10-year market-share-preservation outcome was the Round 3 ambidextrous-subsidiary architecture, because the 1986–2025 panel had shown that disruptive-unit-survival rates under autonomous-subsidiary architecture were approximately 4.2× higher than under in-line-business-unit architecture, and because her 2019 Stuttgart subsidiary had delivered 2.8× the NPV projection under the same architecture. The *Dilema del Innovador* frame sits at the LATAM-family-firm S-curve-and-real-options-and-ambidextrous synthesis tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from Nexum (sim 020, innovation-pipeline frame), InnoLab (sim 118, innovation-lab frame) and InnoParadox (sim 168, paradox frame) — 318 emphasises the classic Christensen canon end-to-end inside a Bogotá family firm.
