## Abstract

Energía del Sur S.A. was, in the case universe, a 22-year-old Santiago-de-Chile electricity distributor founded in 2004 after the Chilean electricity-sector privatisation serving 1.2 million residential, commercial and industrial customers across the southern Santiago metropolitan area — approximately CLP 340bn 2025 revenue, 340 employees, a regulatory mandate from the Comisión Nacional de Energía requiring reorganisation from the current functional structure to a business-unit matrix across four new business units (Residential-Customer-Services, Commercial-and-Industrial, Infrastructure-Operations and Regulatory-and-Public-Affairs), three departments to dissolve, a board-mandated 120-day-reorganisation-window running from April 15 to August 13 2026, and an absolute no-service-interruption operational constraint throughout the transition. On **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:42 a.m., **Isidora Campos-Herrera**, a 49-year-old incoming General Manager arriving on her eighth day in role, opened the first Organisational-Network-Analysis survey results her chief people officer had finalised at 11:14 p.m. the previous evening.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply Cross and Parker's 2004 Organisational-Network-Analysis methodology to Energía del Sur's 340-employee information-exchange network, identify the three structural holes between Finance / Operations / Regulatory-Affairs, identify **Valeria Tapia** as the central broker with 12-year Finance tenure and the organisation's documented circulatory-system position, and articulate why the formal organisation chart is not the map of how work actually gets done.
2. Apply Burt's 1992 structural-holes and brokerage framework to the Tapia broker-position, articulate why information access, information control and credit-for-ideas flow through her, and articulate why the matrix reorganisation risk is that a reorganisation-by-organisation-chart breaks the invisible brokerage the firm runs on and the Round 3 psychological-safety reading registers the break before the board-visible metrics do.
3. Apply French and Raven's 1959 six-power-bases framework to the incoming-GM's first-forty-day posture — Legitimate power (high, formal-authority-on-day-one), Expert power (low, doesn't know the grid yet), Referent power (zero, nobody knows her), Information power (low, doesn't yet know where the brokers sit), Reward power (partial, salary requires board), Coercive power (high but destructive in a matrix where psychological safety is already fragile) — and articulate why the build-Expert-and-Information-through-listening and build-Referent-through-the-first-forty-days posture dominates the Legitimate-and-Coercive power reliance.
4. Apply Galbraith's 1995 Star-Model framework to the Round 2 misalignment diagnosis — Strategy declared, Structure changed to matrix on day one, but Processes still flow budget approvals through functional VPs and Rewards still measure functional KPIs — and articulate why six months without Process-and-Rewards alignment produces matrix regression.
5. Commit the Round 3 three-senior-reassignment and two-role-dissolution package under Edmondson's 1999 psychological-safety frame with Kotter-informed change-resistance absorption, and articulate why the organisational-behaviour frame sim 310 occupies is distinct from Arcturus Influence Game (sim 112, influence-tactics-in-matrix frame) and Novatek Inclusive Organization (sim 055, inclusive-organisation design frame) — 310 emphasises the LATAM-regulated-utility-matrix-reorganisation-ONA-first-lab frame in Spanish with the 120-day-regulatory-mandated-reorganisation context.

On the morning of **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:42 a.m., **Isidora Campos-Herrera** arrived at the Energía del Sur headquarters in Las Condes on her eighth day in the General-Manager role, greeted the two overnight control-centre supervisors completing their shift handoff, and walked into the GM office past the wall of five framed sepia-and-colour photographs (the 2004 privatisation-launch photograph; the 2010 first-grid-modernisation; the 2016 first-smart-meter pilot; the 2022 regulatory-matrix-feasibility study photograph; and the 2026 reorganisation-mandate letter from the Comisión Nacional de Energía dated March 28, 2026). On the desk sat the first Organisational-Network-Analysis survey results her chief people officer Rosario Henríquez had finalised at 11:14 p.m. the previous evening. The 340-node network map had opened on the laptop. Three structural holes were visible between Finance, Operations and Regulatory Affairs. One central broker node — labelled *Sra. Valeria Tapia, Finanzas, 12 años* — was sized approximately three times larger than any other node. The 120-day-reorganisation-window was on day 8 of 120.

---

## The Protagonist

Campos-Herrera, 49, had been General Manager at Energía del Sur since April 15, 2026 — eight days into role at the case opening — had completed an Ingeniería Industrial at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile in 2000 and a Master of Public Administration at the Harvard Kennedy School in 2009, had spent thirteen years at a Chilean sanitation-utility in operations-director and general-manager roles before joining a Lima-based regional-electricity distributor in 2020 as Chief Operating Officer, and had been recruited to Energía del Sur in March 2026 on a board mandate to "run the matrix reorganisation without losing a customer and without losing the organisation." Her organisational-behaviour instincts had been shaped by three teachings: the 2008–2009 Harvard Kennedy School exposure to the Cross-Parker-Burt-Granovetter-Edmondson-Galbraith-French-Raven organisational-network and matrix-organisation canonical literature; a 2014 sanitation-utility reorganisation where the first-forty-day-listening posture had saved three critical brokers her predecessor had been preparing to reassign; and a 2023 Lima electricity-distributor regional-reorganisation where an aggressive Day-1 coercive-posture had cost the firm four of its six documented central brokers within ninety days.

---

## The Decision

Campos-Herrera had to commit, by the 4:00 p.m. board-reorganisation committee meeting, the three-round package.

1. **Round 1 — ONA and Power-Bases.** Accept the ONA survey findings. Identify the three structural holes. Identify the central broker (Valeria Tapia, Finance, 12 years). Commit the build-Expert-and-Information-first, build-Referent-through-the-first-forty-days, use-Coercive-never posture. Commit the OCI-uplift posture through cross-unit rotations, mixed workshops and joint problem-solving sessions — not the redraw-the-org-chart-and-wait approach.
2. **Round 2 — Star-Model Alignment.** Diagnose the misalignment — Strategy declared, Structure changed, but Processes and Rewards unchanged. Commit the six-month Process-and-Rewards alignment plan (budget approvals through business-unit GMs by Day 60; rewards measuring business-unit KPIs by Day 90; shared-services processes articulated by Day 120).
3. **Round 3 — Reassignments and Psychological Safety.** Commit the three senior-reassignment package (the Regulatory-Affairs Director moves to Business-Unit-4 GM; the Finance Director of Operations moves to Business-Unit-3 CFO; the Commercial Director moves to Business-Unit-2 GM). Commit the two role-dissolutions (the Director of Customer Service and the Director of IT-Applications dissolve into their new business-unit functions). Commit the communication-order across written, all-hands, private-conversations and sequencing.

Commitments: the Round 1 ONA-and-power-bases posture; the Round 2 Star-Model alignment plan; the Round 3 reassignment-and-dissolution package and communication sequencing; and a one-page board memo that frames the three-round commitment as a single 120-day reorganisation arc with the no-service-interruption operational constraint as the forcing function.

## Sectoral Background

The 2005–2025 Chilean electricity-distribution category had, in the case universe, registered approximately CLP 4.2tn in aggregate annual revenue across the thirty-one distribution concessions supervised by the Comisión Nacional de Energía, concentrated in six operator tiers (large multinational-owned distributors like Enel Chile, CGE and Chilquinta; mid-market regional-concession operators like Energía del Sur, SAESA and Frontel; smaller specialty and cooperative distributors). The 2024–2026 regulatory cycle had mandated a business-unit-matrix reorganisation across all Chilean electricity distributors serving above 500,000 customers — a response to the 2022 customer-service-quality-index reform — with Energía del Sur positioned in the 1.2m-customer mid-market tier. The 2024 Chilean mid-market-utility organisational-reorganisation panel had documented that approximately 68 % of reorganisations that failed had done so not on structural-redesign grounds but on Star-Model-processes-and-rewards-misalignment grounds within the first six months post-reorganisation.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1959–2025 organisational-behaviour literature produced three findings the simulation had to respect. First, Cross and Parker's 2004 Organisational-Network-Analysis framework, combined with Burt's 1992 structural-holes-and-brokerage canon, had produced documented organisational-performance readings across 340 organisations where the central-broker-preservation discipline correlated at approximately 0.62 with twelve-month-post-reorganisation performance and at approximately 0.78 with knowledge-retention panels. Second, Granovetter's 1973 strength-of-weak-ties framework, operationalised as an Organisational-Connectivity-Index through cross-unit rotations, mixed workshops and joint problem-solving sessions, had produced documented OCI-uplift rates approximately 2.3× higher on funded-cross-silo-interventions than on redraw-the-org-chart-only regimes. Third, Galbraith's 1995 Star-Model framework, applied as a Strategy-Structure-Processes-Rewards-People alignment diagnostic, had shown that Structure-only changes that did not carry Processes-and-Rewards alignment into the first six months produced documented matrix-regression rates at approximately 68 % on panel-match Latin American utility-sector reorganisations.

The 1959–2025 power-and-influence and psychological-safety literature added two findings. French and Raven's 1959 six-power-bases framework, combined with the Edmondson 1999 psychological-safety research, had documented that incoming GMs who relied on the Legitimate-plus-Coercive posture on Days 1-to-40 accumulated documented central-broker-departure rates approximately 3.4× higher than GMs who built Expert-and-Information-and-Referent power through listening postures. And the Kotter 2012 change-resistance absorption literature, applied to reassignment and role-dissolution events, had produced documented talent-retention readings approximately 1.9× higher under written-first-then-all-hands-then-private-conversation-sequencing than under the reverse order.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The 2022 regulatory matrix-feasibility study; the 2024 customer-service-quality-index reform; the March 28, 2026 Comisión Nacional de Energía formal reorganisation mandate; the April 15 Campos-Herrera arrival as incoming General Manager; the Day-1-to-8 Organisational-Network-Analysis survey execution; the April 22 completion of the first ONA map; and the April 23 board-reorganisation-committee commitment were the sequence.

### What Campos-Herrera knew

The ONA survey findings and the three structural holes; Valeria Tapia's central-broker position and her 12-year tenure; the Granovetter weak-ties OCI baseline at 34 on a 0-to-100 scale (documented mid-market-utility benchmark at 52); the French-Raven power-base inventory on her own Day-8 posture; the Galbraith Star-Model diagnosis with Structure-changed-but-Processes-and-Rewards-unchanged; the three senior-reassignment and two role-dissolution candidate package her predecessor had drafted; the Edmondson psychological-safety baseline reading at 3.2 on a 5-point scale (documented mid-market-utility median at 3.8); and the board-reorganisation committee's appetite for a specific 120-day commitment with regulatory compliance on record.

### What she did not know

Whether Valeria Tapia would accept the central-broker-recognition-and-build-around-her posture or would read the acknowledgement as a prelude to eventual dissolution; whether the three senior-reassignment candidates would absorb the reassignment or would depart to Enel, CGE or Chilquinta in the first ninety days; whether the two role-dissolution candidates would accept the business-unit-function transition or would negotiate severance; whether the Comisión Nacional de Energía would accept the 120-day execution timeline as meeting the regulatory mandate or would audit mid-window; whether the customer-service-quality-index would hold at the mandated 87 % threshold through the transition or would slip to the 83 %-regulatory-sanction trigger; and whether the board-reorganisation committee would accept the first-forty-day-listening posture or would press for earlier coercive-power deployment.

### What others were saying

**The Chair of the Board (Energía del Sur, 62)**: *"Isidora, ciento veinte días. Lo que no esté hecho al día ciento veintiuno no se hace. Pero no pierdas a Valeria Tapia."*

**The chief people officer (Rosario Henríquez, 44, eight years at Energía del Sur)**: *"La ONA muestra lo que ya sospechábamos. Valeria Tapia es la organización. Si la movemos sin acompañarla, la organización se rompe."*

**Valeria Tapia (Finance, 47, 12 years)**: *"Doce años escuchando las preguntas que Finanzas no hace, Operaciones no hace, Regulatorio no hace. Todas me las hacen a mí. No sé si es un trabajo. Ya es."*

**The Regulatory-Affairs Director (candidate for Business-Unit-4 GM, 56, 16 years)**: *"Llevo dieciséis años aquí. Si la reasignación es un ascenso, lo acepto. Si es un retiro amable, lo leo y actúo."*

**The Director of Customer Service (candidate for role dissolution, 51, 14 years)**: *"Si me disuelven el rol, lo entiendo. Si me disuelven la carrera, me voy."*

**The Comisión Nacional de Energía auditor (regulatory-counterparty of record)**: *"Agosto trece. Lo demás es conversación. Si no hay unidad de negocio operando, hay sanción."*

**The 1.2m residential customers (aggregate signal from the customer-service-quality-index tracker)**: *"Sin luz no hay reorganización aceptable."*

## Options Under Consideration

Campos-Herrera had narrowed the organisational-behaviour space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — Build-around-Tapia + listen-first power-bases + Star-Model alignment plan + written-then-all-hands-then-private reassignments (Recommended).** Respects Cross-Parker ONA; preserves Valeria Tapia as formally-recognised bridge-builder across the three business units; commits the Expert-and-Information-and-Referent power-base-build through listening; aligns Processes-and-Rewards by Day 60 / Day 90 / Day 120; sequences the three reassignments and two dissolutions in the written-then-all-hands-then-private order. Projected twelve-month post-reorganisation performance at the 1.7× broker-preservation panel reading.
- **Option B — Aggressive Day-1 redraw + move Tapia into Business-Unit-4 + maximum Coercive power + no Star-Model alignment.** Rejected — violates the broker-preservation finding; the Legitimate-plus-Coercive posture accumulates the documented 3.4× central-broker-departure rate; no Star-Model alignment drives the documented 68 % matrix-regression risk; the first-forty-day-coercive posture triggers the documented 2.8×-psychological-safety-collapse pattern.
- **Option C — Delay all reassignments + preserve the status-quo structure under a matrix-label + submit regulatory compliance on paper.** Defensible on risk-aversion grounds but breaches the Comisión Nacional de Energía mandate; accumulates the documented paper-matrix pattern that regresses within eighteen months; under-delivers the board's August 13 commitment; and surrenders the opportunity to formalise Valeria Tapia's broker-role.

Campos-Herrera's private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the single decision most correlated with the twelve-month-post-reorganisation outcome was the Round 1 Valeria-Tapia-build-around-her posture, because the documented ONA panel had shown that broker-preservation correlated at 0.62 with twelve-month performance and at 0.78 with knowledge-retention, and because her 2023 Lima predecessor's aggressive Day-1 posture had cost four of six central brokers in ninety days on an otherwise comparable-sector reorganisation. The *Red que Mueve el Mundo* frame sits at the LATAM-regulated-utility-matrix-reorganisation-ONA-first-lab-Spanish-primer tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from Arcturus Influence Game (sim 112, influence-tactics-in-matrix frame), and Novatek Inclusive Organization (sim 055, inclusive-organisation design frame) — 310 emphasises the 120-day-regulatory-mandated reorganisation with ONA-plus-Star-Model-plus-French-Raven-plus-Edmondson synthesis.
