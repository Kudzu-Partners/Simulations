## Abstract

Stratos Consulting Group was, in the case universe, a London-New-York-Singapore-headquartered global management-consulting firm with USD 3.2B in 2025 revenue across strategy, operations, digital and M&A practices, 4,800 consultants across 18 offices, a 2025 Firm-Productivity Index reading of 124 on a 2023-rebased baseline of 100, a 2025 Bench-Strength Index reading of 42 against a 2023 baseline of 68 (composite captures the firm's projected three-year partner-pipeline fill rate against promotion standards), a 2025 Client-Satisfaction reading of 76 against a 2023 baseline of 81, and a 2025 Engagement Index reading of 58 against a 2023 baseline of 72. The 2024 AI-assistant rollout across the consulting staff — launched under the prior Head of Talent Strategy and positioned as the "Grunt-Work Removal" programme — had compressed the analyst-level workload on literature review, market-sizing, working-file preparation and first-pass synthesis, and had, on the headline numbers, produced the 24% Productivity uplift the 2024 partner meeting had celebrated. The Q4 2025 partner-pipeline review had, however, surfaced a pattern the firm had not seen in four decades: the promotion-ready consultant cohort at Manager and Senior-Manager levels had compressed by 32%, the documented expert-judgment-formation ladders — three-to-five-year apprenticeships anchored on the "grunt work" the AI had automated — had collapsed, and Engagement had fallen across associates who had reported that the "interesting problems" they were now routed to arrived without the pattern-library that previously grounded their judgment. The Q1 2026 board of partners had approved a USD 62M JudgmentForge envelope and a Talent-Strategy-Lead-led five-round commitment cycle against a Firm-Productivity ceiling at 128, a Bench-Strength floor above 64, a Client-Satisfaction floor above 82, an Engagement floor above 70 and a composite JudgmentForge Score above 68. The case opens at 6:28 a.m. on Monday, April 20, 2026, at the Stratos Consulting Group London Mayfair office as **Hassan Mokri**, a 47-year-old Head of Talent Strategy, prepares the Round-1 AI-rollout recalibration. Sixty-six days separate him from the June 25 partner pipeline-and-talent review.

## The Protagonist

Hassan Mokri, 47, had assumed the Head-of-Talent-Strategy role at Stratos Consulting Group on September 1, 2025, from a Tier-1 consulting-adjacent professional-services firm where he had, across sixteen years, led a 2021–2024 apprenticeship-ladder rebuild across a 2,200-consultant cohort that had lifted Bench Strength from 38 to 64 and had closed a 2024 AI-assistant rollout with an explicit judgment-preservation architecture. Before that he had spent eight years as an engagement manager in a strategy practice. The Senior Partner had recruited him in July 2025 specifically on two capabilities: his documented discipline of treating AI adoption as an apprenticeship-re-engineering problem rather than a productivity-only problem, and his willingness to defend a protected-reps operating model against quarterly utilisation pressure. His 2026 variable compensation tied 25% to the Bench-Strength recovery and 15% to the Engagement floor.

## The Decision

Mokri had to commit, by the May 4 partner pre-review, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — AI-rollout commitment.** Three archetypes: judgment-preserving (AI-assistance confined to documented-pattern-library tasks with parallel human-run reps, explicit apprenticeship-reps preserved for analyst and associate levels, Composite 76), productivity-first (AI-assistance everywhere with analyst-level workload compressed 60%+ — Composite 34, bench-strength trigger), or phased (judgment-preserving on strategy practice only — Composite 54). Working envelope: judgment-preserving.
2. **Round 2 — Junior-displacement response.** Three archetypes: designed-apprenticeship-reps (re-engineered learning ladder with documented judgment-formation milestones, rotation through the four previously AI-compressed tasks at reduced frequency but preserved reps, Composite 76), skip-the-grunt-work (accept analyst-level workload compression — Composite 32, promotion-ready trigger), or phased (designed reps on M&A practice only — Composite 54). Working envelope: designed-apprenticeship-reps.
3. **Round 3 — Client-pressure defence.** Three vectors: rate-card-with-apprentice (revised rate card that preserves an apprentice presence on every engagement with explicit learning-ladder commitment to the client, Composite 76), senior-only (respond to client rate-card pressure by deploying senior-only teams — Composite 34, long-horizon pipeline collapse), or phased (rate-card on top-twenty clients only — Composite 54). Working envelope: rate-card-with-apprentice.
4. **Round 4 — Partner-crisis reset.** Three vectors: pipeline-integrity (hold the reps-preservation envelope through the anticipated H2 2026 margin-pressure window, document 36-month pipeline fill recovery, Composite 76), short-term-margin (compress apprentice deployment to protect quarterly margin — Composite 32, reactivation of the 2025 pattern), or phased (pipeline-integrity on two practices only — Composite 56). Working envelope: pipeline-integrity.
5. **Round 5 — Bench-strength rebuild.** Three vectors: institutionalised (judgment-formation milestones embedded in the promotion-ready standards, documented apprenticeship-reps tracked per consultant, rotating master-apprentice cohort program, Composite 78), campaign (one-time training programme — Composite 42), or phased (institutionalised on strategy and operations only — Composite 58). Working envelope: institutionalised.

On Mokri's working model, a Judgment-Preserving / Designed-Reps / Rate-Card / Pipeline-Integrity / Institutionalised envelope delivered Firm Productivity 126, Bench Strength 66, Client Satisfaction 82, Engagement 72, Composite 72.

## Company Background

Stratos Consulting Group had been founded in London in 1971 as a strategy-boutique and had, across fifty-five years, grown into a global management-consulting firm with 4,800 consultants across eighteen offices. The 2019–2023 operating model had delivered a stable partner-pipeline anchored on a documented expert-judgment-formation ladder — three-to-five-year apprenticeships across literature review, market-sizing, working-file preparation, first-pass synthesis, client-interview preparation and structured communication — that had produced an annualised partner-promotion count of 34 against a target of 38.

The 2024 "Grunt-Work Removal" AI-assistant rollout had compressed the analyst-level workload across the first four judgment-formation tasks by an average of 62%. The Q4 2025 partner-pipeline review had surfaced four realities. First, the promotion-ready Manager and Senior-Manager cohort had compressed by 32% against the 2022 baseline. Second, the 2025 Associate-to-Manager promotion panel had reported that 44% of candidates had failed the judgment-demonstration threshold against 18% in 2022. Third, the 2025 Engagement Index had fallen by 14 points across associates who had been routed to "interesting problems" without the AI-compressed pattern-library reps. Fourth, three of the firm's top-twenty clients had flagged "senior-only-delivery" rate-card pressure that the 2024 rollout had enabled.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2023–2025 AI-in-professional-services literature had, across 62 consulting, legal and audit AI rollouts, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, firms that had deployed AI-assistants with the "grunt-work removal" framing had documented an average Bench-Strength compression of 18–26 points inside eighteen months. Second, firms that had deployed AI-assistants with an explicit apprenticeship-reps preservation architecture had sustained Bench Strength above 60 in 74% of cases against 22% for unrestricted deployments. Third, firms that had defended a rate-card-with-apprentice against client pressure had preserved 36-month partner-pipeline-fill in 68% of cases against 24% for firms that had acceded to senior-only-delivery.

Empirically, the post-2022 expertise-formation literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, expert judgment in professional services had, in longitudinal studies, required an average of 2,400–3,600 documented reps across a defined task-library over a 36-48-month window, with substitution by AI assistance in 62% or more of the reps reliably disrupting judgment formation. Second, firms that had embedded judgment-formation milestones in promotion-ready standards had preserved promotion-panel pass-rates above the 2022 baseline in 78% of cases.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The partner board had, in the January 2026 pipeline-and-talent session, approved the USD 62M envelope and the June 25 review calendar. The Head of Professional Development had, on February 10, delivered the judgment-formation-ladder diagnostic. The Global Chief of Staff had, on February 24, delivered the 36-month partner-pipeline-fill projection. The Head of Client Management had, on March 10, delivered the rate-card client-pressure matrix. The Chief Financial Officer had, on March 17, confirmed the envelope.

### What Mokri knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. A Judgment-Preserving / Designed-Reps / Rate-Card / Pipeline-Integrity / Institutionalised Envelope projected Composite 72. A Productivity / Skip / Senior-Only / Short-Term / Campaign Envelope projected Composite 26. A Hybrid Envelope projected Composite 54.

### What he did not know

Whether the 4,800-consultant organisation would absorb a partial walk-back of the 2024 "Grunt-Work Removal" narrative without triggering an associate-and-analyst exit wave; whether the rate-card-with-apprentice would hold against the H2 2026 client-pressure window; whether the H2 2026 margin-compression event would hold the pipeline-integrity envelope against partner compensation pressure; and whether the 36-month pipeline-fill recovery would land inside the board's approval window.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Professional Development**: *"Designed reps. The associates need the pattern-library or they arrive at Manager without the judgment."*

**The Global Chief of Staff**: *"Rate-card-with-apprentice. Senior-only delivery is the 2028 partner shortfall."*

**The Senior Partner (Managing Partner)**: *"Pipeline-integrity. If we trade the reps for the margin, we won't have partners to trade with in three years."*

**The Chief Executive Officer (Senior Partner)**: *"You can't skip the reps and still grow the partner."*

## Options Under Consideration

Mokri had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Judgment-Preserving / Designed-Reps / Rate-Card / Pipeline-Integrity / Institutionalised:** the recommended envelope. Firm Productivity 126. Bench Strength 66. Client Satisfaction 82. Engagement 72. Composite 72. Recommended.
- **Option B — Productivity / Skip / Senior-Only / Short-Term / Campaign:** the status-quo envelope. Composite 26. Rejected — 2028 partner-shortfall trigger.
- **Option C — Hybrid / Phased / Partial / Phased / Phased:** the partial envelope. Composite 54. Rejected — pipeline-collapse spreads to untreated practices.

Mokri's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 2 — the designed-apprenticeship-reps — because it was the round that re-engineered the AI-compressed learning ladder into a sustainable judgment-formation operating model.
