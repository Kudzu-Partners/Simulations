## Abstract

The Head of Data Science at the case-universe São Paulo-headquartered enterprise-analytics consultancy — approximately BRL 140M in 2025 revenue, 180 analysts and 46 senior data scientists across Mercosur, four practice areas (real-estate, banking, consumer-goods, advertising) — had been asked, in the February 2026 Managing Partner mandate, to deliver a Q3 2026 model-governance council commitment with a four-scenario regression-model-building audit anchored on the data-generating-process-first discipline. The Q4 2025 model-governance review had flagged a recurring failure mode across the firm's 226-person data population: analysts were picking regression specifications on R-squared maximisation, producing high-fit models that passed in-sample but failed out-of-sample on the diagnostic suite — residual-vs-fitted non-randomness, endogeneity-biased coefficients, non-stationary time-series, and boundary-breaking linear-probability models on binary outcomes. The February 2026 mandate asked the Head of Data Science to work through four anchor scenarios — house pricing (functional form), customer churn (categorical outcome), sales forecasting (time series) and advertising effectiveness (causal identification) — and commit to a model-building-discipline curriculum the Model-Governance Council would stake on the data-generating-process reading rather than on the R-squared headline. The case opens at 2:14 p.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the firm's São Paulo head office as **Priscila Andrade-Camargo**, a 42-year-old Head of Data Science, prepares the four-scenario commitment.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Scenario-1 House Pricing setup (predictors sqft / beds / age / zip; linear specification residual-vs-fitted non-randomness; log-linear specification with clean diagnostics; elasticity interpretation one-percent-more-sqft-buys-zero-point-seven-percent-more-price) and articulate why the log-linear functional-form dominates the linear on both diagnostics and interpretation — and why the elasticity read is the business decision-moment.
2. Analyse the Scenario-2 Customer Churn setup (binary outcome; linear-probability boundary-break at 0 and 1; logit specification with odds-ratio interpretation; marginal-effect plot across the continuous-complaint covariate) and articulate why the logit specification is the correct family for a binary outcome, why odds ratios — not coefficients — are the reportable interpretation, and why a one-point rise in complaints tripling the odds of churn is the marketing-team-level decision-moment.
3. Diagnose the Scenario-3 Sales Forecasting setup (time series with trend and seasonality; OLS with monthly dummies vs ARIMAX on a small sample; Breusch-Godfrey serial-correlation test) and articulate why the simpler model that passes the Breusch-Godfrey is the right pick on a small sample, why ARIMAX over-fits and loses out-of-sample, and why model parsimony is a diagnostic property rather than a stylistic preference.
4. Respond to the Scenario-4 Advertising Effectiveness setup (classic endogeneity where advertising spend responds to last-period sales; naive OLS estimate 4x revenue return; instrumental-variable panel with weather-shocks IV estimate 1.3x revenue return; same data, three-x causal-effect gap) and articulate why the IV estimate is the causally-identified coefficient, why the naive OLS-induced 4x read would justify over-investment, and why the CFO-level decision-moment turns on the causal-identification read.
5. Design the four-scenario commitment that reads each regression through the data-generating-process lens and commits to a four-module model-building-discipline curriculum for 226 analysts and senior data scientists — Module 1 (Functional Form), Module 2 (Categorical Outcomes), Module 3 (Time Series), Module 4 (Causal Identification) — with a diagnostic-score-plus-spec-score grading discipline, articulating why the best model is not the one with the highest R² but the one whose residuals are boring, and the business pivots on the data-generating-process signal rather than on the R-squared headline.

## The Protagonist

Andrade-Camargo, 42, had been Head of Data Science since 2023, had spent sixteen years across Brazilian econometric consulting, a two-year postdoctoral fellowship at Instituto Quaresmeira de Estudos Econômicos in 2013–2015 on endogeneity and IV identification, and a three-year secondment at a Buenos Aires-based credit-scoring startup in 2019–2022 where she ran a logistic-regression-with-marginal-effects coaching programme for a 32-analyst cohort. In 2024 she co-authored the firm's internal playbook *Residuals Before R-Squared*, which framed regression-model building as a data-generating-process-first discipline. The Managing Partner had assigned her the four-scenario commitment on three considerations: her command of the 2013–2015 Quaresmeira fellowship on causal identification; her discipline of leading the Council read with the residual-diagnostics column rather than the R-squared column; and her willingness to recommend a mandatory four-module curriculum for the 226-person data population.

## The Decision

Andrade-Camargo had to commit, by the 4:30 p.m. Model-Governance Council pre-brief, the four-scenario commitment and the Q3 2026 model-building-discipline-curriculum recommendation.

1. **Scenario 1 — House Pricing.** Log-linear beats linear; elasticity 0.7; residual-vs-fitted clean. Module 1.
2. **Scenario 2 — Customer Churn.** Logit beats linear probability; complaints-to-odds ratio 3.0x per point. Module 2.
3. **Scenario 3 — Sales Forecasting.** OLS with monthly dummies beats ARIMAX on the small sample; Breusch-Godfrey passes. Module 3.
4. **Scenario 4 — Advertising Effectiveness.** IV panel estimate 1.3x revenue return vs naive-OLS 4x. Module 4.

Four-scenario dashboard: period scores 82 / 78 / 85 / 74; weighted R² 0.71; diagnostic-score-plus-spec-score grading applied.

On Andrade-Camargo's working commitment, the curriculum recommendation combined the four modules — Functional Form (Scenario 1), Categorical Outcomes (Scenario 2), Time Series (Scenario 3), Causal Identification (Scenario 4) — delivered across 10 weeks to 226 analysts and senior data scientists in a hybrid self-paced plus live-workshop format, with a diagnostic-score-plus-spec-score grading discipline at the end of each module, a quarterly refresher and a Model-Governance Council sign-off on every client-facing model above a pre-agreed complexity threshold.

## Options Under Consideration

Andrade-Camargo had narrowed the space to three curriculum postures.

- **Option A — Mandatory four-module model-building-discipline curriculum (Functional Form / Categorical Outcomes / Time Series / Causal Identification) with diagnostic-score-plus-spec-score grading and a Model-Governance Council sign-off above a pre-agreed complexity threshold (Recommended):** Budget BRL 880k; expected 42% model-failure-rate reduction; quarterly refresher. Recommended.
- **Option B — Voluntary three-module cohort launch (drop Causal Identification):** rejected — leaves the endogeneity failure mode uncovered and fails the 2024 advertising-effectiveness lesson.
- **Option C — External-training partnership with a Brazilian university:** rejected — loses the firm-specific practice-area depth and cannot integrate with the Model-Governance Council sign-off process.

Her private view was that Option A was the right curriculum and that the scenario most correlated with the Council's eventual acceptance was Scenario 4 — the advertising-effectiveness endogeneity — because it was the scenario where the data-generating-process read diverged most clearly from the R-squared-maximisation read that the Q3 2024 client-delivery miss had followed.
