## Abstract

VERDANA CAPITAL MARKETS was, in the case universe, a London-headquartered European debt-capital-markets operator with EUR 620B in 2025 origination volume and a Sustainable Finance Desk that had, across 2022–2025, closed fifty-eight sustainable-finance issuances across green, social, sustainability and sustainability-linked formats for a combined EUR 84B in transaction volume. In Q4 2025, the desk had, under a single issuance window, lined up three parallel mandates: a **Helios Renewables** EUR 500M Green Bond financing solar-plus-storage capacity, an **Avanta Healthcare** GBP 300M Social Bond financing access-to-care infrastructure, and a **NordChem Industries** EUR 600M Sustainability-Linked Bond (SLB) with a step-up coupon tied to a 2030 scope-1-and-2 absolute-emissions target. The Q1 2026 desk-commitment envelope had priced the three-issuance window at a combined fee pool of EUR 28M, a desk-composite-score floor of 70, a greenwashing-risk ceiling of 30, and a regulatory-resilience floor of 70 on the desk's proprietary resilience index. On Tuesday, February 3, 2026, the Head of the Sustainable Finance Desk had received a draft ESMA guidance circulated by European Securities and Markets Authority staff that tightened Sustainability-Linked Bond Key Performance Indicator materiality and, on a first read, placed the NordChem baseline outside the qualification envelope. The case opens at 5:52 a.m. on Tuesday, February 17, 2026, at the Verdana Capital Markets London headquarters as **Ingrida Vereshchuk**, a 46-year-old Head of the Sustainable Finance Desk, prepares the Round-1 framework-development commitment.

## The Protagonist

Ingrida Vereshchuk, 46, had joined Verdana Capital Markets in May 2019 from a Tier-1 European investment bank where she had, across nine years, run the green-bond-origination desk through the 2014–2019 green-bond-market growth cycle and had, across a 2016 post-ICMA-Green-Bond-Principles book, delivered a framework-review pipeline of forty-four green-bond issuances. Before that, she had spent six years as a senior credit analyst on industrial and utilities sectors at a Nordic bank, with a specialisation in climate-transition credits. The Chief Executive Officer of Verdana Capital Markets had recruited her specifically on the strength of two capabilities: her experience structuring SLB frameworks that cleared the ICMA Sustainability-Linked Bond Principles KPI-materiality test, and her reputation for refusing to ship a use-of-proceeds framework that had not cleared an EU Taxonomy Do-No-Significant-Harm assessment. Her 2026 long-term incentive plan tied 35% to the desk-composite-score trajectory and 15% to the greenwashing-risk ceiling.

## The Decision

Vereshchuk had to commit, by the March 10 Executive Committee commitment, the Round-1 framework-development envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2, 3 and 4.

1. **Round 1 — Framework Development** — how to set four framework levers across the three issuances: taxonomy alignment (full alignment with the EU Taxonomy climate-mitigation technical screening criteria for Helios, partial-and-best-effort for Avanta, partial-and-social-criteria-supplement for NordChem), Do-No-Significant-Harm assessment depth (full six-objective assessment, reduced four-objective, or minimum), Key Performance Indicator selection for NordChem (absolute scope-1-and-2 emissions, intensity scope-1-and-2 emissions, or a multi-KPI basket), and Sustainability Performance Target calibration (ambitious versus market-consistent versus conservative). The working envelope seated full-alignment-plus-full-DNSH-plus-absolute-KPI-plus-ambitious-SPT against a use-of-proceeds-only alternative.
2. **Round 2 — Pricing & Structuring** — how to set three pricing levers: greenium (2–5 basis points on Helios and Avanta, 0–2 basis points on NordChem), tenor (seven-year, ten-year, twelve-year on NordChem), and step-up magnitude (15 basis points on a single-observation-date, 25 basis points on a single-observation-date, or 15 basis points on a two-observation-date structure). The working envelope seated a 3-basis-point greenium on Helios and Avanta, a 0-basis-point greenium on NordChem (the SLB market convention), a ten-year tenor on NordChem, and a 25-basis-point single-observation-date step-up.
3. **Round 3 — SPO & Regulatory Response (with an URGENT ESMA-guidance event)** — how to respond to the ESMA draft guidance that tightens SLB KPI materiality. Three vectors: re-baseline the NordChem KPI against the tightened materiality test and re-SPO through the window with a seven-day framework-amendment process (Commercial cost of three basis points on NordChem pricing, Impact-Credibility uplift of 7 points, Regulatory-Resilience uplift of 12 points); delay the NordChem window to Q3 2026 while Helios and Avanta price on March 23 (Commercial cost of fee-deferral, Impact-Credibility flat); or withdraw NordChem and proceed Helios-and-Avanta-only (Commercial cost of EUR 12M fee-write-down, Impact-Credibility flat).
4. **Round 4 — Investor Roadshow** — how to set three roadshow levers: taxonomy table disclosure (full alignment-and-partial-alignment disclosure, alignment-only disclosure), impact report commitment (annual with external assurance, annual without external assurance, or biennial), and Science-Based-Targets-initiative (SBTi) alignment disclosure on NordChem (pre-committed, under-validation, or not committed). The working envelope seated full-disclosure-plus-annual-externally-assured-plus-SBTi-under-validation against a minimum-disclosure alternative.

On Vereshchuk's working model, a Full-Alignment / 3bp-plus-25bp / Re-Baseline-and-Re-SPO / Full-Disclosure-plus-Externally-Assured envelope delivered Impact Credibility 78, Greenwashing Risk 22, Commercial 74, Regulatory Resilience 78, Composite Score 73. A Use-of-Proceeds-Only / No-Greenium / Delay-or-Withdraw / Minimum-Disclosure envelope delivered Impact Credibility 38, Greenwashing Risk 64, Commercial 52, Regulatory Resilience 34, Composite 42.

## Desk Background

The Sustainable Finance Desk at Verdana Capital Markets had been stood up in 2019 as an extension of the bank's European investment-grade debt-capital-markets franchise and had, across 2022–2025, scaled from six to eighteen full-time structuring-and-origination professionals. The Q1 2026 desk-mandate-book placed three parallel mandates inside a six-week issuance window:

Helios Renewables, the desk's return-client on an EUR 500M senior-unsecured Green Bond financing a fourth wave of solar-plus-storage capacity across Iberia, had been an issuer since 2022 with a strong taxonomy-aligned use-of-proceeds register. Avanta Healthcare, a first-time social-bond issuer on a GBP 300M seven-year senior-unsecured bond financing access-to-care infrastructure across the United Kingdom, had engaged Verdana on the basis of the 2025 ICMA Social Bond Principles update. NordChem Industries, a first-time SLB issuer on an EUR 600M ten-year senior-unsecured sustainability-linked bond with a step-up coupon tied to a 2030 scope-1-and-2 absolute-emissions target, had engaged Verdana on the basis of the 2025 Q4 ICMA SLB Principles update.

The Q4 2025 Executive-Committee dossier had identified three specific pressures the four-round envelope would inherit. First, the ESMA regulatory calendar had flagged a draft guidance on SLB KPI materiality at a high-probability landing inside the Q1 2026 issuance window — the flag that framed the Round-3 URGENT event. Second, the Q4 2025 SPO-provider pipeline had, across the three mandates, placed Second-Party-Opinion-issuance timing at a seven-to-ten-day window from framework finalisation. Third, the Q4 2025 investor-registration book had flagged ten dedicated Article-8-and-Article-9 fund-manager investors whose mandates required a full-alignment taxonomy disclosure.

## Industry and Market Context

The European sustainable-finance landscape in 2026 was characterised by three structural features the envelope sat inside. First, the 2019–2024 green-and-SLB evidence base had, across 184 issuances, documented a greenium differential of 1–5 basis points and an Impact-Credibility differential of 14–24 points between taxonomy-aligned-plus-ambitious-SPT frameworks and use-of-proceeds-only frameworks. Second, the 2020–2024 ESMA and EU-supervisory literature had, across 38 mid-window regulatory tightenings, placed re-baseline-and-re-SPO responses at a 74% Impact-Credibility preservation rate against 22% for delay-or-withdraw responses. Third, the 2018–2024 sustainability-linked-bond evidence base had, across 92 SLBs, placed single-observation-date 25-basis-point step-ups at a Commercial-plus-Regulatory-Resilience composite-score differential of 12–18 points against 15-basis-point multi-observation-date step-ups.

Empirically, the post-2020 sustainable-finance literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, issuances that had cleared a full six-objective DNSH assessment and committed to annual externally-assured impact reports had, across a 2019–2024 sample of 58 comparable issuances, preserved the greenwashing-risk below 25 in 82% of cases against 28% for issuances that had a reduced-DNSH commitment. Second, issuances that had re-baselined KPIs against mid-window ESMA tightenings had, across a 2020–2024 sample of 34 comparable events, preserved the regulatory-resilience above 70 in 79% of cases against 24% for issuances that had delayed-or-withdrawn.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Chief Executive Officer of Verdana Capital Markets had, in October 2025, approved the three-mandate book. The Director of Sustainable Finance Structuring had, on January 26, delivered the framework pre-read across Helios, Avanta and NordChem. The Head of SLB Origination had, on February 2, pre-committed to the 25-basis-point single-observation-date step-up on NordChem. The Head of Regulatory Affairs had, on February 9, circulated the ESMA draft guidance and pre-committed to the re-baseline-and-re-SPO response on the NordChem mandate. The Head of Sales and Syndicate had, on February 13, confirmed the ten-investor Article-8-and-Article-9 mandate-floor test.

### What Vereshchuk knew

The team had modelled three four-round envelopes. A Full-Alignment / 3bp-plus-25bp / Re-Baseline-and-Re-SPO / Full-Disclosure-plus-Externally-Assured Envelope projected Impact 78, GW Risk 22, Commercial 74, Regulatory 78, Composite 73. A Use-of-Proceeds-Only / No-Greenium / Delay-or-Withdraw / Minimum-Disclosure Envelope projected Impact 38, GW Risk 64, Commercial 52, Regulatory 34, Composite 42. A Full-Alignment / 3bp-plus-25bp / Delay-NordChem / Full-Disclosure Envelope projected Impact 68, GW Risk 32, Commercial 58, Regulatory 62, Composite 58 — a trajectory that preserved Helios and Avanta but forfeited the NordChem fee pool.

### What she did not know

Whether the ESMA draft guidance would finalise inside the Round-3 window or slip to Round 4; whether the NordChem Board would, on the re-baseline-and-re-SPO request, hold the ambitious-SPT register; whether the SPO provider would, on the seven-day framework-amendment window, deliver an externally credible amendment opinion; and whether the Article-8-and-Article-9 fund-manager investor book would hold the taxonomy-aligned-plus-full-disclosure commitment through the roadshow.

### What others were saying

**The Director of Sustainable Finance Structuring**: *"Full alignment. Full DNSH. Ambitious SPT. No half-measures."*

**The Head of SLB Origination**: *"Re-baseline NordChem inside the window. Seven days to the amendment SPO."*

**The Head of Regulatory Affairs**: *"Re-SPO-through-the-window. Do not delay."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"The SPO reads the framework twice — once for impact, once for proceeds."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Vereshchuk had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Full-Alignment / 3bp-plus-25bp / Re-Baseline-and-Re-SPO / Full-Disclosure-plus-Externally-Assured:** the recommended envelope. Impact 78. GW Risk 22. Commercial 74. Regulatory 78. Composite 73. Recommended.

- **Option B — Use-of-Proceeds-Only / No-Greenium / Delay-or-Withdraw / Minimum-Disclosure:** the minimal envelope. Impact 38. GW Risk 64. Composite 42. Rejected.

- **Option C — Full-Alignment / 3bp-plus-25bp / Delay-NordChem / Full-Disclosure:** the delay envelope. Composite 58. Rejected as it forfeits the NordChem fee pool.

Vereshchuk's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite Score was Round 3 — the re-baseline-and-re-SPO response to the ESMA draft-guidance tightening that, inside a seven-day amendment window, re-qualified the NordChem baseline and preserved the Regulatory-Resilience index above 75.
