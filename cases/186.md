## Abstract

Belleza Andina was, in the case universe, a Medellín-headquartered LatAm direct-to-consumer beauty brand operating across Colombia, Peru, Chile and Mexico, with USD 1.8M in monthly 2025 revenue, a 2025 Return-on-Advertising-Spend (ROAS) of 3.4× on the reported last-click basis, a 2025 Customer-Acquisition-Cost of USD 24, a 2025 Lifetime-Value reading of USD 118, a 2025 Brand-Lift reading of 38 against a LatAm-beauty peer-benchmark of 52, a 2025 monthly media budget of USD 520K, and a 2025 board-approved five-quarter growth objective of +40% revenue on a flat media envelope. The 2023–2025 operating model had been anchored on a last-click attribution architecture that had, across the cycle, concentrated 62% of spend in Meta and had produced the reported 3.4× ROAS on a signal base that the November 2025 internal review had flagged as structurally weakened by the 2021–2024 iOS-privacy trajectory. The Q1 2026 board had approved a USD 6M five-round performance-and-attribution-rebuild envelope — the DigitalFunnel programme — and had positioned it as the brand's response to three overlapping pressures: a Q1 2026 Apple iOS update that had collapsed Meta-reported ROAS from 4.1× to 2.3× overnight, a Q4 2025 marketing-mix-modelling vendor pilot that had surfaced a 30%-of-lift attribution gap to TV and influencer channels, and a Q1 2026 board paper mandating a +40% revenue growth on the unchanged media envelope. The Q1 2026 board had reaffirmed the USD 6M envelope and a Chief-Marketing-Officer-led five-round commitment cycle against a CAC ceiling below USD 22, an LTV floor above USD 132, a Brand-Lift floor above 50, a ROAS floor above 3.2× on the rebuilt measurement, and a Composite DigitalFunnel Score above 72. The case opens at 6:43 a.m. on Thursday, April 23, 2026, at the Belleza Andina Medellín head office as **Valentina Restrepo-Higuera**, a 39-year-old Chief Marketing Officer, prepares the Round-1 channel-mix rebuild commitment. Sixty-six days separate her from the June 28 board marketing review.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Round-1 channel-mix rebuild commitment (balanced-five-lever envelope across Meta, TikTok, Google, email and influencer vs. concentrated-Meta envelope) and diagnose when the balanced envelope dominates the concentrated envelope on the CAC-and-Brand-Lift composite.
2. Analyze the Round-2 A/B-test significance-discipline commitment (p-under-0.05 gate envelope that blocks variant-ship on any lead that has not crossed the statistical-significance line vs. leader-at-day-three envelope) and recommend the gating envelope on the false-positive-suppression basis.
3. Diagnose the Round-3 attribution-architecture rebuild commitment (data-driven-plus-MMM envelope that couples platform-level data-driven attribution with an independent marketing-mix model vs. last-click envelope) and recommend the rebuild envelope on the credit-assignment accuracy basis.
4. Respond to the Round-4 iOS-signal-loss rebuild commitment (incrementality-plus-aggregate envelope that replaces click-level signal with incrementality testing and aggregate measurement vs. Meta-overweight envelope that defends the legacy mix) and recommend the incrementality envelope on the post-iOS-privacy basis.
5. Design the Round-5 brand-and-performance embedding commitment that converts DigitalFunnel from a performance-only programme into a permanent integrated-measurement capability with brand-tracker, MMM-refresh cadence and board-visible composite dashboard.

## The Protagonist

Valentina Restrepo-Higuera, 39, had assumed the Chief-Marketing-Officer role at Belleza Andina on September 1, 2024, after nine years across growth, performance and brand leadership at two LatAm D2C beauty brands and a Mexican e-commerce marketplace. She had, in 2022–2024, led a post-iOS-14 attribution rebuild at a Chilean D2C brand that had, across a 14-month window, preserved ROAS inside a ±15% band on the rebuilt measurement and lifted Brand-Lift from 32 to 54. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited her in July 2024 specifically on two capabilities: her documented discipline of anchoring paid-media decisions on incrementality plus MMM rather than on platform-reported ROAS, and her willingness to hold A/B-test significance discipline against week-2 growth-team pressure. ## The Decision

Restrepo-Higuera had to commit, by the May 14 board pre-review, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Channel-mix rebuild.** Three archetypes: balanced-five-lever (Meta 32% / TikTok 22% / Google 18% / email 12% / influencer 16%, Composite 76), concentrated-Meta (Meta 62% — Composite 28, post-iOS signal loss), or TikTok-heavy (TikTok 45% — Composite 54). Working envelope: balanced-five-lever.
2. **Round 2 — A/B-test significance discipline.** Three archetypes: p-under-0.05-gate (ship-block on any lead without p<0.05, protected sample-size horizon, Composite 76), leader-at-day-three (early-stop on observed lead — Composite 28, false-positive probability), or directional (read direction without p-gate — Composite 50). Working envelope: p-under-0.05-gate.
3. **Round 3 — Attribution-architecture rebuild.** Three vectors: data-driven-plus-MMM (platform data-driven attribution coupled with quarterly MMM refresh, Composite 78), last-click (legacy last-click only — Composite 24, credit-assignment blindness), or mixed (data-driven without MMM — Composite 50). Working envelope: data-driven-plus-MMM.
4. **Round 4 — iOS-signal-loss rebuild.** Three vectors: incrementality-plus-aggregate (quarterly incrementality testing plus aggregate measurement API integration, Composite 78), Meta-overweight (defend the legacy Meta mix — Composite 26), or email-pivot (over-rotate to email/CRM — Composite 50). Working envelope: incrementality-plus-aggregate.
5. **Round 5 — Brand-and-performance embedding.** Three vectors: integrated-architecture (continuous brand-tracker, quarterly MMM refresh, board-visible composite dashboard, creative-effectiveness register, Composite 78), performance-only (preserve as a performance programme — Composite 34), or phased (tracker on two markets — Composite 54). Working envelope: integrated-architecture.

On Restrepo-Higuera's working model, a Balanced / p<0.05 / Data-Driven-MMM / Incrementality / Integrated envelope delivered CAC 20, LTV 138, Brand Lift 54, rebuilt ROAS 3.3×, Composite 74.

## Company Background

Belleza Andina had been founded in Medellín in 2018 and had, across eight years, built a USD 1.8M-monthly-revenue D2C beauty brand across Colombia, Peru, Chile and Mexico with a 38-SKU assortment anchored on clean-formulation skincare and colour-cosmetic products. The 2020–2024 growth programme had executed on a last-click-attribution architecture and had concentrated spend in Meta at 62% of the monthly envelope — a posture that the November 2025 internal review had flagged as structurally exposed to iOS-14 signal loss and to the expanding iOS-17+ privacy perimeter. The Q1 2026 board-approved USD 6M DigitalFunnel envelope had been positioned explicitly as a five-round performance-and-attribution-rebuild programme.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the March 2026 marketing-review dossier. First, the February 2026 Apple iOS update had shipped a tightening of aggregate-measurement reporting that had collapsed Meta-reported ROAS from 4.1× to 2.3× overnight and had triggered a platform-level signal-loss warning on 42% of the active campaign portfolio. Second, the December 2025 MMM vendor pilot had surfaced that 30% of observed lift across the 2023–2025 period had been attributable to TV and influencer channels that the last-click architecture had systematically under-credited — a finding that had repriced the perceived effectiveness of the legacy mix. Third, the January 2026 board paper had mandated a +40% revenue growth across a five-quarter horizon on the unchanged USD 520K-per-month media envelope.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2022–2025 post-iOS-14 performance-marketing literature had, across 38 documented D2C deployments, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, brands on concentrated-Meta mixes had documented post-iOS ROAS-collapse readings 2.8× more severe than brands on balanced-five-lever mixes. Second, brands anchored on data-driven-plus-MMM architectures had preserved credit-assignment accuracy readings above 70 in 76% of cases against 24% for brands on last-click-only architectures. Third, brands that had installed quarterly incrementality-test cadences had preserved rebuilt-ROAS inside a ±12% band through iOS-update cycles in 78% of cases against 22% for brands without incrementality.

Empirically, the post-2022 D2C brand-and-performance-integration literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, brands that had paired a continuous brand-tracker with a quarterly MMM refresh had delivered Brand-Lift uplifts of 12–20 points across an 18-month window in 74% of cases against 22% for performance-only programmes. Second, integrated board-visible composite dashboards had sustained marketing-effectiveness readings above 70 at the eighteen-month mark in 76% of cases against 24% for performance-only embeddings.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the February 2026 session, approved the USD 6M envelope and the June 28 review calendar. The Head of Growth had, on March 5, delivered the channel-mix diagnostic. The Head of Analytics had, on March 12, delivered the MMM pilot report. The Head of Brand had, on March 19, delivered the brand-lift diagnostic. The Chief Financial Officer had, on March 26, delivered the marketing-mix return profile.

### What Restrepo-Higuera knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. A Balanced / p<0.05 / Data-Driven-MMM / Incrementality / Integrated envelope projected Composite 74. A Concentrated-Meta / Leader-at-Day-3 / Last-Click / Meta-Overweight / Performance-Only envelope projected Composite 22. A Hybrid envelope projected Composite 46.

### What she did not know

Whether the iOS-signal-loss trajectory would deepen through the next operating-system cycle; whether the TikTok-and-influencer channel mix could clear the Q2 significance-and-safety gate; whether the +40% growth objective would be preserved or revised under the pending Q2 board-plan refresh; and whether the integrated board-visible dashboard would clear the November 2026 plan refresh under the new Chief-Financial-Officer reporting cadence.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Growth**: *"Balanced five lever. Sixty-two percent in Meta is a single point of failure now."*

**The Head of Analytics**: *"Data-driven-plus-MMM. Last-click has been wrong for two years. iOS just closed the denial window."*

**The Head of Brand**: *"Integrated architecture. Brand lift does not come out of a performance report."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Performance marketing is an attribution method. And attribution just changed. Build the rebuilt measurement. Pay the growth give-back."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Restrepo-Higuera had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Balanced / p<0.05 / Data-Driven-MMM / Incrementality / Integrated:** the recommended envelope. CAC 20. LTV 138. Brand Lift 54. ROAS 3.3× rebuilt. Composite 74. Recommended.
- **Option B — Concentrated-Meta / Leader-at-Day-3 / Last-Click / Meta-Overweight / Performance-Only:** the legacy envelope. Composite 22. Rejected — post-iOS signal-loss and credit-assignment probability above 72%.
- **Option C — Hybrid / Phased / Phased / Phased / Phased:** the partial envelope. Composite 46. Rejected — measurement architecture fragments under partial gating.

Restrepo-Higuera's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 4 — the iOS-signal-loss rebuild — because it was the round in which incrementality testing substituted for the lost click-level signal and preserved the brand's ability to defend rebuilt-ROAS at the board.
