## Abstract

Manufactura Atlas was, in the case universe, a LatAm industrial manufacturer operating eight plants across Mexico, Colombia and Peru, with USD 640M in 2025 revenue, a USD 12M Q4 2025 board-approved Oracle ERP implementation envelope across a 24-month horizon, a legacy plant-floor environment running on 14 disconnected spreadsheets and three aging MRP modules, a USD 2.1M 2025 IT-operations baseline, and a Q1 2026 board paper that had positioned the ProjectArena programme as the single highest-stakes operational investment of the 2026–2027 cycle. The December 2025 board had approved the USD 12M envelope, the 24-month go-live target of December 2027, a Chief-Information-Officer-led Program Director role, and a five-phase commitment cycle against a Schedule-Performance-Index (SPI) floor above 0.90, a Cost-Performance-Index (CPI) floor above 0.90, a Quality-at-Go-Live reading above 88, and a Composite ProjectArena Score above 72. The case opens at 6:52 a.m. on Thursday, April 23, 2026, at the Manufactura Atlas head office in Monterrey as **Andrés Carvajal-Ospina**, a 44-year-old Program Director, prepares the Phase-1 methodology-selection commitment. Sixty-six days separate him from the June 28 board steering-committee review.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Phase-1 methodology-selection commitment (hybrid envelope that combines waterfall discipline on scope with agile ceremonies on build vs. pure-waterfall envelope vs. pure-agile envelope) and diagnose when hybrid dominates under fixed-envelope constraints.
2. Analyze the Phase-2 scope-discipline commitment (9/10 scope-discipline envelope with a change-control-board gate vs. permissive envelope) and recommend the 9/10 envelope on the scope-creep-exposure basis.
3. Diagnose the Phase-3 change-management envelope (USD 400K envelope vs. USD 80K minimalist envelope) and recommend the USD 400K envelope on the stakeholder-buy-in and adoption-at-go-live basis.
4. Respond to the Phase-4 earned-value gate (SPI/CPI-anchored monthly gate vs. milestone-only gate) when an Operations-VP-sponsored mid-execution scope request drives SPI from 1.00 to 0.78 and CPI from 1.00 to 0.84 inside a single phase.
5. Design the Phase-5 go-live archetype that couples a reduced-scope-with-parallel-run envelope to the Phase-1 hybrid methodology and preserves the Composite ProjectArena Score above 72.

## The Protagonist

Andrés Carvajal-Ospina, 44, had assumed the Program Director role on January 4, 2026, after twelve years across SAP and Oracle ERP implementations at two Mexican manufacturing groups and a Houston-based oilfield-services integrator. He had, in 2022–2024, co-led a USD 9M Oracle Cloud implementation at a LatAm cement producer that, through a hybrid waterfall-plus-agile methodology and a parallel go-live, had closed at SPI 0.94 and CPI 0.91. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited him in November 2025 specifically on two capabilities: his documented discipline of anchoring scope-control boards inside the steering committee rather than delegating them to the PMO, and his willingness to defend a reduced-scope go-live against operational pressure for full-scope cutover. His 2026 variable compensation tied 35% to the SPI floor, 25% to the CPI floor and 20% to the Quality-at-Go-Live reading.

## The Decision

Carvajal-Ospina had to commit, by the May 14 steering-committee pre-review, the Phase-1 envelope and the directional intent for Phases 2–5.

1. **Phase 1 — Methodology.** Three archetypes: hybrid (waterfall discipline on scope and financials, agile ceremonies on build and test, Composite 76), waterfall (locked scope, serial gates — Composite 46, scope-change fragility), or agile (locked team, flexible scope — Composite 34, envelope-control fragility). Working envelope: hybrid.
2. **Phase 2 — Scope discipline.** Three archetypes: 9/10 anchored (change-control-board gating every mid-execution request, Composite 78), permissive (business-owner approval only — Composite 28, scope-inflation risk), or 7/10 (hybrid gating — Composite 56). Working envelope: 9/10 anchored.
3. **Phase 3 — Change-management envelope.** Three vectors: USD 400K envelope (training, super-users, communications, plant-level champions, Composite 76), USD 80K envelope (training only — Composite 32), or USD 200K envelope (phased — Composite 56). Working envelope: USD 400K.
4. **Phase 4 — Earned-value gate.** Three vectors: SPI/CPI-anchored monthly (PV, EV, AC reviewed in every steering-committee session, Composite 78), milestone-only (quarterly — Composite 34), or phased (SPI-only — Composite 52). Working envelope: SPI/CPI-anchored monthly.
5. **Phase 5 — Go-live archetype.** Four vectors: reduced-scope-with-parallel-run (non-critical modules deferred to wave two, parallel with legacy for 90 days, Composite 78), full-scope (Composite 34), parallel-full (Composite 46, double-running cost), or delay-three-months (Composite 54). Working envelope: reduced-scope-with-parallel-run.

On Carvajal-Ospina's working model, a Hybrid / 9-Scope / 400K-Change / EVM-Monthly / Reduced-and-Parallel envelope delivered SPI 0.95, CPI 0.92, Quality 92, Composite 73.

## Company Background

Manufactura Atlas had been founded in Monterrey in 1978 and had, across forty-eight years, built a USD 640M-revenue industrial manufacturer with eight plants across Mexico, Colombia and Peru, a 2,800-SKU assortment anchored on fabricated-metal components and pressure vessels, and a distribution footprint into automotive, oil-and-gas and construction-industrial customers across the Americas. The 2018–2024 IT cycle had left the group on a fragmented MRP/spreadsheet environment that the September 2025 diagnostic had priced at USD 6.8M in annual forgone EBITDA from inventory-accuracy and plant-scheduling gaps. The Q4 2025 board-approved USD 12M Oracle ERP envelope — the ProjectArena programme — had been positioned explicitly as a five-phase rebuild of the methodology, scope-control, change-management, earned-value-gate and go-live architecture, with a December 2027 cutover target.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the March 2026 steering-committee dossier. First, the November 2025 Oracle statement-of-work had confirmed the USD 12M envelope, a 24-month calendar and a USD 3.2M professional-services carve-out. Second, the January 2026 kick-off had produced a scope-baseline of 14 modules across finance, supply chain, manufacturing and HR, with a 100-point scope index used as the reference. Third, the March 2026 Operations-VP-sponsored request for three additional reporting modules had pushed the scope index from 100 to 122 in a single phase — the specific event the Phase-4 earned-value gate had been designed to catch.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2018–2025 LatAm ERP-implementation literature had, across 46 documented mid-market programmes above USD 8M, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, hybrid methodologies had delivered go-live SPI readings 14–22 points above pure-waterfall and 8–16 points above pure-agile in 72% of cases in the USD 8M–USD 20M range. Second, programmes with change-management envelopes above 4% of total budget had documented Quality-at-Go-Live readings 18–26 points above programmes below 1.5%, and adoption-at-60-day readings 24–32 points higher. Third, programmes anchored on monthly SPI/CPI earned-value gates had surfaced scope-creep events 3–5 months earlier than programmes on milestone-only gates, with 1.8× lower final CPI-erosion readings.

Empirically, the post-2018 go-live literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, reduced-scope-with-parallel-run go-lives had delivered Quality-at-Go-Live readings 12–20 points above full-scope cutovers in 74% of cases while preserving 82% of the Year-1 benefit case. Second, parallel-run envelopes exceeding 90 days had eroded CPI faster than they had preserved Quality — the 60-to-90-day parallel window had been the documented optimum.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the December 2025 session, approved the USD 12M envelope and the June 28 steering-committee-review calendar. The Oracle Functional Lead had, on March 5, delivered the scope-baseline workbook. The Change-Management Lead had, on March 12, delivered the stakeholder-and-adoption map. The PMO Director had, on March 19, delivered the earned-value-dashboard specification. The Plant-Operations Lead had, on March 26, delivered the cutover-readiness brief.

### What Carvajal-Ospina knew

The team had modelled three five-phase envelopes. A Hybrid / 9-Scope / 400K-Change / EVM-Monthly / Reduced-Parallel envelope projected Composite 73. A Waterfall / 5-Scope / 80K-Change / Milestone / Full-Scope envelope projected Composite 26. A Pure-Agile / 3-Scope / 200K-Change / Phased / Delay envelope projected Composite 38.

### What he did not know

Whether the Operations-VP-sponsored scope request would resurface inside Phase 4 after the scope-control board declined the mid-execution version; whether the Colombia and Peru plants would absorb the Mexico-calibrated change-management cadence on their local-language schedule; whether the 90-day parallel-run window would be sufficient for the inventory-reconciliation-at-cutover; and whether the December 2027 go-live target would clear the January 2028 year-end close without overlap risk.

### What others were saying

**The Oracle Functional Lead**: *"Hybrid methodology. Waterfall on the scope baseline, agile on the build-and-test ceremonies. That is how the envelope works."*

**The Change-Management Lead**: *"Four hundred thousand in change management. That is the line between eighty-percent adoption at go-live and forty-percent."*

**The PMO Director**: *"Monthly SPI and CPI. The earned-value dashboard surfaces the scope creep before it becomes a crisis."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Reduced scope. Parallel run. Ninety days. The iron triangle doesn't bend. It snaps."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Carvajal-Ospina had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Hybrid / 9-Scope / 400K-Change / EVM-Monthly / Reduced-and-Parallel:** the recommended envelope. SPI 0.95. CPI 0.92. Quality 92. Composite 73. Recommended.
- **Option B — Waterfall / 5-Scope / 80K-Change / Milestone / Full-Scope:** the minimalist envelope. Composite 26. Rejected — scope-inflation probability above 76% without the change-control gate.
- **Option C — Pure-Agile / 3-Scope / 200K-Change / Phased / Delay:** the permissive envelope. Composite 38. Rejected — envelope-control fragility under fixed USD 12M.

Carvajal-Ospina's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single phase most correlated with the final Composite was Phase 4 — the earned-value gate — because it was the gate that decided whether the Operations-VP-sponsored mid-execution scope request became an SPI-0.78 event the programme absorbed in April 2026 or an SPI-0.62 crisis the programme surfaced in the October 2027 board review.
