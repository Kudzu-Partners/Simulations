## Abstract

The Director of Internal Communications at the case-universe Santiago-headquartered pan-LATAM management consulting firm — approximately USD 180M in 2025 revenue, 620 client-facing professionals, 8 offices across Chile, Peru, Colombia, Mexico and Brazil — had been asked, in the February 2026 Managing Partner mandate, to deliver a Q3 2026 firm-wide business-writing curriculum with a six-document rewrite-audit anchored on measurable writing signals (word reduction, readability grade, passive-voice share, jargon density, structure discipline, tone discipline). The Q4 2025 client-deliverable audit had flagged a recurring failure mode: the firm's client-facing documents averaged readability grade 14.2 (Ph.D-level) against a target of 9.5 (early-college), passive-voice share 31% against a target of 12%, and jargon density approximately 3.4x the gold-standard benchmark across comparable consulting deliverables. The February 2026 mandate asked the Director of Internal Communications to work through six progressively bad document archetypes — bloated email, structureless memo, jargon-heavy board deck, crisis communication, data-dump report, and passive-aggressive Slack thread — rewrite each against the gold-standard benchmark, and commit to a curriculum the Managing Partner Council would stake on the answer-first/cut-hard/own-the-tone discipline rather than on the prose-elegance headline. The case opens at 1:52 p.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the firm's Santiago head office as **Camilo Herrera-Valdivia**, a 44-year-old Director of Internal Communications, prepares the six-document commitment.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Doc-1 Bloated Email setup (312 words for a meeting request, target 95, readability grade 14, rewrite to grade 9.4 score 91) and articulate why a Ph.D-grade readability on a calendar invite is a decision-speed problem rather than a prose-style problem — and why the 70% word-reduction is the single most visible signal in the before/after comparison.
2. Analyse the Doc-2 Structureless Memo setup (strategic memo rewritten using the Pyramid Principle: answer first, three reasons, supporting detail) and the Doc-3 Jargon Board Deck setup (synergize / leverage / paradigm rewritten to combine / use / change; jargon density drops 70%) and articulate why the Pyramid Principle is the single highest-leverage writing discipline for management communication and why jargon density is a direct proxy for communicative accountability.
3. Diagnose the Doc-4 Crisis Communications setup (passive voice at 42%, "mistakes were made" rewritten to "we made a mistake", score 88) and articulate why crisis writing is an accountability test rather than a legal test — and why the active-voice rewrite is the coaching intervention that most reliably converts a defensive crisis memo into an accountable one.
4. Respond to the Doc-5 Data Dump Report setup (14 charts, 2 arguments, rewrite: cut 8 charts, add a lead-sentence takeaway on each remaining chart, score 92) and the Doc-6 Passive-Aggressive Slack setup (rewrite: direct ask plus acknowledgement of the other person's constraint, score 85) and articulate why digital-communication discipline has become a measurable workplace-productivity variable and why the acknowledgement element on Slack is the single differentiator between a blocked thread and a moving one.
5. Design the six-document commitment that reads each writing artifact through the measurable-signal lens and commits to a four-module firm-wide curriculum (Answer First / Cut Hard / Own the Tone / Gold-Standard Comparison) for 620 client-facing staff, with an explicit before/after scoring discipline, articulating why writing is not decoration, it is decision-speed, and the business pivots on the answer-first, cut-hard, own-the-tone discipline rather than on the prose-elegance headline.

## The Protagonist

Herrera-Valdivia, 44, had been Director of Internal Communications since 2023, had spent seventeen years across journalism, public-affairs writing and internal-communications practice, and a three-year secondment at a Buenos Aires-based management consultancy's communications office in 2017–2020 where he ran the first measurable-signal rewrite audit of the firm's client deliverables. In 2024 he co-authored the firm's internal playbook *Answer First, Cut Hard, Own the Tone*, which framed business writing as a measurable-signal discipline rather than a prose-elegance discipline. The Managing Partner had assigned him the six-document commitment on three considerations: his command of the 2017–2020 audit methodology; his discipline of leading the Council read with the before/after scoring column rather than the gold-standard-by-narrative column; and his willingness to recommend a four-module curriculum that would pull every client-facing professional through the same benchmark.

## The Decision

Herrera-Valdivia had to commit, by the 4:30 p.m. Managing Partner Council pre-brief, the six-document commitment and the Q3 2026 firm-wide curriculum recommendation.

1. **Doc 1 — Bloated Email.** 312 words for a meeting request, target 95, readability grade 14. Rewrite to 95 words, grade 9.4, score 91.
2. **Doc 2 — Structureless Memo.** Rewrite using the Pyramid Principle: answer first, three reasons, supporting detail. Score 86.
3. **Doc 3 — Jargon Board Deck.** Synergize, leverage, paradigm rewritten to combine, use, change. Jargon density -70%. Score for rewritten deck tracked across the four modules.
4. **Doc 4 — Crisis Communications.** Passive voice from 42% to 11%. "Mistakes were made" to "we made a mistake". Accountable tone. Score 88.
5. **Doc 5 — Data Dump Report.** 14 charts to 6, lead-sentence takeaway on each. Score 92.
6. **Doc 6 — Passive-Aggressive Slack.** Direct ask + acknowledgement of the other person's constraint. Score 85.

Average score across six rewrites: 88. Word reduction: 58%. Readability grade: 9.4. Passive voice: 11%.

On Herrera-Valdivia's working commitment, the firm-wide curriculum recommendation combined four modules — Answer First (Docs 1, 2), Cut Hard (Docs 3, 5), Own the Tone (Docs 4, 6), and Gold-Standard Comparison (all six docs under the before/after scoring discipline) — across 620 client-facing professionals over 12 weeks in a hybrid self-paced plus live-coaching format. Budget USD 340k; expected cycle-time reduction on client deliverables approximately 18%.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1970–2025 business-writing literature produced three findings the commitment had to respect. First, Pyramid Principle discipline produced documented reader-decision-speed readings 34–48% faster than unstructured business memos across 1990–2024 comparable studies. Second, active-voice rewrites on crisis communications produced documented stakeholder-trust readings approximately 22% higher than passive-voice equivalents across 2015–2024 crisis-response samples. Third, readability-grade reductions from 14 to 10 produced documented reader-comprehension readings approximately 41 percentage points higher on comparable management-communication content.

The post-2015 workplace-productivity literature added two findings. Jargon-density reductions of 50%+ produced documented client-satisfaction improvements of 14–19% in consulting-deliverable reviews. And Slack-and-chat direct-ask-with-acknowledgement discipline produced documented thread-resolution-time reductions of approximately 28% in enterprise-communication studies.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The February 2026 Managing Partner mandate asked for the six-document commitment by Q3. The Q1 2026 document-archetype selection, the rewrite production against the gold-standard benchmark, and the four-module curriculum outline had all landed by April 16. The Managing Partner had asked Herrera-Valdivia to bring the commitment to the April 22 Council pre-brief.

### What Herrera-Valdivia knew

Measurable-signal rewriting dominates prose-elegance rewriting on reader-decision-speed and comprehension outcomes; Pyramid Principle discipline is the single highest-leverage writing intervention; active-voice crisis communication outperforms passive-voice; and direct-ask-with-acknowledgement discipline on Slack reduces thread-resolution time materially.

### What he did not know

Whether the 620-professional cohort would absorb the curriculum at the 12-week cadence or require a 16-week extended format; whether the Q4 client-NPS document-clarity dimension would produce office-level asymmetries; whether the USD 340k budget would clear the Finance Committee review; and whether the Managing Partner Council would accept a firm-wide mandatory curriculum or push for a voluntary cohort launch.

### What others were saying

**The Senior Communications Manager**: *"Four modules. Twelve weeks. Before-and-after scoring on every client deliverable. The curriculum is the architecture; the discipline is the product."*

**The Chief Learning Officer**: *"The Pyramid Principle is the single highest-leverage module. If one module has to drop, it is the Jargon module — jargon is a symptom; structure is the cause."*

**The Managing Partner**: *"If the curriculum reduces deliverable cycle-time by more than fifteen percent, the Council passes the budget."*

**The Head of Client Service**: *"The client-NPS document-clarity dimension is the external signal. If the curriculum moves that number from seven-point-two to seven-point-eight across the pan-LATAM portfolio, we have a case to take to every Managing Partner in the firm."*

## Options Under Consideration

Herrera-Valdivia had narrowed the space to three curriculum postures.

- **Option A — Four-module firm-wide mandatory curriculum (Answer First / Cut Hard / Own the Tone / Gold-Standard Comparison):** the recommended curriculum. Budget USD 340k; expected 18% deliverable cycle-time reduction; before/after scoring discipline on every client deliverable. Recommended.
- **Option B — Two-module voluntary cohort launch (Answer First + Own the Tone only):** rejected — captures roughly 45% of the value and fails the firm-wide-discipline commitment.
- **Option C — Three-module mandatory plus external-coach partnership:** rejected — overcommits the budget at USD 520k and duplicates internal learning-and-development capacity.

His private view was that Option A was the right curriculum and that the document most correlated with the Council's eventual acceptance was Doc 2 — the Structureless Memo rewritten under the Pyramid Principle — because it was the rewrite where the measurable-signal read diverged most clearly from the prose-elegance read that the pre-2024 curriculum had followed.
