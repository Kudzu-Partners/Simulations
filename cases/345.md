## Abstract

Grupo Servicios Caribe S.A. was, in the case universe, a 28-year-old Santo-Domingo-headquartered Dominican-Republic hospitality-and-facility-services group founded in 1998 by Don Federico Estévez-Medina, privately held, providing housekeeping, F&B, security and facility-management services to 42 hotels across Punta Cana, Bávaro, Santo Domingo, Puerto Plata and La Romana, 2025 consolidated revenue DOP 4.8B, 3,400 employees, with a Senior Manager of Operations role reporting to the COO. On **Monday, April 20, 2026**, at 8:15 a.m. Santo Domingo local, in the third-floor Operations office at the Av. 27 de Febrero corporate headquarters, a newly promoted Senior Manager opened her first four-conversation week — inherited from Jorge Estévez, "el que sabe todo", the highly directive predecessor who had taught the team to wait.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply the Whitmore 1992 GROW discipline — Goal, Reality, Options, Will — to the Carlos performance-gap conversation, keep the coachee's thinking at the center, and resist the directive-culture temptation to give the answer.
2. Apply the CCL SBI feedback framework — Situation, Behavior, Impact — to rewrite vague-or-character-attack-or-absent feedback into a specific, behavioral, impact-named formulation, and name the default-absent pattern of high-PDI cultures.
3. Apply the Downey 2003 directive-to-non-directive spectrum to four specific conversations — Carlos, Yolanda, Lissette, Roberto — pick the appropriate position for each, and track Montero's instinct-to-give-the-answer move-by-move.
4. Apply the Hofstede 2010 Dominican-Republic PDI-65 dimension and the Rosinski 2010 four-LATAM-resistance-pattern catalogue — El-Jefe-que-Sabe, Respeto-a-la-Jerarquía, Silencio-Protector, Aspiración-Indirecta — to the four conversations and adapt rather than translate the coaching approach.
5. Apply the Lombardo-Eichinger 1996 70-20-10 IDP framework to rewrite three Individual Development Plans with the primary action a stretch experience rather than a course, and articulate why *El Gerente que Desarrolla* (sim 345) is a peer-coaching senior-manager frame distinct from Banco Cor Multiplicadores (sim 338), which is a senior-leadership culture frame, and from Alex Kim FeedbackLoop (sim 266), which is a feedback-skills drill.

On the morning of **Monday, April 20, 2026**, at 8:15 a.m., **Diana Montero-Espaillat** opened the third-floor Operations office at the Grupo Servicios Caribe corporate headquarters on Av. 27 de Febrero 349, Santo Domingo. On her desk sat four files — Carlos, Yolanda, Lissette, Roberto. On her calendar sat four conversations — 9:30, 11:00, 14:00, 16:15. On her screen sat Jorge Estévez's handover document, which ended with one line: *"Si tienen dudas, te preguntan. Si te preguntan, contestás."* Her four-month tenure as Senior Manager had begun four weeks earlier. Her mandate — to be the coaching-culture role model — had begun that Monday.

---

## The Protagonist

Montero-Espaillat, 36, had graduated in 2013 from PUCMM in Administración Hotelera, had completed a Barna Management School MBA in 2019, had worked 2013–2020 at Hodelpa Hotels in F&B operations — rising to F&B manager at Hodelpa Garden Court in Santiago — and had joined Servicios Caribe in 2020 as supervisor, promoted to department head in 2023, and promoted to Senior Manager of Operations on March 23, 2026. Her seven reports included Carlos (supervisor, five years tenure, performance gap), Yolanda (high-potential, twenty-eight, recent DOP 180K error), Lissette (supervisor, eight years tenure, in open conflict with Yolanda), Roberto (supervisor, high-performer, with a thirty-percent-higher offer from a Punta Cana competitor), and three additional reports not in active coaching focus that week. Her HR Director, Rosa Estévez-Medina, 48, had been the one to name the coaching-culture mandate explicitly.

---

## The Decision

The Senior Manager had to commit, across the first 90 days of her promotion, a three-round manager-as-coach package.

1. **Round 1 — GROW + SBI.** Carlos performance-gap conversation.
2. **Round 2 — Downey spectrum.** Four conversations mapped.
3. **Round 3 — Culture + IDP.** High-PDI adaptation, 70-20-10 plan.

---

## Sectoral Background

The 2020–2025 Caribbean hospitality-services sector had, in the case universe, recorded an average 31% annual turnover across supervisor and front-line roles, with Punta Cana and Bávaro running structurally higher at 38–42% due to resort-pool poaching. Employee engagement surveys across the Dominican hospitality-services set scored at 48 (Gallup-normalized) against a LATAM median of 52 and a global median of 66, with "my manager asks before telling" scoring at 31 against a global 54. Hofstede's Dominican-Republic Power Distance Index sat at 65 (high) and Individualism at 30 (low-collectivist), producing the five Rosinski-named LATAM resistance patterns to manager-as-coach adoption. The 1992 Whitmore *Coaching for Performance*, the 1998 CCL SBI model, the 2003 Downey *Effective Coaching*, the 2010 Hofstede-Hofstede-Minkov *Cultures and Organizations*, the 2010 Rosinski *Coaching Across Cultures* and the 1996 Lombardo-Eichinger *Career Architect* were the anchors the simulation had to respect.

## The Situation

### How Montero got here

The 1998 Servicios Caribe founding; the 2013 Montero PUCMM graduation and Hodelpa Santiago hire; the 2020 Servicios Caribe supervisor entry; the 2023 department-head promotion; the 2026 Jorge Estévez retirement announcement; the February 2026 coaching-culture company-wide announcement; the March 23 Senior Manager promotion; the April 20 first Monday of the first four-conversation week.

### What Montero knew

The GROW model and the SBI framework from a Q4 2025 corporate workshop; the Downey spectrum from her Barna MBA leadership course; the Hofstede PDI 65 figure from the Q1 2026 coaching-culture kickoff deck; the four Rosinski LATAM resistance patterns from the same kickoff; the 70-20-10 IDP discipline from Rosa Estévez-Medina's April 3 HR leadership session; the four files on her desk; Jorge's twelve-year directive-culture legacy in the team.

### What she did not know

Whether Carlos would accept a non-directive conversation as useful or read it as Montero avoiding the feedback call; whether Yolanda would hear the Downey non-directive development reframe or stay in auto-flagelación; whether Lissette would accept coaching-mediation or demand arbitration; whether Roberto would be retained by a career conversation or by a counter-offer alone; whether the remaining three reports — and the other seven supervisors on her parallel radar — would read her coaching posture as weakness in a PDI-65 culture.

### What others were saying

**Rosa Estévez-Medina (48, HR Director)**: *"Diana, te formamos en una cultura directiva. Ahora querés desaprenderla. Cada conversación cuenta."*

**Jorge Estévez (retired predecessor)**: *"Si tienen dudas, te preguntan. Si te preguntan, contestás."*

**Carlos (supervisor, 47, performance gap)**: *"Jefa, dígame qué tengo que hacer."*

**Yolanda (high-potential, 28, DOP 180K error)**: *"Fui yo. No tengo excusa. Renuncio si me lo pide."*

**Lissette (supervisor, 41, conflict with Yolanda)**: *"Yolanda no respeta el protocolo de housekeeping."*

**Roberto (supervisor, 34, Punta Cana offer)**: *"Treinta por ciento más, jefa. Pero me formé aquí."*

**COO Ricardo Peguero-Burgos (54)**: *"La cultura de coaching se hace o no se hace. La sim dice tiene rumbo. Demuéstralo esta semana."*

## Options Under Consideration

The Senior Manager had narrowed the first-week coaching commitment to three postures.

- **Option A — GROW + SBI on Carlos at 9:30 with Montero at the non-directive end of Downey for the first twenty minutes of the conversation before any advice is offered; Downey directive-coaching-plus-development reframe on Yolanda at 11:00 separating the DOP 180K error from her worth and moving her into an IDP with a stretch assignment; Downey mediation-coaching on Lissette-Yolanda at 14:00 asking each to name the other's legitimate concern before surfacing the housekeeping-protocol substantive issue; Downey non-directive-plus-career-coaching on Roberto at 16:15 resisting the counter-offer reflex and letting him articulate the pull toward the Punta Cana competitor before Montero offers anything; Hofstede-Rosinski adaptation reading each conversation through the PDI-65 lens and naming Silencio-Protector when it fires; three IDPs rewritten with a stretch experience as the primary action (Recommended).** Respects the Whitmore / CCL / Downey / Hofstede / Rosinski / Lombardo-Eichinger canon; builds the coaching-culture proof of concept in the first four conversations; protects Roberto before the offer window closes.
- **Option B — Directive posture across all four conversations — Montero tells each person what to do, fires SBI feedback on Carlos, accepts Yolanda's resignation offer, arbitrates the Lissette-Yolanda conflict, counter-offers Roberto on salary.** Rejected — preserves short-term resolution speed and signals "la jefa sabe" in a PDI-65 culture, but contradicts the coaching-culture mandate, destroys the Yolanda retention case, and locks Montero into Jorge's inherited pattern.
- **Option C — Escalate all four conversations to Rosa Estévez-Medina and the COO pending further coaching training.** Forfeits the first-90-day window and signals that the promotion was premature.

Montero's view was that Option A was the right commitment, and that the single decision most correlated with the 12-month outcome was Yolanda — because the 2022–2026 Caribbean hospitality-services turnover record documented that high-potential young professionals who experienced their first major error followed by a directive-shame conversation left within six months at 74%, against 11% when the same error was reframed as a development moment under Downey non-directive discipline. The *El Gerente que Desarrolla* frame sits at the senior-manager-peer-coaching tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from Banco Cor Multiplicadores (338) which is a senior-leadership culture frame, and from Alex Kim FeedbackLoop (266) which is a feedback-skills drill.
