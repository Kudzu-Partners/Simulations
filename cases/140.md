## Abstract

AURELIA RETAIL CHAIN was, in the case universe, a pan-Iberian retail chain with USD 3.2B in 2025 revenue, 412 stores across Spain, Portugal and Andorra, a private-label range representing 28% of gross sales, and a 2026 annual-procurement-spend envelope of USD 1.8B across roughly 2,400 active suppliers and seventy-two category families. The 2023–2025 procurement-operating model had been anchored on a transactional three-RFPs-per-category-per-year discipline that had delivered cost-savings run-rate inside the 2–4% band against a pan-European peer-benchmark of 5–7% and an internal 2026 target of 6% — a shortfall that the Q4 2025 CFO memo had priced at USD 36M of annualised gross-profit forgone. The Q1 2026 board had approved a USD 1.8B spend envelope and a Chief-Procurement-Officer-led four-round procurement-rebuild cycle against a cost-savings floor above 5%, a Supplier-Relationship Score above 72 on the strategic-supplier cohort, an ESG Score floor above 55 against the 2025 baseline of 38, and a composite Procurement Score above 72. The case opens at 5:58 a.m. on Tuesday, April 14, 2026, at the Aurelia Retail Chain central office in Barcelona as **Noa Feliu**, a 44-year-old Chief Procurement Officer, prepares the Round-1 Kraljic-segmentation commitment. Sixty-eight days separate her from the June 23 board procurement-review.

## The Protagonist

Noa Feliu, 44, had joined Aurelia Retail Chain as Chief Procurement Officer on February 3, 2026, from a Tier-1 European grocery retailer where she had, across twelve years, led two category-management rebuilds on packaging and private-label grocery, and had closed a 2022 Kraljic-matrix-anchored procurement transformation that had delivered 5.4% of aggregate cost-savings while moving the Strategic-quadrant supplier-relationship score from 52 to 78. Before that, she had spent six years as a Director of Procurement at a Madrid-based fashion retailer. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited her specifically on the strength of two capabilities: her experience resegmenting category portfolios under a Kraljic discipline, and her reputation for refusing to ship a sourcing event that had not cleared a documented quadrant-to-mode mapping. Her 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the Cost-Savings outcome and 20% to the ESG-Score floor.

## The Decision

Feliu had to commit, by the April 28 procurement-committee pre-review, the Round-1 Kraljic-segmentation envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–4.

1. **Round 1 — Kraljic category-segmentation.** Seventy-two category families × two axes (supply risk, spend importance). Three archetypes: quadrant-differentiated-segmentation (18 Leverage, 14 Strategic, 31 Non-Critical, 9 Bottleneck categories — each with a quadrant-specific relationship mode, Composite 76), blanket-transactional-segmentation (all categories on three-RFPs-per-year transactional relationship, Composite 42), or two-bucket (strategic-versus-transactional only — Composite 58, Bottleneck-quadrant risk blindspot). Working envelope: quadrant-differentiated.
2. **Round 2 — Sourcing-mode selection.** Three archetypes: quadrant-matched-sourcing (reverse-auction on Leverage-quadrant categories with three-bidder-floor, joint-roadmap-plus-co-investment on Strategic-quadrant categories with twelve-month horizon, inventory-buffer-plus-second-source on Bottleneck, standardise-and-consolidate on Non-Critical — Cost-Savings 6.2%, Supplier-Relationship Score 74), blanket-reverse-auction (reverse-auction across all 72 categories — Cost-Savings 4.8%, Supplier-Relationship Score 42, Strategic-supplier-attrition risk), or blanket-partnership (joint-roadmap on all categories — Cost-Savings 2.1%, Supplier-Relationship Score 82, cost-savings floor forfeited). Working envelope: quadrant-matched.
3. **Round 3 — Make-versus-buy on Private-Label Packaging.** Category is Strategic-quadrant (high supply risk on specification compliance, high spend importance). Three vectors: keep-in-house (retain existing Strategic-quadrant supplier relationship, joint-roadmap continuity, Cost-Savings on category flat but Supplier-Relationship Score uplift), outsource (6% category-level cost-savings at USD 12M annualised, Supplier-Relationship Score downgrade, shelf-continuity risk on private-label packaging integrity), or dual-source-with-legacy-partner (split volume, Cost-Savings 3%, Supplier-Relationship Score preserved). Working envelope: keep-in-house.
4. **Round 4 — URGENT Tier-2 cotton-supplier audit-failure event.** Event: the Q2 2026 Tier-2 audit surfaces a labour-standards violation at a Tier-2 cotton supplier supplying approximately 18% of the private-label apparel range. Three vectors: supplier-development (USD 200K investment across a nine-month remediation plan — Tier-2 supplier passes re-audit by Q4, timeline intact, ESG Score uplift +12), supplier-termination (exit relationship immediately — timeline-break risk on Q3 2026 private-label apparel launch, ESG Score uplift +6 but continuity penalty), or escalate-and-wait (keep volume flowing through remediation period, ESG Score drift −8, regulatory-disclosure risk inside CSRD framework). Working envelope: supplier-development.

On Feliu's working model, a Quadrant-Differentiated / Quadrant-Matched-Sourcing / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development envelope delivered Cost Savings 5.8%, Supplier-Relationship Score 74, ESG Score 58, Composite 78. A Blanket-Transactional / Blanket-Reverse-Auction / Outsource / Supplier-Termination envelope delivered Cost Savings 4.8% (lower net of Strategic-supplier disruption cost), Supplier-Relationship Score 38, ESG Score 44, Composite 42. A Two-Bucket / Quadrant-Matched / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development envelope delivered Cost Savings 4.9%, Supplier-Relationship Score 66, ESG Score 56, Composite 64 — trajectory that cleared ESG floor but forfeited Cost-Savings-above-5%.

## Company Background

Aurelia Retail Chain had been founded in Barcelona in 1978 as a single general-merchandise store and had, across forty-eight years, grown into a pan-Iberian chain of 412 stores across Spain (384), Portugal (26) and Andorra (2), with USD 3.2B in 2025 revenue, a 2.4-million-active-customer loyalty program, and a 2025 private-label range representing 28% of gross sales across apparel, home and fresh-food categories. The 2023–2025 procurement operating model had been anchored on a transactional-discipline approach that, across seventy-two category families and approximately 2,400 active suppliers, had delivered run-rate cost savings of 2–4% against the 2026 6% target — a shortfall whose root cause the 2025 internal audit had attributed to a quadrant-blind sourcing approach that applied reverse-auctions to Strategic-quadrant packaging categories and joint-roadmaps to Non-Critical office-supplies categories.

The Q1 2026 board dossier had identified three specific pressures the four-round envelope would inherit. First, the 2025 ESG Score of 38 had placed Aurelia outside the pan-European-retail sustainability-benchmark band of 55–68 — a gap the Q1 2026 EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive disclosure calendar had placed at the centre of the 2026 investor-roadshow narrative. Second, the Q1 2026 Tier-2 cotton-supplier audit cycle had flagged one major supplier for a labour-standards violation. Third, the Q1 2026 private-label apparel Q3 launch plan had committed the Tier-2 cotton volume against a fixed-timeline landing schedule.

## Industry and Market Context

The European retail strategic-procurement landscape in 2026 was characterised by three structural features the envelope sat inside. First, the 2018–2024 Kraljic-transformation evidence base had, across 68 European retail and consumer-goods procurement rebuilds, documented a Cost-Savings differential of 2.4–3.8 points between quadrant-differentiated envelopes and blanket-transactional envelopes — a pattern that anchored the Round-1 commitment. Second, the 2020–2024 quadrant-matched sourcing literature had, across 42 European retail portfolios, placed quadrant-matched-sourcing commitments at a Supplier-Relationship Score differential of 28–34 points against blanket-reverse-auction envelopes. Third, the 2018–2024 Tier-2 supplier-development literature had, across 38 retail-apparel Tier-2 audit-failure events, placed supplier-development envelopes at a twelve-month ESG-Score uplift of 8–14 points against supplier-termination envelopes while preserving continuity in 82% of cases against 46% for termination responses.

Empirically, the post-2022 procurement literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, portfolios that had differentiated supplier-relationship modes by Kraljic quadrant had, across a 2019–2024 sample of 48 European retailers, delivered cost savings above 5% in 79% of cases against 26% for portfolios on blanket-transactional modes. Second, portfolios that had retained in-house capability on Strategic-quadrant private-label-packaging categories had, across the same sample, preserved Supplier-Relationship Score above 72 in 81% of cases against 32% for portfolios that had outsourced Strategic-quadrant categories.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Chief Executive Officer had, in the November 2025 board review, approved the USD 1.8B envelope and the Chief-Procurement-Officer-led rebuild mandate. The Category-Management Lead had, on February 17, delivered the seventy-two-category Kraljic-segmentation first draft. The Sustainable-Sourcing Manager had, on February 24, delivered the Tier-2 supplier-audit programme. The Chief Financial Officer had, on March 2, confirmed the USD 36M gross-profit-forgone estimate. The Head of Private-Label had, on March 9, delivered the make-vs-buy diagnostic on private-label packaging. The Tier-2 cotton-supplier audit failure had landed on March 23 with a Q2 remediation-or-termination clock.

### What Feliu knew

The team had modelled three four-round envelopes. A Quadrant-Differentiated / Quadrant-Matched-Sourcing / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development Envelope projected Composite 78. A Blanket-Transactional / Blanket-Reverse-Auction / Outsource / Supplier-Termination Envelope projected Composite 42. A Two-Bucket / Quadrant-Matched / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development Envelope projected Composite 64.

### What she did not know

Whether the seventy-two-category Kraljic segmentation would, on the deeper spend-analytics review inside Round 2, surface re-classifications that shifted five-to-eight Non-Critical categories into the Leverage quadrant; whether the Tier-2 cotton-supplier remediation would clear the nine-month timeline inside the Q4 2026 re-audit window; whether the pan-European competitive-retail environment would, inside H2 2026, compress the Cost-Savings target from 6% toward 7%; and whether the Q3 2026 EU CSRD disclosure calendar would expand the Tier-2-audit scope to cover additional category families.

### What others were saying

**The Category-Management Lead**: *"Quadrant-differentiated. Eighteen Leverage, fourteen Strategic, thirty-one Non-Critical, nine Bottleneck. Every category gets its own sourcing mode."*

**The Sustainable-Sourcing Manager**: *"Supplier-development on the Tier-2 cotton failure. Termination breaks the Q3 launch."*

**The Head of Private-Label**: *"Keep packaging in-house. The supplier relationship protecting our shelf is worth more than the six percent."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Procurement isn't buying cheaper. It's buying right."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Feliu had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Quadrant-Differentiated / Quadrant-Matched-Sourcing / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development:** the recommended envelope. Cost Savings 5.8%. Supplier-Relationship Score 74. ESG Score 58. Composite 78. Recommended.
- **Option B — Blanket-Transactional / Blanket-Reverse-Auction / Outsource / Supplier-Termination:** the cost-only envelope. Composite 42. Rejected — Cost-Savings dilution from Strategic-supplier disruption and ESG-Score forfeit.
- **Option C — Two-Bucket / Quadrant-Matched / Keep-In-House / Supplier-Development:** the simplified envelope. Composite 64. Rejected — forfeits Cost-Savings-above-5% on Bottleneck-quadrant blind spot.

Feliu's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 1 — the seventy-two-category Kraljic segmentation that anchored each downstream round's sourcing-mode, make-vs-buy and audit-response decision inside a quadrant-consistent framework.
