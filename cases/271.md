## Abstract

The Head of Quantitative Risk at the case-universe Bogotá-headquartered systematic asset manager — approximately COP 2.8 trillion under management across Colombian AFP pension mandates, family-office multi-asset mandates and two fixed-income funds, 46 quant analysts and 12 senior portfolio engineers — had been asked, in the March 2026 Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia thematic-review letter, to deliver an April 22 response covering the firm's systematic stack and the six linear-algebra diagnostics the Superintendencia had flagged during the February 2026 on-site inspection. The Q1 2026 internal validation had surfaced a recurring failure mode across the quant function: portfolio engineers and recommender-system engineers were signing off on production models on back-test-headline-return evidence, not on the numerical-stability column of the validation report — ill-conditioned covariance matrices, PCA truncation that dropped predictive content, Markov transition matrices that passed the row-sum-to-one check while hiding a zeroed cell, Leontief inversions on matrices that violated Hawkins-Simon feasibility, and SVDs that kept more singular values than the matrix could support. The March 2026 letter asked the Head of Quantitative Risk to work through the six flags — one covariance, one PCA, one Markov, one Leontief, one SVD, one correlated-Brownian — and commit to a matrix-governance remediation the SFC would accept as the basis for keeping the firm's licence. The case opens at 2:06 p.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the firm's Chicó head office as **Camila Restrepo-Arango**, a 44-year-old Head of Quantitative Risk, prepares the six-flag commitment.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Flag-1 covariance-matrix setup (estimation window, condition number 10,200 on a 34-asset Colombian equity covariance, mean-variance optimiser amplifying rounding error into +340% long Ecopetrol and –280% short Pacific Rubiales, prospectus ten-percent single-position cap breached, Decreto 2555 already violated) and articulate why the condition number is the pre-optimisation diagnostic that matters, and why shrinkage or factor-model covariances dominate raw sample covariance at this condition-number scale.
2. Analyse the Flag-2 PCA-truncation setup (34 factors, eight components retained at the 85% cumulative-variance threshold, ninth component — 3% of variance — dropped, ninth component carrying the pension-reform-redemption signal, COP 180bn client outflows in six weeks) and articulate why in-sample variance explained is the wrong retention criterion on a predictive PCA, and why out-of-sample predictive contribution is the right one.
3. Diagnose the Flag-3 Markov-transition setup (five-state client-lifecycle matrix, Dormant→Churned transition cell zeroed by a coding error, rows still summing to one, every CK-equation row-validation passing, client lifetime value overstated by COP 8.2bn) and articulate why row-sum validation is necessary but not sufficient — cell-level Chapman-Kolmogorov coherence is the correct discipline.
4. Respond to the Flag-4 Leontief (I–A)⁻¹ cost-allocation inversion (nine-fund cost-sharing matrix, Hawkins-Simon feasibility condition violated, two AFP funds thought profitable flipping to loss-making under the correct inversion) and to the Flag-5 SVD-recommender setup (recommender matrix nominally 180×34 but effective rank 12, 25 singular values retained, noise fitted as signal, COP 1.2bn of alternatives pushed to conservative AFP clients) and articulate why Hawkins-Simon and rank tests are non-negotiable pre-inversion and pre-SVD checks.
5. Design the six-flag commitment that reads each matrix through the numerical-stability lens and commits to a governance framework — condition-number triggers, Chapman-Kolmogorov cell checks, Hawkins-Simon feasibility tests, SVD rank tests, PCA out-of-sample retention thresholds, correlated-Brownian off-diagonal reporting — with a quarterly re-validation cadence and an SFC-aligned escalation protocol, articulating why every matrix tells a story and the governance is what keeps it out of the press release.

## The Protagonist

Restrepo-Arango, 44, had been Head of Quantitative Risk since 2023, had spent fifteen years across Colombian buy-side quant roles, a two-year postdoctoral appointment at Universidad de los Andes in 2013–2015 on numerical linear algebra for covariance estimation, and a three-year secondment at a New York multi-asset manager in 2019–2022 where she had led the post-incident review on a covariance-condition-number blow-up that had cost USD 42M across the firm's LATAM mandates. In 2024 she co-authored the firm's internal playbook *Before the Optimiser, the Diagnostics*, which framed matrix-level numerical stability as a pre-production discipline rather than a post-production audit artefact. The CRO had assigned her the six-flag commitment on three considerations: her command of the 2013–2015 Uniandes work on shrinkage covariance estimation; her discipline of leading the risk read with the condition-number column rather than the back-test return column; and her willingness to recommend an SFC-facing governance commitment that the portfolio engineers would find uncomfortable.

## The Decision

Restrepo-Arango had to commit, by the 4:30 p.m. CRO pre-brief, the six-flag remediation commitment and the governance-framework memorandum that would go to the SFC by Monday.

1. **Flag 1 — Covariance.** Condition number 10,200. Shrink to Ledoit-Wolf or move to a factor-model covariance. Trigger at 1,000.
2. **Flag 2 — PCA.** Retain the ninth component. Switch retention criterion from in-sample variance to out-of-sample predictive contribution.
3. **Flag 3 — Markov.** Repair the Dormant→Churned cell to 0.12. Impose cell-level Chapman-Kolmogorov validation.
4. **Flag 4 — Leontief.** Re-derive the A matrix. Hawkins-Simon feasibility check as a pre-inversion gate.
5. **Flag 5 — SVD.** Truncate at rank 12. Mandatory rank-test before any SVD deployment.
6. **Flag 6 — Correlated Brownian.** Restore off-diagonal terms. Report the full covariance to the hedging desk, not the diagonal.

Combined flagged AUM exposure: COP 1.4 trillion — about half the firm's book. Combined estimated client-facing remediation cost: COP 1,800M across legal, client-retention and rebate. Porvenir AFP — a COP 420bn mandate — had asked for a briefing by the end of the week. Two family-office clients had put redemption notices on the desk already.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1950–2025 numerical-linear-algebra literature produced three findings the commitment had to respect. First, shrinkage and factor-model covariance estimators produced documented out-of-sample portfolio-variance readings 22–38% lower than raw sample covariance on comparable 2010–2024 emerging-market equity universes, with the margin widening as the condition number rose. Second, PCA retention by out-of-sample predictive contribution produced documented signal-retention readings approximately 1.4–1.8× higher than variance-explained retention on comparable predictive-PCA studies. Third, Hawkins-Simon feasibility and SVD rank tests produced documented inversion-error reductions of 60–90% across the comparable 2015–2024 operations-research literature.

The post-2000 model-governance literature added two findings. Mandatory pre-production numerical-stability gates produced documented model-failure-rate reductions of 45–55% across comparable 2018–2024 regulated-buy-side portfolios. And cell-level Chapman-Kolmogorov validation on Markov models produced documented transition-matrix-error detection rates approximately 3× higher than row-sum-only validation.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The February 2026 SFC on-site inspection produced six observations on the systematic stack. Restrepo-Arango's team had reproduced every one of the six flags by the end of March. Porvenir AFP had asked for a briefing on April 18. The CRO had asked for the six-flag commitment by April 22.

### What Restrepo-Arango knew

Condition-number-driven covariance blow-ups are the single most common source of mean-variance optimiser catastrophes; PCA retention by in-sample variance is a documented failure mode; row-sum-only Markov validation misses cell-level errors; Hawkins-Simon violations and SVD rank errors are both reliably caught by pre-inversion tests.

### What she did not know

Whether the SFC response would be accepted on the memorandum alone or would require a follow-on on-site; whether Porvenir would stay in the mandate after the briefing; whether the two family-office redemption notices would become nine family-office redemption notices by month-end; and whether the 2027 SFC rule-set would land before or after the firm's re-authorisation window.

### What others were saying

**The Senior Portfolio Engineer**: *"The back-test looked fine. The problem is the numerical stack underneath the back-test."*

**The Chief Risk Officer**: *"If the six flags are genuinely closed and the governance framework is credible, the SFC will accept the memorandum. If any one of the six is still open on Monday, we lose the licence conversation."*

**The Head of Client Coverage**: *"Porvenir's investment committee meets Thursday. If we do not have a written commitment on the Markov cell and the PCA component by Wednesday evening, they will not put the mandate to a vote."*

**The General Counsel**: *"The Decreto 2555 single-position breach is the exposure we cannot argue our way out of. The commitment has to include the covariance remediation as a precondition for the SFC reply."*

## Options Under Consideration

Restrepo-Arango had narrowed the space to three remediation postures.

- **Option A — Six-flag remediation with a numerical-stability governance framework (Recommended):** condition-number triggers at 1,000; PCA out-of-sample retention thresholds; Chapman-Kolmogorov cell validation; Hawkins-Simon and SVD rank gates; correlated-Brownian off-diagonal reporting; quarterly re-validation; SFC-aligned escalation. Remediation cost COP 1,800M. Recommended.
- **Option B — Close the three major flags (covariance, PCA, Markov) and defer Leontief, SVD and correlated-Brownian:** rejected — leaves the AFP cost-allocation exposure open and the recommender-system exposure open, and the SFC letter is explicit that all six must close.
- **Option C — Replace the systematic stack with an off-the-shelf vendor platform:** rejected — the vendor platform has its own condition-number and rank-retention defaults, and the lift-and-shift would take nine months, past the mandate-renewal window.

Her private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the flag most correlated with SFC acceptance was Flag 1 — the Decreto 2555 covariance breach — because it was the flag where the firm's legal exposure diverged most sharply from the numerical-stability read that the model had missed.
