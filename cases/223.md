## Abstract

Kepler Multi-Factor was, in the case universe, an Amsterdam-headquartered systematic multi-factor asset manager with USD 850M in AUM, a five-factor palette (Value, Momentum, Quality, Size, Low-Vol), a global-equity benchmark with regional tilts, a 2022–2025 prior-strategy Sharpe of 0.96 across the predecessor fund, a ten-period regime-rotation simulator co-developed with the University-of-Maastricht quant-finance department and a 2025 institutional-client roster of twelve allocators at an average investment of USD 70M. The 2024–2025 cycle had left the manager at a factor-allocator-disciplined but regime-switching-exposed posture: the allocator tool's Bayesian regime-classifier had correctly flagged the late-expansion phase on March 18, 2026 with a posterior probability of 0.82, and had issued a tilt suggestion (Quality 35 / Momentum 30 / Low-Vol 15 / Value 10 / Size 10) that had generated a trailing +11% return and a 1.14 Sharpe across the first two simulation periods. The Q2 2026 context had compounded three pressures: a recession-probability lift in the central-bank-nowcast to 0.47, a Momentum-factor drawdown that had printed -18% across a single stressed period, and a factor-dispersion widening from 9% to 18% that had re-priced factor-rotation decisions. The case opens at 6:48 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the Kepler Multi-Factor Amsterdam trading floor as **Camila Neubauer-Arrondo**, a 43-year-old Portfolio Manager for Multi-Factor Strategies, prepares the Phase-1 regime-diagnosis commitment.

Diversification across them is not."*

## The Protagonist

Neubauer-Arrondo, 43, had assumed the Portfolio-Manager role for Multi-Factor Strategies at Kepler on April 1, 2023, after sixteen years across quant research, portfolio management and strategy leadership at two European quant houses and one US-based systematic manager. She had, in 2018–2022, managed a USD 1.6B multi-factor strategy at a predecessor house that had printed a 1.02 Sharpe across five years through a documented equal-weight-across-five-factors default discipline that had beaten the average of dynamic-tilt allocations by +240 bps cumulatively. She had, in 2020, publicly rejected a tactical rotation into Low-Vol at the onset of the pandemic drawdown on the grounds that the regime-classifier had not exited the expansion window, and had absorbed a short-duration underperformance before the recovery rewarded the equal-weight posture. The Kepler management committee had recruited her on three capabilities: her documented discipline of equal-weighting factors when the regime-classifier carried posterior probability below 0.90, her willingness to ignore allocator-suggested tilts that reflected trailing-window performance rather than forward-looking regime priors, and her command of drawdown-managed outcome discipline that had capped max drawdowns below -12% on two prior strategies.

## The Decision

Neubauer-Arrondo had to commit, by the 8:00 a.m. investment-committee session, the Phase-1 regime-diagnosis envelope and the directional intent for Phases 2–10.

1. **Phase 1 — Regime diagnosis.** A single envelope: accept the Bayesian regime-classifier's late-expansion posterior of 0.82 and the factor-cycle-window reading. Working envelope: accept.
2. **Phase 2 — Allocator-suggested tilt.** Three envelopes. A full-tilt envelope (Quality 35 / Momentum 30 / Low-Vol 15 / Value 10 / Size 10; trailing +11%; Sharpe 1.14). A half-tilt envelope (blend 50% allocator-tilt, 50% equal-weight). A no-tilt envelope (equal-weight throughout). Working envelope: half-tilt for Phases 2–3.
3. **Phase 4 — Regime switch.** A single envelope: accept the contraction-regime print and the factor-return pattern (Momentum -18%, Value -9%, Quality -4%, Low-Vol +2%, Size -14%). Working envelope: accept.
4. **Phase 5 — Allocation decision.** Three archetypes. An aggressive-rotation envelope (shift into Low-Vol at 40% weight; tactical). A hold-tilt envelope (maintain the late-expansion Quality-Momentum tilt; accept the contraction-phase drag). A equal-weight envelope (20/20/20/20/20 across five factors; default to diversification). Working envelope: equal-weight.
5. **Phases 6–10 — Equal-weight discipline.** A single envelope: maintain equal-weight through Phases 6–10 regardless of interim regime prints. Working envelope: accept.
6. **Phase 10 — Outcome dashboard.** Target envelope: +9.8% annualised, Sharpe 1.04, max drawdown -11%, low factor dispersion.

On Neubauer-Arrondo's working model, an Accept / Half-Tilt / Accept / Equal-Weight / Hold-Equal-Weight / +9.8%-Sharpe-1.04 envelope delivered the Phase-10 dashboard above and outperformed the aggressive-Low-Vol tactical rotation by +320 bps cumulatively across Phases 5–8.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2015–2025 systematic-multi-factor literature had, across 58 documented ten-phase factor-allocation cycles on USD 500M–USD 2B AUM strategies, produced three findings the allocation had to respect. First, equal-weight-across-factors default envelopes had, on ten-period cycles that included at least one regime switch, outperformed tactical-rotation envelopes by an average of +280 bps cumulatively in 64% of documented cases. Second, Bayesian-regime-classifier tilt envelopes with posterior thresholds inside 0.85 had delivered Sharpe lifts of 0.04–0.09 against equal-weight alternatives in 52% of cases, but had compressed Sharpe by 0.10–0.18 in the remaining 48%. Third, drawdown-managed outcome envelopes with max-drawdown caps inside -12% had preserved allocator retention at a 2.1:1 premium versus uncapped strategies.

Empirically, the 2021–2025 factor-correlation literature had two further findings. First, Low-Vol-Quality pairs had exhibited stable 0.58-0.72 correlations through the cycle, constraining the diversification benefit of a tilt-and-pair. Second, Value-Momentum pairs had exhibited -0.15 to -0.28 correlations, rewarding an equal-weight commitment that owned both factors simultaneously.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Q1 2026 regime-classifier had printed late expansion at posterior 0.82. The allocator tool had suggested the Quality-Momentum-heavy tilt. Phases 1–2 had delivered +11% and Sharpe 1.14. On March 26, 2026, the classifier had printed a contraction-regime transition at posterior 0.67. Phase 4 had delivered a -4.8% composite return with Momentum and Size driving the drawdown.

### What Neubauer-Arrondo knew

The allocator tool's tilt was calibrated on a trailing-window; the equal-weight envelope had historically outperformed through regime switches; the factor-correlation matrix rewarded the equal-weight commitment; and the max-drawdown cap of -12% was reachable only if the Phase-5 decision defaulted to equal-weight.

### What she did not know

Whether the Phase-7 recovery would reward Value, Momentum and Size as the simulator's historical base-rate suggested; whether the Low-Vol tactical rotation would catch a defensive-factor premium that the equal-weight envelope would miss; and whether the Phase-10 Sharpe would land at the 1.04 target or compress to the 0.92–0.98 range under Phase-5-to-9 tactical drift.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Quant Research**: *"Equal-weight the stack. The correlation matrix rewards it. Every factor is cyclical — balance is the signal."*

**The Head of Execution**: *"Aggressive Low-Vol. We caught the defensive premium. It's the fast rotation or the slow drag."*

**The Chief Risk Officer**: *"Equal-weight keeps max drawdown inside eleven. The tactical rotation pushes it to fourteen. Retention lives below twelve."*

**The Managing Partner**: *"Factors are cyclical. Diversification across them is not."*

## Options Under Consideration

Neubauer-Arrondo had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Accept / Half-Tilt / Accept / Equal-Weight / Hold-EW / +9.8%-Sharpe-1.04:** the recommended envelope. Phase-10 annualised +9.8%, Sharpe 1.04, max drawdown -11%. Recommended.
- **Option B — Accept / Full-Tilt / Accept / Aggressive-Low-Vol / Tactical-Rotation / +6.6%-Sharpe-0.72:** the tactical envelope. Phase-10 annualised +6.6%, Sharpe 0.72, max drawdown -14%. Rejected — recovery-phase opportunity cost and drawdown-breach risk.
- **Option C — Accept / No-Tilt / Accept / Equal-Weight / Hold-EW / +8.8%-Sharpe-0.96:** the pure-passive envelope. Phase-10 annualised +8.8%, Sharpe 0.96, max drawdown -10%. Rejected — leaves early-phase Sharpe uplift on the table.

Her private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the phase most correlated with the factors-are-cyclical-diversification-is-not posture was Phase 5 — the allocation decision at the regime switch — because it was the phase that decided whether Kepler defaulted to equal-weight (Option A) or chased the tactical rotation (Option B) at the moment of maximum behavioural pressure.
