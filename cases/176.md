## Abstract

Solaris Software was, in the case universe, a Madrid-headquartered SaaS group with EUR 550M in 2025 annual recurring revenue across vertical-software lines for hospitality, retail and professional services, 3,800 employees in 12 countries with significant clusters in Madrid, Lisbon, Berlin, London and Mexico City, a 2025 Engagement reading of 62 on a 2023-rebased baseline of 68, a 2025 Innovation reading of 55 against a peer benchmark of 60 (composite captures patent filings, product-release velocity and post-launch outcome), an 18% 2025 voluntary Turnover against an industry benchmark of 14%, a 2025 Culture-Coherence reading of 48 against a peer benchmark of 60 (composite captures shared-language, ritual adherence and cross-team trust pulse), and a 2025 Cost-per-FTE of EUR 84K against an industry benchmark of EUR 78K, weighted by the company's lease-and-real-estate footprint. The Madrid head-office lease was due to expire in October 2027, an 18-month decision window that would commit a EUR 12M-per-annum real-estate envelope. The Q1 2026 board had approved a EUR 8M CultureHybrid envelope and named four events the March 2026 people-and-culture-committee dossier had to address: a January 2026 Engagement pulse confirming the 62 reading and surfacing a 28-point manager-versus-employee gap on the question "is hybrid working at Solaris?"; a February 2026 Innovation diagnostic showing patent filings down 34% year-over-year; a March 2026 voluntary turnover spike to 21% in three engineering pods; and a March 2026 manager survey in which 41% of managers reported the hybrid model "is failing" while 68% of employees reported it "is working". The Q1 2026 board had approved a Vice-President-of-People-led five-round commitment cycle against an Engagement floor above 70, an Innovation floor above 64, a Turnover ceiling below 14%, a Culture-Coherence floor above 64, a Cost-per-FTE ceiling below EUR 80K and a Composite CultureHybrid Score above 66. The case opens at 6:36 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the Solaris Software Madrid head office as **Lucía Fernández**, a 44-year-old Vice-President of People, prepares the Round-1 hybrid-policy commitment.

2. Analyze the Round-2 cultural-infrastructure investment (rituals-and-rhythms envelope that funds onboarding rituals, cross-timezone norms and feedback cadences vs. tooling-only envelope) and recommend the rituals envelope on the Culture-Coherence-and-Innovation basis.
3. Diagnose the Round-3 innovation-crisis response (deliberate-collision envelope that funds product-and-engineering co-working weeks and structured cross-pod design rituals vs. office-mandate envelope) and recommend the collision envelope on the patent-filings basis.
4. Respond to the Round-4 manager-perception-gap closure (manager-as-co-author envelope vs. policy-broadcast envelope) and recommend the co-author envelope on the manager-engagement basis.
5. Design the Round-5 sustainable-architecture embedding commitment that converts the hybrid model from a policy into a permanent operating-model with rituals, measurement and modelled leadership behaviour.

It is an operating model with rituals."* Sixty-four days separated her from the June 25 board people-and-culture review.

## The Protagonist

Lucía Fernández, 44, had assumed the Vice-President-of-People role on September 1, 2024, after twelve years across people, organisation design and operating-model leadership at three pan-European technology firms. She had, in 2022–2024, designed a 2,200-employee hybrid operating model that had documented a 12-point Engagement lift and a 7-point Culture-Coherence lift across an 18-month window. The Solaris Software Chief Executive Officer had recruited her in June 2024 specifically on two capabilities: her documented discipline of treating hybrid work as an operating-model-with-rituals problem rather than a policy-and-tooling problem, and her willingness to hold a manager-as-co-author envelope against pressure for a top-down mandate. Her 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the Engagement floor and 20% to the Culture-Coherence floor.

---

## The Decision

Fernández had to commit, by the May 4 board pre-review, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Hybrid-policy design.** Three archetypes: anchor-day-architecture (two team-aligned anchor days per week plus three remote days, codified team-autonomy guardrails on which two days, country-cluster norms layered on top, Composite 76), team-autonomy-only (no anchor days, full team autonomy — Composite 38, Culture-Coherence-decay probability), or phased (anchor on engineering and product only — Composite 54). Working envelope: anchor-day-architecture.
2. **Round 2 — Cultural-infrastructure investment.** Three archetypes: rituals-and-rhythms (fund onboarding rituals, cross-timezone norms, manager-feedback cadences and quarterly all-hands rituals at EUR 2.8M, Composite 76), tooling-only (collaboration-tool licensing without ritual scaffolding — Composite 38), or phased (rituals on three BUs only — Composite 56). Working envelope: rituals-and-rhythms.
3. **Round 3 — Innovation-crisis response.** Three vectors: deliberate-collision (fund quarterly product-and-engineering co-working weeks, structured cross-pod design rituals, paired-pod rotation, Composite 76), office-mandate (mandate three or four anchor days for engineering — Composite 36, Engagement-and-Turnover spike probability), or phased (collision on three pods only — Composite 56). Working envelope: deliberate-collision.
4. **Round 4 — Manager-perception-gap closure.** Three vectors: manager-as-co-author (codify managers as co-authors of the hybrid model with a published 200-manager council, manager-led ritual ownership, manager-experience scorecard, Composite 76), policy-broadcast (publish the policy with manager training only — Composite 36, manager-attrition probability), or phased (co-author on three regions — Composite 56). Working envelope: manager-as-co-author.
5. **Round 5 — Sustainable-architecture embedding.** Three vectors: sustainable-architecture (codify the hybrid model into the annual operating plan, integrate measurement into the quarterly business review, model leadership behaviour from the executive committee, refresh the lease-and-real-estate envelope to support the architecture, Composite 78), policy-only (annual policy without operating-model integration — Composite 42), or phased (architecture on three countries only — Composite 56). Working envelope: sustainable-architecture.

On Fernández's working model, an Anchor / Rituals / Collision / Co-Author / Architecture envelope delivered Engagement 72, Innovation 64, Turnover 13%, Culture Coherence 66, Cost per FTE EUR 79K, Composite 70.

## Company Background

Solaris Software had been founded in Madrid in 2009 and had, across seventeen years, built a EUR 550M-ARR vertical-software business with a 3,800-employee organisation across 12 countries. The 2020–2022 pandemic-era operating model had been a fully remote model and the 2022–2024 post-pandemic transition had defaulted to a team-autonomy-only hybrid policy without anchor days or codified rituals. By Q4 2025 the organisation had documented an 18% turnover, a 28-point manager-versus-employee gap on the hybrid question and a 34% year-over-year decline in patent filings.

The four events the March 2026 dossier had named. First, the January 2026 Engagement pulse had confirmed the 62 reading and surfaced the 28-point manager-versus-employee gap on the hybrid question. Second, the February 2026 Innovation diagnostic had documented the 34% year-over-year decline in patent filings, with the Chief Product Officer attributing the decline to insufficient cross-pod product-and-engineering collaboration. Third, the March 2026 voluntary turnover had spiked to 21% in three engineering pods. Fourth, the March 2026 manager survey had documented the 41%-versus-68% manager-versus-employee gap on the question "is hybrid working at Solaris?".

## Industry and Market Context

The 2022–2025 hybrid-work-design literature had, across 84 documented programmes, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, programmes that had codified anchor-day architecture had documented Culture-Coherence readings 16–22 points higher than team-autonomy-only programmes, with no measurable Engagement penalty and a documented Turnover lift of 4–6 points. Second, programmes that had funded rituals-and-rhythms infrastructure had documented Innovation readings 12–18 points higher than tooling-only programmes. Third, programmes that had executed deliberate-collision rituals — co-working weeks and cross-pod design rituals — had documented patent-filing recoveries of 22%–34% within 12 months against 0%–8% for office-mandate programmes, with the office-mandate programmes additionally documenting Engagement declines of 8–12 points.

Empirically, the post-2023 manager-experience literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, organisations that had positioned managers as co-authors of the hybrid model had documented manager-engagement readings 18–24 points higher than organisations that had positioned managers as policy-implementers. Second, sustainable-architecture embedding into the annual operating plan and quarterly business review had sustained Culture-Coherence readings above 64 in 72% of cases against 24% for policy-only embedding.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the February 2026 people-and-culture session, approved the EUR 8M envelope and the June 25 review calendar. The Head of People Analytics had, on March 6, delivered the Engagement-and-Turnover diagnostic. The Chief Product Officer had, on March 14, delivered the Innovation-decline diagnostic. The Head of Real Estate had, on March 21, delivered the lease-decision pack. The Chief Financial Officer had, on March 28, delivered the Cost-per-FTE pack.

### What Fernández knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. An Anchor / Rituals / Collision / Co-Author / Architecture Envelope projected Composite 70. A Team-Autonomy / Tooling / Mandate / Broadcast / Policy-Only Envelope projected Composite 28. A Hybrid Envelope projected Composite 50.

### What she did not know

Whether the engineering pods that had spiked to 21% turnover would recover within the 9-month window once the deliberate-collision protocol was deployed; whether the manager population would absorb the co-author designation as ownership rather than as workload; whether the lease-and-real-estate envelope refresh would clear the Cost-per-FTE ceiling; and whether the executive committee would model the anchor-day architecture before the June board.

### What others were saying

**The Head of People Analytics**: *"Anchor-days. The Culture-Coherence reading is the team-autonomy-only model telling us shared language is decaying."*

**The Chief Product Officer**: *"Deliberate-collision. Patents are not down because of remote — they are down because product and engineering have not been in a room together."*

**The Head of Real Estate**: *"The lease-refresh has to fit the architecture, not the policy."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Hybrid is not a policy. It is an operating model with rituals. Build the architecture. Pay the lease."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Fernández had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Anchor / Rituals / Collision / Co-Author / Architecture:** the recommended envelope. Engagement 72. Innovation 64. Turnover 13%. Culture Coherence 66. Cost per FTE EUR 79K. Composite 70. Recommended.
- **Option B — Team-Autonomy / Tooling / Mandate / Broadcast / Policy-Only:** the status-quo-extended envelope. Composite 28. Rejected — Engagement-and-Turnover spike probability above 65%.
- **Option C — Hybrid / Phased / Phased / Phased / Phased:** the partial envelope. Composite 50. Rejected — anchor-day architecture fragments under partial coverage.

Fernández's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 4 — the manager-as-co-author closure — because it was the round that converted the manager population from a policy-implementation friction layer into a co-authoring constituency.
