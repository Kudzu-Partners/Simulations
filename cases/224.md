## Abstract

Merrowgate Rates Desk was, in the case universe, the US-rates trading function of a New York-headquartered mid-to-large broker-dealer with a firm-wide balance sheet of USD 62B, daily Treasuries turnover of USD 8.4B, a matched-book repo exposure of USD 36B, 48 desk professionals, a 2025 net trading revenue of USD 310M and a 2025 desk VaR of USD 4.2M at a 99% one-day horizon. The 2024–2025 cycle had left the desk at a transmission-chain-aware but repo-capacity-squeezed posture: the Fed had held the funds target at 4.25–4.50% since Q4 2025, the 2Y-10Y curve had stood at -65 bps at the Q1 2026 close, and the SOFR-IOER basis had traded flat for eleven consecutive weeks before beginning to print wide in mid-April. The Q2 2026 context had compounded three pressures: an FOMC-hike probability that had lifted to 0.56 from 0.22 across two weeks; a month-end repo-demand pulse that had tightened GC collateral by 8 bps; and a growth-surprise index that had printed a three-month high that threatened a disinflation-pause narrative. The case opens at 6:28 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the Merrowgate Rates Desk New York trading floor as **Thomas Kallenberg-Ordoñez**, a 51-year-old Managing Director and Head of US Rates Trading, prepares the Phase-1 opening-snapshot commitment.

## The Protagonist

Kallenberg-Ordoñez, 51, had assumed the Managing-Director and Head-of-US-Rates-Trading role at Merrowgate on October 1, 2021, after twenty-four years across Treasuries, repo and rates-derivatives trading at two US primary dealers and one European bank. He had, in 2019–2021, led the repo-desk response to the September 2019 repo squeeze at a predecessor dealer by tapping the then-standing-repo-facility window within thirty-eight minutes of the first 20-bp through-IOER print, and had processed USD 18B of overnight facility usage across the first week. He had, in 2022, co-authored the desk run-book on facility usage with the understanding that "the facility exists exactly for this — using it isn't a stigma — refusing to use it is". The Merrowgate management committee had recruited him on three capabilities: his documented discipline of reading transmission-chain signals across the front-end, belly and long-end differentially rather than treating the curve as a single object; his willingness to tap standing facilities at the first clean signal of a funding squeeze; and his command of the curve-repositioning reflex that had, at the predecessor dealer, captured steepener PnL on three documented growth-surprise windows.

## The Decision

Kallenberg-Ordoñez had to commit, by the 8:00 a.m. desk-head session, the Phase-1 opening-snapshot envelope and the directional intent for Phases 2–12.

1. **Phase 1 — Opening-snapshot diagnosis.** A single envelope: accept the Fed Funds / 2Y / 10Y / Repo / Curve prints as the starting conditions. Working envelope: accept.
2. **Phase 3 — FOMC hike.** A single envelope: read the 25-bp hike into the transmission pattern (2Y +18, 10Y +6, Repo +24). Working envelope: accept.
3. **Phase 5 — Repo squeeze.** A single envelope: diagnose the 15-bp-through-IOER print as a balance-sheet-capacity breach rather than a transient demand spike. Working envelope: balance-sheet breach.
4. **Phase 6 — Funding decision.** Three archetypes. A tap-the-SRF envelope (access the facility at the posted rate; spread normalises in two days). A widen-the-bid envelope (pay up to clear the squeeze at the private market; preserve optics). A wait-for-month-end envelope (delay action; absorb incremental funding cost). Working envelope: tap-the-SRF.
5. **Phase 9 — Curve re-steepening.** A single envelope: reposition the duration book to absorb the +15 bps 2Y-10Y steepener on the growth-surprise print. Working envelope: reposition.
6. **Phase 12 — Outcome dashboard.** Target envelope: Curve +45 bps cumulative, policy-rate peak, credit spreads +30 bps, Phase Score 76.

On Kallenberg-Ordoñez's working model, an Accept / Accept / Breach / Tap-SRF / Reposition / +45-bp-Curve-and-Score-76 envelope delivered the Phase-12 dashboard above and a Composite Fed-sets-front-end-market-decides-rest rating.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2018–2025 US-rates-trading-desk literature had, across 42 documented twelve-phase trading cycles on USD 30B–USD 120B balance-sheet desks, produced three findings the trading had to respect. First, transmission-chain-literate reading of FOMC hikes that explicitly decomposed 2Y response from 10Y response had, on 25-bp-hike windows, delivered desk-PnL premiums of 14–22% in 66% of documented cases, against treat-the-curve-as-one alternatives. Second, tap-the-standing-facility envelopes at the first 15-bp-through-IOER print had normalised spreads inside two days and preserved matched-book PnL in 78% of documented cases, against widen-the-bid alternatives that had compressed the matched-book margin by 8–14 bps. Third, curve-repositioning envelopes that had recognised growth-surprise-driven re-steepening inside the first two sessions had captured 60–90% of the eventual steepener move in 62% of documented cases.

Empirically, the 2020–2025 standing-facility-stigma literature had two further findings. First, facility-usage did not correlate with subsequent private-market funding cost in 83% of windows, invalidating the stigma hypothesis on contemporaneous data. Second, dealers who used the facility in the first squeeze window outperformed non-users by 22 bps of trailing-annualised matched-book margin on average across the following quarter.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Q4 2025 FOMC statement had held the funds target at 4.25–4.50%. The Q1 2026 dot-plot had shown a median path to one additional hike in 2026. Month-end March 2026 had tightened GC by 8 bps, and mid-April had printed a 15-bp-through-IOER squeeze. The April 20 growth-surprise index had printed a three-month high. The FOMC meeting had ended on April 21 with a 25-bp hike and hawkish guidance.

### What Kallenberg-Ordoñez knew

The transmission pattern read (2Y +18, 10Y +6, Repo +24); the matched-book capacity was at 96% of the internal limit; the SRF was posted at IOER; the growth-surprise print was inconsistent with a sustained disinflation path; and the curve was inverted at -65 bps going into the April 21 meeting.

### What he did not know

Whether the repo-squeeze would extend past the April month-end or clear into early May; whether the 10Y-response to additional hikes would remain at the 6-bp-per-25-bp ratio or accelerate as the disinflation-pause narrative took hold; whether the steepener would clear +60 bps by end-Q2 or reverse on a renewed disinflation print; and whether the facility-usage would trigger board-level optics discussion despite the data on the stigma hypothesis.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Repo**: *"Tap the facility. Fifteen through IOER is a balance-sheet breach. The facility exists exactly for this."*

**The Head of Treasuries**: *"Reposition for the steepener. Two minus ten will print plus-fifteen by Friday."*

**The Chief Risk Officer**: *"Stay inside VaR. The repo tap frees capacity for the duration reposition."*

**The Desk Chief Executive**: *"The Fed sets the front end. The market decides the rest."*

## Options Under Consideration

Kallenberg-Ordoñez had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Accept / Accept / Breach / Tap-SRF / Reposition / Score-76:** the recommended envelope. Phase-12 curve +45 bps, credit +30, Score 76. Recommended.
- **Option B — Accept / Accept / Breach / Widen-Bid / No-Reposition / Score-58:** the optics-first envelope. Phase-12 curve +45 bps, Score 58, matched-book margin -12 bps. Rejected — facility-stigma myth and missed steepener.
- **Option C — Accept / Accept / Breach / Tap-SRF / No-Reposition / Score-66:** the funding-only envelope. Phase-12 Score 66, steepener-PnL left on the table. Rejected — half-completed trade.

His private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the phase most correlated with the Fed-sets-front-end-market-decides-rest posture was Phase 6 — the funding decision — because it was the phase that decided whether Merrowgate tapped the facility and preserved matched-book capacity for the Phase-9 reposition (Option A) or defended optics at the cost of the trade (Options B and C).
