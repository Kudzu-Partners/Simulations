## Abstract

Andina Industrial S.A. was, in the case universe, a Lima-headquartered LATAM mid-cap industrial operator with USD 380M in 2025 revenue, EUR 62M in 2025 EBITDA, 2,260 employees across Peru, Chile and Colombia, three operating divisions (industrial-machinery, components-manufacturing and after-market-services), a 2025 Market Capitalisation of USD 220M, a 2025 Book Equity of USD 95M, a 2025 Senior-Notes balance of USD 60M (YTM 7.8%), a 2025 Revolver balance of USD 27M (drawing-cost 7.3%), a 2025 effective-tax rate of 35%, a Beta of 1.15, a 2026 risk-free rate of 5.1% and an Equity Risk Premium of 7.4%. The Q1 2026 board had approved a four-round CFO Command capital-allocation cycle — the corporate-finance-and-capital-allocation programme — and had positioned it as the CFO function's response to three overlapping pressures: a Q1 2026 standing capital-allocation pipeline of five named investment opportunities with an aggregate request of USD 64M against an available envelope of USD 40M, a Q4 2025 capital-structure diagnostic that had priced the Andina balance sheet at 2.4× Debt-to-EBITDA against a peer-median of 3.2× and a tax-shield-capture gap of USD 14M annually, and a 2025 credit-rating-agency review that had reaffirmed the BBB- rating one notch above junk with a stable outlook contingent on Debt-to-EBITDA inside 3.6× through 2027. The case opens at 6:24 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the Andina Industrial Lima head office as **Esteban Cifuentes-Vásquez**, a 50-year-old Chief Financial Officer, prepares the Round-1 WACC-calibration commitment.

The rating is the chain."*

## The Protagonist

Cifuentes-Vásquez, 50, had assumed the Chief-Financial-Officer role at Andina Industrial S.A. on March 1, 2024, after twenty years across treasury, capital-markets and CFO leadership at a Bogotá-based industrial-services group and a Buenos Aires-based consumer-goods operator. He had, in 2020–2023, led a USD 240M four-year capital-allocation cycle at the Bogotá group that had sustained a BBB- credit rating across two refinancing windows, had captured USD 24M in cumulative tax-shield through three named senior-notes issuances and had cleared a documented post-issuance WACC reduction of 110 bps. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited him in January 2024 specifically on two capabilities: his documented discipline of defaulting to Market-Weighted-and-YTM WACC calibrations rather than Book-Weighted-and-Coupon shortcuts that had priced two peer mid-cap operators into upper-band project-acceptance overruns, and his willingness to default to capital-rationing-with-optionality envelopes rather than full-deploy postures that had consumed Q3 contingent-opportunity capacity in adjacent operators. His 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the Composite CFO Command Score, 25% to the rating-defence trajectory and 20% to the tax-shield-capture composite.

## The Decision

Cifuentes-Vásquez had to commit, by the 8:30 a.m. board-finance-committee pre-brief, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–4.

1. **Round 1 — WACC calibration.** Three archetypes: Market-Weighted-and-YTM 11.6% envelope (Composite 78, project-acceptance disciplined, rating-defence aligned), Book-Weighted-and-Coupon 9.0% shortcut (Composite 22, upper-band project-acceptance, rating exposure), or Market-Weighted-and-Coupon 10.4% partial (Composite 52, partial). Working envelope: Market-and-YTM at 11.6%.
2. **Round 2 — Capital allocation.** Three vectors: USD 36M to Projects 1, 2 and 5 with USD 28M aggregate NPV (Composite 76, NPV-per-dollar above peer ratio), USD 40M to all five projects with USD 22M aggregate NPV (Composite 38, NPV-per-dollar below peer ratio — Project 3 IRR-attractive at 14% but NPV-negative under risk-adjusted WACC), or USD 24M to Projects 1 and 2 only with USD 18M aggregate NPV (Composite 58, partial). Working envelope: 36M to 1-2-5.
3. **Round 3 — Capital structure.** Three vectors: USD 20M senior-notes issuance pushing Debt-to-EBITDA to 3.6× with BBB- to BB+ rating drop and 80-bps WACC reduction (Composite 78, USD 14M tax-shield captured, USD 0.3M incremental spread cost), equity-only envelope (Composite 42, no tax-shield-capture, equity-issuance-cost), or USD 30M senior-notes-aggressive (Composite 28, BB rating drop, covenant exposure). Working envelope: USD 20M senior-notes.
4. **Round 4 — Optionality preservation.** Two vectors: USD 4M dry-powder envelope (Composite 76, Q3 contingent-opportunity capacity preserved) or full-deploy (Composite 52, no optionality). Working envelope: USD 4M dry-powder.

On Cifuentes-Vásquez's working model, a Market-YTM-WACC / 36M-to-1-2-5 / 20M-Senior-Notes / 4M-Dry-Powder envelope delivered Post-Issuance WACC 10.8%, Aggregate NPV USD 28M, Credit Rating BB+, Tax-Shield USD 14M, Composite 82.

## Company Background

Andina Industrial S.A. had been founded in Lima in 1988 and had, across thirty-eight years, built a USD 380M-revenue LATAM mid-cap industrial operator organised into three operating divisions: an industrial-machinery division (USD 180M revenue, 22 named industrial-OEM customers), a components-manufacturing division (USD 120M revenue, three plant footprints) and an after-market-services division (USD 80M revenue, recurring-revenue ratio of 88%). The 2023–2025 cycle had left the operator at a defended-cost-leadership posture in machinery, an under-leveraged capital structure (Debt-to-EBITDA at 2.4× against a peer-median of 3.2×) and a 2025 effective-tax-rate of 35% against a tax-shield-capture gap of USD 14M annually. The Q4 2025 capital-allocation diagnostic had priced five named investment opportunities against the USD 40M envelope.

Three Q1 2026 inputs were named in the April 2026 CFO Command workbook. First, the Q1 2026 capital-allocation pipeline: Project 1 (machinery line-extension, USD 14M, NPV USD 12M, IRR 18%), Project 2 (components-plant capacity expansion, USD 12M, NPV USD 9M, IRR 17%), Project 3 (after-market-services geography entry, USD 14M, NPV –USD 1.5M at calibrated WACC despite IRR 14%, owing to country-risk adjustment), Project 4 (machinery-platform R&D, USD 14M, NPV USD 4M, IRR 13.5%) and Project 5 (after-market-services digital platform, USD 10M, NPV USD 7M, IRR 16%). Second, the Q1 2026 rating-agency dialogue: BBB- maintained with stable outlook contingent on Debt-to-EBITDA inside 3.6× and Interest-Coverage above 4.0× through 2027. Third, the Q1 2026 senior-notes-market window: the USD 20M issuance was pricing inside a 30-bps incremental spread relative to the existing notes.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2019–2025 LATAM mid-cap CFO literature had, across 36 documented capital-allocation cycles on operators with USD 200M–USD 500M revenue and BBB-or-BB-rated credit, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, Market-Weighted-and-YTM WACC calibrations had produced WACC readings 180–280 bps above Book-Weighted-and-Coupon shortcuts in 74% of documented cycles, and had preceded subsequent project-acceptance disciplines in 68% of cases. Second, capital-rationing envelopes that had deployed 80–95% of the available envelope and had ranked projects strictly by NPV-per-dollar at the calibrated WACC had captured 28–42% more aggregate NPV in 64% of documented cases, against full-deploy IRR-only postures. Third, USD 20M–USD 40M senior-notes issuances at Debt-to-EBITDA approaching 3.6× had captured tax-shield USD 8M–USD 18M annually in 72% of documented cases, and had cleared rating-defence at the BB+ floor with a path back to BBB- inside twenty-four months in 64% of cases.

Empirically, the post-2022 corporate-finance literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, optionality-preservation envelopes at 8–12% of the available capital had captured Q3 contingent-opportunity NPV of USD 3M–USD 6M in 58% of documented cycles. Second, equity-only capital-structure postures at sub-2.5× Debt-to-EBITDA had under-captured tax-shield by USD 8M–USD 14M annually in 68% of documented LATAM mid-cap cycles.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the December 2025 session, approved the four-round CFO Command cycle. The Treasurer had, on March 8, delivered the senior-notes-market window dialogue. The Head of FP&A had, on March 15, delivered the project-by-project NPV-at-calibrated-WACC workbook. The Director of Investor Relations had, on March 22, delivered the rating-agency dialogue summary. The Head of Tax had, on March 29, delivered the tax-shield-capture model.

### What Cifuentes-Vásquez knew

The team had modelled three four-round envelopes. A Market-YTM-WACC / 36M-to-1-2-5 / 20M-Senior-Notes / 4M-Dry-Powder envelope projected Composite 82. A Book-Coupon-WACC / 40M-to-all-five / Equity-Only / Full-Deploy envelope projected Composite 14, with named project-acceptance overrun and rating-defence drift. A Market-YTM-WACC / 24M-to-1-2-only / Equity-Only / Full-Deploy envelope projected Composite 56.

### What he did not know

Whether the Q2 2026 senior-notes-market window would hold the 30-bps incremental-spread anchor through the issuance close; whether the rating-agency dialogue would absorb the BB+ rating-drop with a clear path back to BBB- inside the twenty-four-month window; whether the Q3 2026 contingent-opportunity pipeline would surface the named opportunity that justified the USD 4M dry-powder envelope; and whether Project 3 (the country-risk-adjusted-NPV-negative project) would resurface in the Q4 2026 cycle with an updated country-risk-premium.

### What others were saying

**The Treasurer**: *"Twenty million in senior notes. The window is open. Fourteen million in tax-shield against three hundred k of incremental spread. The arithmetic is settled."*

**The Head of FP&A**: *"Projects one, two and five. Twenty-eight million NPV. Project three is IRR-attractive but NPV-negative once you add the country-risk premium."*

**The Director of Investor Relations**: *"BB-plus is the floor. The path back to BBB-minus inside twenty-four months is the narrative."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"WACC is the hurdle. The rating is the chain."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Cifuentes-Vásquez had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Market-YTM-WACC / 36M-to-1-2-5 / 20M-Senior-Notes / 4M-Dry-Powder:** the recommended envelope. Post-Issuance WACC 10.8%. Aggregate NPV USD 28M. Credit Rating BB+. Tax-Shield USD 14M. Composite 82. Recommended.
- **Option B — Book-Coupon-WACC / 40M-to-all-five / Equity-Only / Full-Deploy:** the shortcut envelope. Composite 14. Rejected — project-acceptance overrun and tax-shield-capture gap.
- **Option C — Market-YTM-WACC / 24M-to-1-2-only / Equity-Only / Full-Deploy:** the conservative envelope. Composite 56. Rejected — tax-shield-capture gap and aggregate NPV below peer ratio.

Cifuentes-Vásquez's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 1 — the WACC calibration — because it was the round that decided whether Project 3's 14%-IRR-attractive proposal cleared the hurdle (it did under the 9.0% Book-Coupon shortcut and ate USD 1.5M of NPV) or fell below it (under the 11.6% Market-YTM anchor) and freed the USD 14M for the higher-NPV-per-dollar Projects 1 and 5.
