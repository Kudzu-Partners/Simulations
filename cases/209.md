## Abstract

Andina Consumer Products was, in the case universe, a LatAm consumer-packaged-goods operator headquartered in Santiago with USD 420M in 2025 revenue, 1,640 employees across Chile, Peru and Colombia, forty-two named SKUs spanning two brand portfolios (premium-branded and private-label-manufactured-for-retailer), three manufacturing plants (two Santiago, one Lima), and a 2025 aggregate overhead pool of USD 6.7M that had been, across the 2022–2025 cycle, allocated to SKU cost on a peanut-butter volume-weighted basis. The Q4 2025 controllership diagnostic had priced the volume-weighted allocation as the single largest contributor to a 2025 portfolio-margin-truth-gap: the premium-SKU-line had been reported at +15% gross margin but priced at –4% under an activity-based rebuild; the private-label-line had been reported at +3% but priced at +18%. The Q4 2025 board had approved a four-round four-month CostLab rebuild — the management-accounting-and-margin-decisions programme — and had positioned it as the finance function's response to three overlapping pressures: a Q1 2026 retailer-X price-negotiation window on the premium-SKU-line that had opened against a standard-cost-based defence floor the retailer's procurement team had priced below the true ABC floor, a 2025 cumulative USD 1.2M unfavourable-aggregate-variance reading that the December 2025 controllership memorandum had not decomposed, and a 2026 SG&A-envelope challenge from the CFO to cut USD 1.8M without touching the commercial organisation. The case opens at 6:36 a.m. on Tuesday, April 21, 2026, at the Andina Santiago head office as **Roxana Peñarroja-Brizuela**, a 44-year-old Finance Director, prepares the Round-1 ABC-rebuild commitment.

## The Protagonist

Peñarroja-Brizuela, 44, had assumed the Finance-Director role at Andina Consumer Products on July 1, 2024, after fifteen years across management accounting, activity-based-costing-rebuild and variance-analysis leadership at a São Paulo-based consumer-packaged-goods group and a Lima-based accounting-advisory firm. She had, in 2020–2023, led an ABC-rebuild at the São Paulo group that had moved forty-seven SKU-margin readings across a 28-point range and had preceded a 420-bps enterprise gross-margin lift inside eighteen months. The Chief Financial Officer had recruited her in May 2024 specifically on two capabilities: her documented discipline of defaulting to activity-weighted driver allocations rather than volume-weighted peanut-butter shortcuts that had priced two peer consumer-goods operators into retailer-negotiation-floor collapses, and her willingness to default to Volume-Rate-Mix variance decompositions rather than aggregate-variance postures that had consumed remediation capital in adjacent operators. Her 2026 variable compensation tied 35% to the annual gross-margin trajectory, 25% to the SG&A zero-based-envelope target and 20% to the retailer-negotiation-floor composite.

## The Decision

Peñarroja-Brizuela had to commit, by the 8:15 a.m. finance-committee pre-brief, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–4.

1. **Round 1 — ABC driver reweighting.** Two archetypes: activity-weighted envelope using machine-hours / setups / quality-checks / shipments (Composite 78, premium-SKU-line flips from +15% to –4%, private-label flips from +3% to +18%) or volume-weighted peanut-butter shortcut (Composite 22, margin-truth obscured). Working envelope: activity-weighted.
2. **Round 2 — Retailer-X price-negotiation defence.** Two vectors: ABC-costed-floor envelope at the true –4%-to-0% margin anchor (Composite 76, retailer concedes the 4%-point gap) or standard-cost-based floor at the 15% illusion (Composite 22, retailer extracts 8% price giveback). Working envelope: ABC-costed-floor.
3. **Round 3 — USD 1.2M unfavourable-variance decomposition.** Two vectors: Volume-Rate-Mix decomposition (Volume +84k favourable, Rate –900k unfavourable, Mix –480k unfavourable — Composite 76) or aggregate-variance hold (Composite 34, remediation untargeted). Working envelope: decomposition.
4. **Round 4 — Zero-based-budgeting SG&A commitment.** Two vectors: USD 1.8M ZBB envelope without touching commercial (Composite 78) or uniform 6% cut across SG&A (Composite 42, commercial-organisation collateral). Working envelope: ZBB-without-commercial.

On Peñarroja-Brizuela's working model, an Activity-Weighted / ABC-Floor / VRM-Decomposition / ZBB envelope delivered Total Score 78, Composite 76.

## Company Background

Andina Consumer Products had been founded in Santiago in 1992 and had, across thirty-four years, built a USD 420M-revenue LatAm consumer-packaged-goods operator organised into two brand portfolios (a premium-branded portfolio with 22 SKUs and a private-label-manufactured-for-retailer portfolio with 20 SKUs) across three manufacturing plants (two Santiago, one Lima). The 2023–2025 cycle had left the group at a defended mid-tier positioning against two regional multinational CPG peers, and the Q4 2025 controllership diagnostic had priced the peanut-butter overhead allocation as the single largest contributor to the portfolio-margin-truth gap.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the March 2026 CostLab-scoping document. First, the Q1 2026 retailer-X price-negotiation window had opened on the premium-SKU-line — retailer-X had requested a 5% price reduction on the argument that the SKU-line carried 15% gross margin. Second, the 2025 year-end controllership close had reported an aggregate USD 1.2M unfavourable variance that the December memorandum had not decomposed. Third, the February 2026 CFO SG&A-envelope challenge had set a USD 1.8M SG&A cut target against the 2026 envelope without touching the commercial organisation.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2019–2025 CPG management-accounting literature had, across 42 documented ABC-rebuild cycles on operators above USD 200M revenue, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, activity-weighted ABC allocations against at least four named drivers (machine-hours, setups, quality-checks, shipments) had surfaced SKU-margin readings 12–26 points different from volume-weighted peanut-butter shortcuts in 68% of documented cases, and had inverted at least one named SKU-line margin sign in 54% of cases. Second, retailer-negotiation windows defended at the ABC floor had preserved 58–74% of the retailer-requested price-giveback in 64% of documented cases, against 22% for standard-cost-based defences. Third, Volume-Rate-Mix variance decompositions had targeted remediation at the named rate-variance driver in 78% of documented cases, and had preceded 60–80% rate-variance recovery inside twelve months in 54% of cases.

Empirically, the post-2022 CFO-office literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, zero-based-budgeting SG&A rebuilds at 10–14% of the SG&A envelope had been sustainable through subsequent-year cycles in 66% of documented cases when commercial-organisation capacity had been ring-fenced. Second, uniform-percentage SG&A cuts across the envelope had priced subsequent-year commercial-capacity gaps in 72% of documented cases.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the December 2025 session, approved the four-round CostLab cycle. The Controller had, on March 7, delivered the 2025 year-end variance-attribution brief. The Cost-Analytics lead had, on March 14, delivered the ABC driver-candidate audit. The Commercial-Director had, on March 21, delivered the retailer-X negotiation timeline. The Chief Human Resources Officer had, on March 28, delivered the SG&A-envelope organisation map.

### What Peñarroja-Brizuela knew

The team had modelled three four-round envelopes. An Activity-Weighted / ABC-Floor / VRM-Decomposition / ZBB envelope projected Composite 76. A Volume-Weighted / Standard-Floor / Aggregate-Variance / Uniform-Cut envelope projected Composite 18. An Activity-Weighted / Standard-Floor / VRM-Decomposition / Uniform-Cut envelope projected Composite 46.

### What she did not know

Whether the retailer-X procurement team would accept the ABC-based floor at the negotiation table or escalate through the executive-sponsor channel; whether the Rate-variance root-cause diagnostic would surface as raw-material-cost or overhead-absorption drift; whether the Q2 2026 manufacturing-plant mix would shift following the ABC-driven SKU-prioritisation; and whether the ZBB SG&A rebuild would hold inside the CHRO-approved ring-fence through the Q3 2026 organisation cycle.

### What others were saying

**The Controller**: *"Activity-weighted. Four drivers. The peanut-butter allocation has been lying to the P&L for three years."*

**The Commercial-Director**: *"Defend the premium-SKU-line at minus four, not plus fifteen. Retailer X has been winning this negotiation because we were giving them our cost as a gift."*

**The Cost-Analytics Lead**: *"Decompose the one-point-two. Rate is nine-hundred-k unfavourable. That's where remediation lives."*

**The Chief Financial Officer**: *"Standard costing averages. ABC accuses."*

## Options Under Consideration

Peñarroja-Brizuela had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Activity-Weighted / ABC-Floor / VRM-Decomposition / ZBB:** the recommended envelope. Total Score 78. Composite 76. Recommended.
- **Option B — Volume-Weighted / Standard-Floor / Aggregate-Variance / Uniform-Cut:** the legacy envelope. Composite 18. Rejected — retailer-negotiation-floor collapse probability above 70%.
- **Option C — Activity-Weighted / Standard-Floor / VRM-Decomposition / Uniform-Cut:** the partial envelope. Composite 46. Rejected — retailer-floor still compromised.

Peñarroja-Brizuela's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 1 — the ABC driver reweighting — because it was the round that surfaced the premium-SKU and private-label margin-sign inversions that made the subsequent rounds' retailer-defence and variance-decomposition and ZBB commitments actionable rather than rhetorical.
