## Abstract

Fenmarrow Capital London was, in the case universe, the London FX derivatives arm of a European mid-market sell-side bank operating a EUR/USD options market-making book with EUR 3M in pre-quarter cash buffer, a 2025 Vega-limit ceiling of EUR 250k, a 2025 VaR-limit ceiling of EUR 400k, a 2025 Gamma-limit range of ±EUR 120k per 1% spot move, and a quarterly P&L-target of +EUR 300k anchored on market-making flow and controlled directional exposure. The Q2 2026 trading quarter opened on Tuesday, April 21, 2026, with EUR/USD spot at 1.0800, implied vol at 12%, the book flat on Delta, Gamma and Theta, the ECB April-24 policy meeting seven trading days out, and a 2026 risk-committee scorecard that had priced a quarterly composite above 70 against a peer-desk cohort as the baseline-retention threshold. The Q1 2026 review had priced three observations the desk had to carry into Q2. First, the 2025 full-year P&L on the EUR/USD book had been +EUR 410k against a +EUR 1.2M peer median, traced to excessive unhedged short-vega carry through three ECB surprise windows. Second, the 2025 maximum-VaR-breach frequency had been four days against a zero-breach mandate. Third, the Q4 2025 post-mortem had documented an 82-minute average time-to-collar after the November 2025 Fed surprise — double the desk's documented 40-minute target. The case opens at 5:54 a.m. on Tuesday, April 21, 2026, at the Fenmarrow Capital Canary Wharf floor as **Naomi Ashworth-Carrington**, a 38-year-old Head of FX Options, prepares the Round-1 spread-and-notional commitment.

## The Protagonist

Ashworth-Carrington, 38, had assumed the Head-of-FX-Options role at Fenmarrow Capital London on July 1, 2024, after fourteen years across FX options, exotic derivatives and vega-book management at a global investment bank in London and a Swiss private bank in Zurich. She had, in 2021–2024, run the EUR-book at a tier-one London desk that had cleared three ECB surprise windows without a VaR breach and had sustained a Vega discipline below EUR 180k across twenty-eight trading months. The Chief Market Officer had recruited her in May 2024 specifically on two capabilities: her documented discipline of defaulting to collar hedges rather than delta-only hedges through surprise windows that had priced two peer desks into short-vega drawdowns, and her willingness to default to selective-roll theta-containment rather than hold-and-bleed postures that had consumed excessive P&L in adjacent desks. Her 2026 variable compensation tied 35% to the quarterly P&L, 25% to the VaR-breach count and 20% to the Vega-and-Gamma-discipline composite.

## The Decision

Ashworth-Carrington had to commit, by the 7:15 a.m. pre-market call, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Spread-and-notional sizing.** Three archetypes: 3-bps spread with EUR 5M notional and no-hedge (Composite 58, market-making flow captured, short-vega exposure priced), 5-bps spread with EUR 3M notional and delta-hedge (Composite 66, reduced flow, Vega controlled), or 8-bps spread with EUR 2M notional and full-collar (Composite 52, flow compressed). Working envelope: 3-bps with EUR 5M.
2. **Round 2 — Hedging pivot after ECB surprise.** Three vectors: collar-hedge (long 1.0850 call, short 1.0600 put — gamma positive, theta EUR 12k/day, Composite 78), double-down (add EUR 5M short-vega — Composite 20, VaR breach probability above 80%), or delta-only (Composite 48, vega unhedged). Working envelope: collar-hedge.
3. **Round 3 — Vega-and-gamma reconstitution.** Two vectors: long-gamma reconstitution (pay theta, keep convexity through the post-ECB regime — Composite 76), or short-gamma (collect theta, short convexity — Composite 32). Working envelope: long-gamma.
4. **Round 4 — Theta-bleed containment.** Two vectors: selective-roll (roll the short-dated collar legs selectively, contain theta at EUR 8k/day — Composite 76), or hold-and-bleed (hold the collar to expiry — Composite 42, P&L giveback above EUR 200k). Working envelope: selective-roll.
5. **Round 5 — Quarter-end limit-discipline.** Two vectors: disciplined-close (Vega EUR 150k, VaR EUR 300k, Gamma ±EUR 80k — Composite 78), or push-for-carry (Vega EUR 250k, VaR EUR 400k — Composite 48, risk-committee exposure). Working envelope: disciplined-close.

On Ashworth-Carrington's working model, a 3-bps-5M / Collar / Long-Gamma / Selective-Roll / Disciplined-Close envelope delivered P&L +EUR 410k, VaR EUR 280k, Vega EUR 140k, Composite 74.

## Company Background

Fenmarrow Capital London had been established as the London FX-derivatives arm of a European mid-market sell-side bank in 2008 and had, across eighteen years, built a EUR/USD market-making book at USD 18B in 2025 notional flow with twenty-two market-making-and-volatility traders, four quant-risk analysts, three prime-brokerage-coverage sales and a documented risk-limit architecture: Vega ceiling EUR 250k, VaR ceiling EUR 400k, Gamma range ±EUR 120k. The 2023–2025 cycle had left the EUR/USD book at +EUR 620k cumulative P&L against a +EUR 2.1M peer-cohort median, traced in the Q4 2025 post-mortem to excessive unhedged short-vega carry through three ECB surprise windows and a documented 82-minute average time-to-collar after the November 2025 Fed surprise.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2019–2025 FX-derivatives literature had, across 48 documented EUR/USD options-desk quarterly cycles above USD 12B in notional flow, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, 3-bps-spread no-hedge envelopes had captured flow but had consumed EUR 180k–EUR 320k in short-vega P&L through documented ECB surprise windows in 68% of cases, against EUR 40k–EUR 80k for 5-bps delta-hedge envelopes. Second, collar-hedge pivots inside a 40-minute window after a surprise volatility event had preserved quarterly P&L in 74% of documented cycles — delays above 90 minutes had consumed 42–58% of the prior quarter's P&L in 62% of cases. Third, long-gamma reconstitutions after a surprise window had out-earned short-gamma reconstitutions by EUR 180k per EUR 10M of notional in 66% of documented regimes.

Empirically, the post-2022 sell-side-trading literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, VaR-breach events above two days per quarter had triggered risk-committee interventions in 72% of documented desks — zero-breach quarters had been the baseline-retention threshold in 88%. Second, quarter-end Vega readings above EUR 200k had priced next-quarter limit reductions in 64% of documented cycles.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The risk committee had, in the January 2026 session, reaffirmed the Vega, VaR and Gamma limits and the zero-breach mandate. The Head of FX Trading had, on April 7, delivered the Q2 2026 market-making plan. The Head of Market Risk had, on April 14, delivered the ECB-surprise scenario matrix. The Head of Volatility Trading had, on April 17, delivered the skew-and-term-structure note.

### What Ashworth-Carrington knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. A 3-bps-5M / Collar / Long-Gamma / Selective-Roll / Disciplined-Close envelope projected Composite 74. A 3-bps-5M / Double-Down / Short-Gamma / Hold-and-Bleed / Push-for-Carry envelope projected Composite 12 with VaR-breach probability above 70%. A 5-bps-3M / Delta-Only / Short-Gamma / Hold-and-Bleed / Disciplined-Close envelope projected Composite 42.

### What she did not know

Whether the ECB would cut twenty-five or fifty basis points on April 24; whether the EUR/USD skew would steepen past the –2.5 risk-reversal threshold inside the April-24 window; whether the twenty-two-trader seat would hold its new VaR-attribution discipline through the surprise window; and whether the prime-brokerage sales channel would book the EUR 5M notional flow the pricing envelope required.

### What others were saying

**The Head of FX Trading**: *"Three basis points. Five million. If we price at five, half the flow goes to the competitor desk before lunch."*

**The Head of Market Risk**: *"Collar on the morning of April 24. If we hold short-vega into the announcement, we're looking at a four-hundred-k drawdown on the left tail."*

**The Head of Volatility Trading**: *"Long gamma post-ECB. The regime after a surprise is not the regime before."*

**The Chief Market Officer**: *"The hedge IS the position."*

## Options Under Consideration

Ashworth-Carrington had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — 3-bps-5M / Collar / Long-Gamma / Selective-Roll / Disciplined-Close:** the recommended envelope. P&L +EUR 410k. VaR EUR 280k. Vega EUR 140k. Composite 74. Recommended.
- **Option B — 3-bps-5M / Double-Down / Short-Gamma / Hold-and-Bleed / Push-for-Carry:** the carry envelope. Composite 12. Rejected — VaR-breach probability above 70%.
- **Option C — 5-bps-3M / Delta-Only / Short-Gamma / Hold-and-Bleed / Disciplined-Close:** the conservative envelope. Composite 42. Rejected — flow capture below the retention threshold.

Ashworth-Carrington's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 2 — the post-ECB hedging pivot — because it was the round that decided whether the 40-minute time-to-collar discipline held or whether the desk added to a losing short-vega position inside a regime it could no longer predict.
