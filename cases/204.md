## Abstract

NovaTech was, in the case universe, a European enterprise-software group headquartered in Copenhagen with EUR 3.8B in 2025 revenue that had, on February 14, 2026, closed the EUR 2.5B acquisition of DataPulse — a data-integration specialist of 1,840 employees headquartered in Amsterdam with EUR 620M in 2025 revenue, a 2025 EBITDA margin of 22%, a Q4 2025 Rule-of-40 reading of 52 and a named-platform customer book of 1,420 enterprise accounts. The February 14 deal-close announcement had priced EUR 300M in three-year run-rate cost synergies, EUR 120M in run-rate revenue synergies and a twelve-month integration window against a Q1 2027 investor-day deadline. The Q1 2026 board had approved a five-round Integration-Management-Office charter — the MergeWorks programme — and had positioned it as the group's response to three overlapping pressures: a 2024–2025 market-cap compression against two enterprise-software peers that had closed a 14-point revenue-growth lead, a 2026 CEO commitment to investors that the Q1 2027 investor day would show EUR 180M of in-flight cost-synergy capture, and a Q1 2026 DataPulse talent diagnostic that had priced a 22% first-year named-employee attrition risk against an integration-sector baseline of 14%. The case opens at 6:12 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the NovaTech Copenhagen head office as **Kasper Bendtsen-Holm**, a 46-year-old Head of the Integration Management Office, prepares the Round-1 integration-intensity commitment. Two hundred and fifty-four days separated him from the Q1 2027 investor day.

## The Protagonist

Bendtsen-Holm, 46, had assumed the Head-of-the-IMO role at NovaTech on February 1, 2026, after seventeen years across post-merger integration leadership at a London-based software-and-services group, a Boston enterprise-software group and a Copenhagen-based management consultancy. He had, in 2020–2023, led the IMO of a EUR 1.8B London software acquisition that had captured EUR 210M of EUR 240M committed cost synergies inside eleven months and had sustained Talent Retention above 84% across the first twelve months. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited him in December 2025 specifically on two capabilities: his documented discipline of defaulting to best-of-both integration envelopes rather than aggressive-cost-out postures that had priced two peer integrations into named-talent walkouts, and his willingness to default to targeted-customer rebuild spend rather than internal-comms-only postures that had consumed customer-retention reserves in adjacent deals. His 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the twelve-month Synergy-Capture trajectory, 25% to the Talent-Retention floor and 20% to the Customer-Retention floor.

## The Decision

Bendtsen-Holm had to commit, by the May 5 IMO-steering-committee pre-brief, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Integration-intensity calibration.** Three archetypes: Intensity-8 aggressive-cost-out at EUR 35M (Composite 42, Synergies +EUR 90M, Talent –12%), Intensity-5 best-of-both at EUR 20M (Composite 72, Synergies +EUR 55M, Talent –4%), or Intensity-3 slow-and-preserve at EUR 12M (Composite 58, Synergies +EUR 28M). Working envelope: Intensity-5 best-of-both.
2. **Round 2 — CTO-resignation pivot.** Three vectors: EUR 15M retention with intensity-4 slowdown (Composite 74, Morale +12, Talent arrested), press-on at intensity-8 (Composite 18, Talent collapse below 80%), or full-pause (Composite 46, synergy stall). Working envelope: retention-and-slowdown.
3. **Round 3 — Synergy-pacing.** Three vectors: EUR 300M over 12 months (Composite 48, Talent pressure), EUR 300M over 14 months (Composite 76, calibrated), or EUR 300M over 18 months (Composite 58, investor-commitment drift). Working envelope: 14-month calibrated.
4. **Round 4 — Customer-and-morale rebuild.** Two vectors: targeted-customer-visit envelope at EUR 8M account-coverage (Composite 76, Customer Retention +7 pts) or internal-comms-only (Composite 44). Working envelope: targeted-customer.
5. **Round 5 — Post-merger capability-embedding.** Two vectors: full-stack (Composite 76, joint-roadmap, combined-incentive architecture) or ad-hoc (Composite 42). Working envelope: full-stack.

On Bendtsen-Holm's working model, an Intensity-5 / Retention-Pivot / 14-Month / Targeted-Customer / Full-Stack envelope delivered Synergies EUR 280M, Talent 82%, Customer 91%, Morale 74, Composite 74.

## Company Background

NovaTech had been founded in Copenhagen in 2004 and had, across twenty-two years, built a EUR 3.8B-revenue European enterprise-software group with 9,200 employees across Denmark, Germany, the United Kingdom, Spain and Poland, three operating lines (enterprise-platform, industry-vertical-solutions and customer-data-platform), and a February 14, 2026 close of the EUR 2.5B acquisition of DataPulse. The target had been purchased at a 12.8× trailing EBITDA multiple against a 2025 weighted-peer multiple of 11.4× — the EUR 2.5B deal priced a 90-bps premium that the deal model had justified on the EUR 300M three-year run-rate cost-synergy pool. The Q4 2025 pre-close due-diligence had documented a 22% first-year named-employee attrition risk in the DataPulse engineering bench and a 14-point Rule-of-40 gap to the top-quartile enterprise-software cohort.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the April 2026 IMO-steering-committee dossier. First, the March 8, 2026 resignation of the DataPulse Chief Technology Officer — the named architect of the customer-data-platform stack — triggered a 28-point Morale drop and a 12-point Talent-Retention drop across the first forty days. Second, the March 19 enterprise-customer advisory board had documented a 14-point customer-confidence drop in the four weeks following the CTO resignation. Third, the March 31 NovaTech Q1 2026 investor-update had reaffirmed the EUR 180M in-flight synergy commitment to the Q1 2027 investor day.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2019–2025 enterprise-software post-merger-integration literature had, across 42 documented integrations above EUR 1.5B deal value, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, Intensity-8 aggressive-cost-out envelopes had captured 35–45% more Year-1 synergies but had consumed 18–24 points of Talent Retention in 68% of cases, against 6–10 points for Intensity-5 best-of-both envelopes. Second, named-CTO or named-head-of-engineering resignations inside the first ninety days had preceded Customer-Retention drops of 8–14 points in 72% of documented cases, and had been arrested in 64% of cases through retention-and-slowdown pivots inside a thirty-day window. Third, twelve-month synergy-capture targets above 75% of three-year run-rate pools had preceded named-talent walkouts in 58% of cases, against 22% for fourteen-to-fifteen-month calibrated pacing.

Empirically, the post-2022 enterprise-software integration literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, targeted-customer-visit spend at EUR 4–EUR 10M in the first six months of a deal above EUR 1B had sustained Customer-Retention readings above 90% in 72% of documented cases. Second, joint-roadmap-and-combined-incentive architectures embedded inside the first twelve months had priced positive Year-2 revenue-synergy capture in 68% of cases, against 28% for ad-hoc post-merger postures.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the December 2025 session, approved the EUR 2.5B acquisition. The Head of Synergy Capture had, on March 6, delivered the EUR 300M cost-synergy plan. The Head of Talent had, on March 13, delivered the Q1 attrition diagnostic. The Head of Customer Success had, on March 20, delivered the enterprise-customer advisory-board read. The Chief Human Resources Officer had, on March 27, delivered the retention-envelope scoping.

### What Bendtsen-Holm knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. An Intensity-5 / Retention-Pivot / 14-Month / Targeted-Customer / Full-Stack envelope projected Composite 74. An Intensity-8 / Press-On / 12-Month / Internal-Comms-Only / Ad-Hoc envelope projected Composite 16. An Intensity-3 / Full-Pause / 18-Month / Targeted-Customer / Full-Stack envelope projected Composite 48.

### What he did not know

Whether the DataPulse engineering bench would absorb a successor-CTO inside the Q2 2026 window; whether the top-ten DataPulse enterprise customers would hold through the April-to-June 2026 renewal window; whether the 14-month calibrated pacing would hold investor commitment at the Q1 2027 investor day; and whether the EUR 15M retention envelope would arrest the named-engineer walkout list inside the ninety-day window.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Synergy Capture**: *"Intensity-5. Best-of-both. If we push to eight we lose twenty-five engineers in the customer-data-platform stack inside sixty days."*

**The Head of Talent**: *"Fifteen million. Retention grants. Six-month vesting on the named twenty-two. The attrition risk is not abstract."*

**The Head of Customer Success**: *"Eight million on targeted customer visits. The top-twenty accounts are sixty-two percent of the DataPulse ARR."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Synergies are a promise. Retention is the bill."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Bendtsen-Holm had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Intensity-5 / Retention-Pivot / 14-Month / Targeted-Customer / Full-Stack:** the recommended envelope. Synergies EUR 280M. Talent 82%. Customer 91%. Morale 74. Composite 74. Recommended.
- **Option B — Intensity-8 / Press-On / 12-Month / Internal-Comms-Only / Ad-Hoc:** the aggressive envelope. Composite 16. Rejected — named-talent-walkout probability above 70%.
- **Option C — Intensity-3 / Full-Pause / 18-Month / Targeted-Customer / Full-Stack:** the cautious envelope. Composite 48. Rejected — investor commitment at Q1 2027 drifts.

Bendtsen-Holm's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 2 — the CTO-resignation pivot — because it was the round that decided whether the DataPulse engineering bench held through the Q2 2026 renewal window or whether the customer-data-platform customer book followed the named engineers out.
