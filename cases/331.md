## Abstract

Botica del Sol S.A.C. was, in the case universe, a fourteen-year-old Lima-headquartered modern pharmacy chain founded in 2012 by two Universidad del Pacífico alumni and a Peruvian private-equity minority partner, now operating 87 own-brand retail locations across Lima Metropolitana and five provinces, at 2025 revenue PEN 284M, 1,240 employees, and a customer-service-representative opening at the Avenida Próceres branch in San Juan de Lurigancho paying PEN 600 gross per month part-time. On **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:12 p.m., at the family kitchen table on Jirón Lúcumo in San Juan de Lurigancho, **Andrea Quispe Mamani**, 17, finalist at I.E. José María Arguedas, had 48 hours until the Saturday-9:00-a.m. interview with Srta. Fernández, Head of HR, with the CV draft still marked with five errors in red pencil, no employer dossier, and no STAR answer prepared.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply a five-error CV audit (photo, email domain and format, delegate-experience entry, languages section, typo) to the Quispe draft, rebuild the CV to ATS-friendly and first-job-appropriate standards, and articulate why each error is a screening-stage signal rather than a formatting preference.
2. Construct a full STAR (Situation / Task / Action / Result) answer to a probable behavioral question, argue why Result is the most frequently omitted and most heavily weighted component, and apply the Bolles 1970 transferable-skills inventory to translate delegate-of-class and family-store experience into labor-market language.
3. Sequence a five-moment interview performance (arrival, greeting, *háblame de ti*, behavioral, closing) and apply the Mehrabian 1971 seven-thirty-eight-fifty-five non-verbal channel split to diagnose why words alone explain only a small share of the evaluator's impression.
4. Evaluate an AFP-versus-ONP pension enrollment decision on a forty-plus-year horizon for a seventeen-year-old Peruvian first-time formal worker, contrast the four AFP commission structures (Integra, Hábitat, Prima, Profuturo), and commit the correct choice given the time horizon.
5. Apply the Fisher-Ury 1981 positions-versus-interests framework to the Thursday-shift-swap-with-Valentina and the Friday-two-hour-unpaid-overtime-with-supervisor scenarios, invoke the 25% overtime-surcharge rule under Peruvian labor law, and articulate why *Mi Primera Entrevista* occupies a secondary-career-readiness slot in the 2026 Express catalogue distinct from MiEmpresa First Company (sim 079) and Nexus Human Capital (sim 031).

On the evening of **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:12 p.m., **Andrea Quispe Mamani** sat at the family kitchen table on Jirón Lúcumo in San Juan de Lurigancho, Lima, the printed CV draft between a cup of quaker-avena and a red-pencil annotated copy her tutor had returned that afternoon. Saturday at 9:00 a.m. was her first formal interview: customer service at Botica del Sol, Avenida Próceres branch. PEN 600 gross per month. Part-time. Six hours a day, Monday to Friday. The printed email confirmation was signed by *Srta. Fernández, Head of HR, Botica del Sol S.A.C.*

---

## The Protagonist

Quispe Mamani, 17, was finishing Quinto de Secundaria at I.E. José María Arguedas with a 16.4/20 running average and a specialisation in the administrative-commercial track. She had served two consecutive years as *delegada de aula* and had run the school store on Saturday mornings since sixth grade, managing a micro-inventory of roughly 40 SKUs and approximately PEN 180 of weekly rotation. Her mother was a nurse-technician at a MINSA public clinic; her father worked as an independent electrician. Her older brother, 23, had landed his first formal job in 2024 through a Computrabajo ad and had coached her on the CV five errors, the STAR method, and — crucially — the AFP question, because he had himself picked ONP by default in 2024 and had spent a year regretting it.

---

## The Decision

Quispe Mamani had to commit, by the Saturday 9:00 a.m. interview clock, a three-round career-readiness package.

1. **Round 1 — Prepare.** Fix the five CV errors. Compile the Botica del Sol employer dossier. Write one STAR answer.
2. **Round 2 — Interview.** Arrive five minutes early. Open with a firm handshake and CV ready. Deliver the *háblame de ti* in 90 seconds. Answer the behavioral in STAR. Close with a growth-opportunity question rather than a salary-first question.
3. **Round 3 — First week.** Sign the contract. Pick the AFP. Handle Valentina's Thursday shift-swap request with a Fisher-Ury interest move. Claim the 25% overtime surcharge on the Friday two-hour unpaid ask without losing the supervisor.

---

## Sectoral Background

The 2015–2025 Lima modern-pharmacy-retail category had, in the case universe, registered approximately PEN 5.8 billion in 2025 annual revenue across the four dominant operators, and employed roughly 34,000 customer-service representatives with a first-year turnover rate of approximately 38%. Botica del Sol's 2024 annual report disclosed an 87-store footprint, a mission statement anchored on *"acercar el cuidado de la salud al barrio"*, a documented 2025 customer-satisfaction score of 81 across the chain, and a young-first-job hiring policy that covered roughly 22% of its 1,240 headcount. Peruvian youth informality sat above 55% in 2025 — the single most decisive economic transition in the life of a seventeen-year-old Limeño was the walk into the first formal job. The 1981 Fisher-Ury negotiation canon and the 1971 Mehrabian non-verbal channel study were the two frameworks the simulation had to respect.

## The Situation

### How Andrea got here

The 2022–2025 Quinto-de-Secundaria administrative-commercial specialisation; the two consecutive delegada-de-aula terms; the 2020–2026 Saturday school-store operation; the brother's 2024 Computrabajo-to-first-job transition and his ONP-regret story; the April 18, 2026 Computrabajo application; the April 20 tutor CV review returning five errors in red pencil; and the April 23 7:12 p.m. kitchen-table preparation were the sequence.

### What Quispe Mamani knew

The five CV errors (unprofessional photo, the `princesa.andrea99@gmail.com` email, missing delegate entry, missing languages section, *farmaceútica* typo); the Mehrabian 7-38-55 split; the STAR structure; the Botica del Sol mission and 87-location footprint; the PEN 600 gross figure; the Peruvian labor-law 25% overtime-surcharge rule on the first two hours and 35% on subsequent hours; and the four AFP commission-structure options.

### What she did not know

Whether Srta. Fernández would ask the *háblame-de-ti* first or the behavioral first; whether the closing-question move would be read as ambitious or presumptuous; whether Prima's mixed commission or Integra's flow-only commission would cost less across her 43-year horizon to age 60; whether Valentina's Thursday shift-swap request would be a one-off favor or the opening move of a recurring pattern; and whether the Friday two-hour unpaid-overtime ask was a culture test, a law violation, or both.

### What others were saying

**The brother, Miguel (23, first-formal-job in 2024)**: *"Arregla el correo. Pon todo. Y no elijas ONP."*

**The tutor (42, Ciencias Sociales, I.E. José María Arguedas)**: *"La entrevista no es de talento. Es de preparación."*

**Srta. Fernández (38, Head of HR, in the confirmation email)**: *"Trae tu CV impreso, DNI y copia del récord de estudios. Puntualidad."*

**Valentina, the future peer colleague (in a hypothetical WhatsApp message sketch)**: *"Oye, ¿me cubres el jueves? Me debes una luego."*

**The Peruvian youth-informality signal (55%+ in 2025)**: silent backdrop.

---

## Options Under Consideration

Quispe Mamani had narrowed the career-readiness decision space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — Full preparation, structured interview, AFP with long-horizon logic, Fisher-Ury interests, overtime-rights enforcement (Recommended).** Respects the CV-audit / STAR / Mehrabian / Fisher-Ury canon; signs the PEN 600 contract with AFP Integra or Prima selected on commission-and-return grounds; answers Valentina with "sí, si anotamos cuándo me devuelves el favor"; claims the 25% surcharge on the two overtime hours politely and in writing.
- **Option B — Go in without preparation, answer honestly but unstructured, pick ONP by default, say yes to the shift-swap, say yes to the unpaid overtime.** Rejected — reproduces the youth-informality pattern, surrenders the STAR-wins-over-generalities finding, locks in the ONP pension at a 43-year cost, establishes a precedent of exploitability.
- **Option C — Decline the Saturday interview and apply elsewhere first to practice.** Defensible on nervousness grounds but surrenders the Botica del Sol opportunity and forgoes the learning accelerant that comes from interviewing with real stakes.

Quispe Mamani's private view was that Option A was the right commitment, and that the single decision most correlated with the shift outcome was the AFP choice — because her 43-year horizon to age 60 compounded any monthly commission differential into a six-figure-soles lifetime difference, and because her brother's 2024 ONP-regret story was the most concrete proof-point she had. The *Mi Primera Entrevista* frame sits at the secondary-career-readiness tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from MiEmpresa First Company (sim 079, first-venture frame) and Nexus Human Capital (sim 031, executive-HR frame).
