## Abstract

ALTIPLANO VENTURES was, in the case universe, a Bolivian diversified holding company operating across mining (copper, lithium and zinc concentrate across three Altiplano operations, 62% of 2025 revenue) and agriculture (soy, quinoa and specialty grains across eastern Bolivia and the Santa Cruz lowlands, 38% of 2025 revenue), with USD 800M in 2025 revenue, USD 224M in EBITDA, USD 55M in discretionary-capital envelope, an Exploration Index reading of 0 against an emerging-market diversified-holding peer-benchmark of 12–18, an Adaptability Index of 0 against a peer-benchmark of 14–22, and a corporate-strategy-operating model that had, across the 2020–2025 commodity super-cycle, maximised throughput and margin capture without installing deliberate slack for exploration or serendipity. The 2023–2025 CEO-operating model had been anchored on a plan-dominant discipline that had delivered revenue stability but had imported a near-zero Exploration Index — a pattern the Q4 2025 Chief-Strategy-Officer memo had flagged as structurally fragile against the probability-weighted commodity-cycle forecasts for 2026–2028. The Q1 2026 board had approved a five-round Chief-Executive-Officer-led strategic-uncertainty commitment against an EBITDA-defence floor of USD 160M, an Exploration-Index floor above 14, an Adaptability-Index floor above 18, and a Composite Strategic-Uncertainty Score above 70. The case opens at 5:44 a.m. on Thursday, April 16, 2026, at the Altiplano Ventures headquarters in La Paz as **Sebastián Vargas**, a 52-year-old Chief Executive Officer, prepares the Round-1 plan-versus-exploration commitment. Seventy-one days separate him from the June 26 board strategy-review.

4. Respond to the Round-4 pivot-or-persist commitment (signals read as structural rather than cyclical) and recommend the systematic capital-shift-to-non-commodity envelope against the persist-through-cycle envelope on the EBITDA-trajectory basis.
5. Design the Round-5 sustained-serendipity architecture (twenty-percent discretionary to exploration, moderate governance, coherence bonuses) and diagnose when slack-plus-optionality becomes a sustained capability rather than a one-time stunt.

## The Protagonist

Sebastián Vargas, 52, had assumed the Chief-Executive-Officer role at Altiplano Ventures on March 1, 2022, from a Tier-1 Chilean diversified holding where he had, across fourteen years, led two capital-allocation rebuilds and had closed a 2018 non-commodity-pivot that preserved EBITDA-above-USD-140M through the 2019–2020 commodity cycle by virtue of a twenty-percent exploration-discipline he had installed in 2016. Before that he had spent seven years as Head of Corporate Development at a Peruvian mining group. The board had recruited him specifically on two capabilities: his documented willingness to install organisational slack against CFO resistance, and his discipline on refusing to treat a commodity-cycle downturn as a trigger to kill exploration budgets. His 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the EBITDA-defence outcome and 25% to the Exploration-Index floor.

---

## The Decision

Vargas had to commit, by the May 5 board pre-review, the Round-1 plan-versus-exploration envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Plan-versus-exploration philosophy with organisational slack.** Three archetypes: balanced-philosophy (70% planned capital, 30% exploration, moderate organisational slack installed across mining and agriculture, Composite 72), plan-dominant (95% planned, 5% exploration, no slack — Composite 42, fragility under black swan), or exploration-bias (50% planned, 50% exploration, high slack — Composite 58, EBITDA-defence fragility). Working envelope: balanced-philosophy.
2. **Round 2 — TerraMetrics minority position.** Three archetypes: minority-position (USD 12M for 18% equity, climate-analytics telemetry, board-observer seat, Composite 76), full-acquisition (USD 68M for 100% — Composite 52, capital-concentration risk), or pass (no position — Composite 58, optionality forfeit). Working envelope: minority-position.
3. **Round 3 — Commodity black swan.** Copper and lithium −25% for 18–24 months; mining EBITDA projected −40%. Three vectors: defend-and-wait (organisational slack absorbs shock, no aggressive restructure, TerraMetrics telemetry informs wait-and-see, redeploy capital slowly, Composite 74), aggressive-restructure (close two mining operations, reduce headcount 22% — Composite 48, optionality forfeit and community-license risk), or double-down-on-commodities (counter-cyclical commodity investment — Composite 38, EBITDA-defence breach). Working envelope: defend-and-wait.
4. **Round 4 — Pivot-or-persist.** Signals read as structural (not cyclical). Three vectors: systematic-capital-shift-to-non-commodity (shift discretionary capital systematically into agricultural and TerraMetrics-aligned adjacencies, Composite 76), persist-through-cycle (treat as cyclical, hold-position — Composite 46, EBITDA-trajectory risk), or rapid-full-exit (exit commodity positions — Composite 42, value destruction). Working envelope: systematic-shift.
5. **Round 5 — Sustained-serendipity architecture.** Three vectors: twenty-percent-discretionary-plus-moderate-governance (20% of discretionary capital permanently allocated to exploration, moderate governance with documented criteria, coherence-bonuses for exploration leaders who deliver learning even without revenue, Composite 80), one-time-exploration-fund (single-cycle fund — Composite 58, not-a-capability), or zero-slack (return to plan-dominant philosophy — Composite 34, regression). Working envelope: twenty-percent-plus-moderate-governance.

On Vargas's working model, a Balanced-Philosophy / Minority-Position / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / Twenty-Percent-Plus-Governance envelope delivered EBITDA-defence USD 176M, Exploration Index 18, Adaptability Index 22, Composite 80. A Plan-Dominant / Pass / Aggressive-Restructure / Persist / Zero-Slack envelope delivered EBITDA-defence USD 124M, Exploration Index 0, Adaptability Index 0, Composite 32. A Balanced / Full-Acquisition / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / One-Time-Fund envelope delivered EBITDA-defence USD 152M, Exploration Index 12, Adaptability Index 14, Composite 58.

## Company Background

Altiplano Ventures had been founded in La Paz in 1984 as a single copper-concentrate trading operation and had, across forty-two years, grown into a diversified holding operating across mining and agriculture, with USD 800M in 2025 revenue, USD 224M in EBITDA and approximately 4,800 employees across three mining operations in the Bolivian Altiplano, four agricultural operations across the Santa Cruz lowlands and eastern Bolivia, and a small commercial-trading desk. The 2020–2025 corporate-strategy operating model had been anchored on a plan-dominant discipline that had captured the commodity super-cycle and delivered consistent EBITDA — but had left the organisation with an Exploration Index and an Adaptability Index both at zero against an emerging-market diversified-holding peer-benchmark of 12–22.

The Q1 2026 board dossier had identified three specific pressures the five-round envelope would inherit. First, the March 2026 commodity-cycle forward-curve had placed a 58% probability weight on a copper and lithium price decline of 20–30% across an eighteen-to-twenty-four-month window. Second, the TerraMetrics approach of February 2026 had imported a 90-day decision window on the USD 12M minority-position term-sheet. Third, the 2026 investor-narrative had committed to a Composite Strategic-Uncertainty Score above 70, a commitment the five-round envelope was designed to deliver.

## Industry and Market Context

The LATAM diversified-holding strategic-uncertainty landscape in 2026 was characterised by three structural features the envelope sat inside. First, the 2018–2024 organisational-slack evidence base had, across 42 LATAM and South-Asian diversified holdings, documented a Composite Strategic-Uncertainty Score differential of 22–28 points between balanced-philosophy envelopes and plan-dominant envelopes. Second, the 2020–2024 minority-position-for-learning literature had, across 34 emerging-market holdings, placed minority-position envelopes at a twenty-four-month optionality-value differential of 3.8–5.2× invested capital against pass envelopes. Third, the 2018–2024 commodity-black-swan literature had, across 28 LATAM mining-and-agriculture holdings, placed defend-and-wait envelopes at an EBITDA-defence differential of USD 38–62M against aggressive-restructure envelopes on the USD-800M-revenue base.

Empirically, the post-2022 strategic-uncertainty literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, holdings that had installed twenty-percent discretionary exploration budgets with moderate governance had, across a 2019–2024 sample of 36 emerging-market holdings, preserved an Exploration Index above 14 in 82% of cases against 8% for holdings on plan-dominant models. Second, holdings that had treated structural signals as cyclical and persisted through cycle had, across the same sample, registered EBITDA-defence forfeits of USD 30–70M per USD-billion of revenue against systematic-capital-shift counterparts.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the November 2025 strategy-review, approved the uncertainty-anchored mandate and the June 26 review calendar. The Chief Financial Officer had, on January 19, delivered the capital-allocation philosophy options. The Chief Strategy Officer had, on January 26, delivered the Exploration-and-Adaptability-Index baseline. The Head of Corporate Development had, on February 12, delivered the TerraMetrics term-sheet. The commodity-cycle forward-curve had been updated on March 9. The TerraMetrics 90-day decision window had started on February 18.

### What Vargas knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. A Balanced-Philosophy / Minority-Position / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / Twenty-Percent-Plus-Governance Envelope projected Composite 80. A Plan-Dominant / Pass / Aggressive-Restructure / Persist / Zero-Slack Envelope projected Composite 32. A Balanced / Full-Acquisition / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / One-Time-Fund Envelope projected Composite 58.

### What he did not know

Whether the copper-and-lithium down-cycle would last eighteen or twenty-four months — or whether the forward-curve 58% probability-weight would prove conservative; whether the TerraMetrics technology would mature inside the eighteen-month horizon to deliver the agricultural-data advantage the Chief Strategy Officer had priced into the envelope; whether the systematic-capital-shift to non-commodity adjacencies would absorb capital at a rate the CFO's covenants would tolerate; and whether the twenty-percent discretionary exploration discipline would be sustainable inside the board-reporting cadence without drift back to plan-dominance by Q4 2026.

### What others were saying

**The Chief Financial Officer**: *"Balanced. Seventy-thirty. Moderate slack. The commodity cycle will not wait for us to argue about it."*

**The Chief Strategy Officer**: *"TerraMetrics. Twelve million for eighteen percent. We do not buy the company; we buy the telemetry."*

**The Head of Corporate Development**: *"Defend and wait. The worst thing we can do is restructure in month three of a twenty-four-month cycle."*

**The Chair of the Board**: *"You cannot plan serendipity. You can make room for it."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Vargas had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Balanced / Minority-Position / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / Twenty-Percent-Plus-Governance:** the recommended envelope. EBITDA-defence USD 176M. Exploration Index 18. Adaptability Index 22. Composite 80. Recommended.
- **Option B — Plan-Dominant / Pass / Aggressive-Restructure / Persist / Zero-Slack:** the defensive envelope. Composite 32. Rejected — optionality forfeit and EBITDA-defence breach.
- **Option C — Balanced / Full-Acquisition / Defend-and-Wait / Systematic-Shift / One-Time-Fund:** the one-cycle envelope. Composite 58. Rejected — capital-concentration risk and serendipity-as-capability forfeit.

Vargas's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 5 — the sustained-serendipity architecture that made slack-plus-optionality a capability rather than a one-time stunt, and that anchored every downstream commodity-cycle the holding would face.
