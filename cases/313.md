## Abstract

InduColora S.A.S. was, in the case universe, a 54-year-old Medellín-headquartered paints-and-coatings manufacturer founded in 1972 by the Restrepo family and now in its third-generation leadership serving four product lines (Industrial Coatings, Architectural Paints, Premium Architectural, Specialty Solvents) across Colombia — approximately COP 148bn 2025 revenue, 312 employees, a single-rate traditional-costing system allocating COP 12.4bn of overhead at COP 68,889 per machine hour, a year-on-year revenue uplift of 12% against a net-margin collapse from 8.2% to 2.1%, a sales-incentive structure paying higher commissions on Specialty-Solvent than on the other three lines, and an April 24 board meeting asking the General Manager to explain where the money went. On **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:14 a.m., **Dr. Valentina Restrepo-Cadavid**, a 43-year-old incoming General Manager and third-generation family-member arriving on her ninetieth day in role, opened the Activity-Based-Costing diagnostic her controller had finalised at 11:02 p.m. the previous evening and began drafting the three-round package.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply Horngren's single-rate-traditional-costing framework to InduColora's COP 68,889-per-machine-hour allocation, articulate why traditional costing is legal, auditable and NIIF-compliant yet systematically wrong for decision purposes when the cost drivers diverge from volume, and articulate why the financial statements were right and the internal decision system was wrong.
2. Apply Cooper and Kaplan's 1988 Activity-Based-Costing framework to the four-product portfolio, identify setups / quality-tests / regulatory-filings / engineering-change-orders as the four activity drivers, quantify the Specialty-Solvent 25%-of-units-versus-71%-of-setups-and-88%-of-filings consumption asymmetry, and restate the contribution margin from the 53.5%-apparent to the −18.0%-ABC reading.
3. Apply Horngren's relevant-cost framework to the Specialty-Solvent make-or-buy decision, articulate why the COP 74,000 fully-absorbed cost is not the relevant cost for the decision, identify the COP 58,000 relevant-cost-of-making reading, and articulate why the decision reverses under relevant-cost logic.
4. Apply Eccles's 1985 transfer-pricing framework to the Grupo-Andino Inmobiliaria-Andina internal supply, compare the market / cost-plus / negotiated options, articulate the ABC-cost-plus invisibility problem, and articulate why every transfer-pricing choice is a governance choice disguised as an accounting choice.
5. Apply Horngren's three-way variance-analysis framework (price / efficiency / volume) to the COP 15.4m Industrial-Coatings cost overrun, disaggregate the 3.36-unfavourable materials-price variance, the 6.84-unfavourable efficiency variance and the 5.2-unfavourable volume variance, reset the sales-incentive structure to remove the Specialty-Solvent bonus, and articulate why the managerial-accounting frame sim 313 occupies is distinct from Andina ConsumerProducts CostLab (sim 209) — 313 emphasises the LATAM pedagogical-primer family-firm frame with the ABC / relevant-cost / transfer-pricing / variance-analysis synthesis.

On the morning of **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 7:14 a.m., **Dr. Valentina Restrepo-Cadavid** arrived at the InduColora headquarters in the Guayabal industrial district on her ninetieth day in the General-Manager role, greeted the two overnight-shift parrilleros finishing a batch, and walked into the GM office past the wall of five framed photographs (the 1972 founding of the original Restrepo y Cia. workshop; the 1989 first industrial-scale line; the 2003 regional-Antioquia expansion; the 2015 Grupo-Andino holding-company consolidation; the 2023 NIIF-Plenas-compliance migration). On the desk sat the 88-page ABC diagnostic her controller had finalised at 11:02 p.m. The top-line number was visible on the first chart: apparent Specialty-Solvent contribution margin 53.5%, ABC-restated contribution margin −18.0%. The 90-day diagnostic window was on day 90 of 90.

---

## The Protagonist

Restrepo-Cadavid, 43, had been General Manager at InduColora since January 23, 2026 — ninety days into role at the case opening — had completed an Ingeniería Industrial at EAFIT in 2004 and an MBA at the Wharton School in 2010, had spent seven years at a global paints-and-coatings multinational in São Paulo through 2017, had led the Andean regional commercial office of a chemicals major between 2018 and 2023, and had returned to Medellín in 2023 as heir-apparent and member of the Restrepo family council before the January 2026 GM appointment. Her managerial-accounting instincts had been shaped by three formative exposures: the 2009 Wharton cost-accounting elective taught on the Cooper-Kaplan ABC canon; the 2016 São-Paulo restatement of a specialty-solvent line where ABC had revealed a cross-subsidy similar to the one InduColora now exhibited; and the 2021 Andean transfer-pricing restatement for a sister-company intercompany supply that had fractured a family board until the governance framing rescued the negotiation.

---

## The Decision

Restrepo-Cadavid had to commit, by the 4:00 p.m. Restrepo-family-council-and-board-session, the three-round package.

1. **Round 1 — ABC diagnostic and contribution-margin restatement.** Accept the ABC reading. Identify the cross-subsidy. Commit the contribution-margin restatement for use in the product-portfolio and sales-incentive decisions.
2. **Round 2 — Make-or-Buy and transfer pricing.** Commit the relevant-cost-logic-continue-making posture on Specialty-Solvent. Commit the ABC-cost-plus transfer-pricing method for the Inmobiliaria-Andina supply with a governance-framed communication to the Grupo-Andino board.
3. **Round 3 — Variance analysis and sales-incentive reset.** Disaggregate the COP 15.4m cost overrun across price / efficiency / volume. Assign accountability by variance. Reset the sales-incentive structure to remove the Specialty-Solvent bonus and install a margin-weighted commission formula. Recommend Specialty-Solvent repricing-or-discontinuation by Q3.

Commitments: the Round 1 ABC-diagnostic commitment; the Round 2 relevant-cost and transfer-pricing commitment; the Round 3 variance-disaggregation and sales-incentive-reset commitment; and a one-page board-and-family-council memo that frames the three-round commitment as a single 90-day diagnostic-plus-180-day-repositioning arc.

## Sectoral Background

The 1985–2025 Colombian paints-and-coatings category had, in the case universe, registered approximately COP 3.4tn in aggregate annual revenue across the 87 Registro-Nacional-de-Pinturas-registered manufacturers, concentrated in three operator tiers (multinational brands like PPG, AkzoNobel and Sherwin-Williams; regional independents like Pintuco, InduColora and Coralit; specialty-and-architectural niche firms). The 1988–2025 specialty-solvent-product-line panel had documented a recurring pattern where family-owned manufacturers with traditional-costing systems systematically over-estimated specialty-line profitability and under-estimated volume-line profitability, with documented restatement-under-ABC readings showing contribution-margin reversals in approximately 42% of panel-match cases.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1987–2025 managerial-accounting literature produced three findings the simulation had to respect. First, Cooper and Kaplan's 1988 ABC framework, combined with the Kaplan-Norton 1996 cost-accounting-for-decisions canon, had produced documented restatement-under-ABC readings across approximately 1,400 panel-match manufacturing cases showing contribution-margin reversals in approximately 42% of cases and sales-incentive-structure mis-alignment in approximately 68%. Second, Horngren's relevant-cost-and-decision framework had documented make-or-buy-reversal rates approximately 2.4× higher under relevant-cost logic than under fully-absorbed-cost logic. Third, Eccles's 1985 transfer-pricing framework had documented that governance-framed transfer-pricing choices produced family-firm-board-harmony readings approximately 1.8× higher than accounting-framed transfer-pricing choices.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The 1972 Restrepo y Cia. founding; the 1989 industrial-scale line; the 2003 regional-Antioquia expansion; the 2015 Grupo-Andino consolidation; the 2023 NIIF-Plenas migration that left the internal management-accounting system on a traditional-costing footing; the January 23, 2026 Restrepo-Cadavid appointment; the 90-day diagnostic commissioned on Day 3; the April 22 ABC diagnostic completion; and the April 23 board-and-family-council session were the sequence.

### What Restrepo-Cadavid knew

The year-on-year revenue uplift at 12% and the net-margin collapse from 8.2% to 2.1%; the ABC diagnostic findings on the Specialty-Solvent cross-subsidy; the COP 67,000-per-unit outsourcing offer from a distributor; the Grupo-Andino Inmobiliaria-Andina transfer-pricing request; the COP 15.4m cost overrun on Industrial Coatings; the sales-incentive structure paying Specialty-Solvent bonuses; the Restrepo family council's appetite for a non-family-fracturing repositioning; and the April 24 board meeting.

### What she did not know

Whether the sales-team would accept the Specialty-Solvent incentive-removal without turnover; whether the Grupo-Andino board would accept the ABC-cost-plus transfer-pricing method over the family-council-preferred negotiated method; whether the Industrial-Coatings efficiency-variance accountability would be accepted by the production director; whether the TiO2 price lock-in discipline the procurement director had missed would be repeatable under the proposed incentive reset; and whether the Specialty-Solvent repricing-or-discontinuation decision would be palatable to the family-council in year fifty-five.

### What others were saying

**The Restrepo-family-council senior (uncle of the protagonist, 71)**: *"Valentina, cincuenta y cuatro años en esta familia. El color de los solventes es nuestro color. No lo apagues sin mirarme a los ojos."*

**The Controller (CFO direct-report, 39, four years)**: *"Los estados no mienten. El sistema interno sí. Esto es ABC en estado puro."*

**The Production Director (51, 22 years, first-generation-raised)**: *"Si me dicen que fui ineficiente, quiero ver el número. Si el número sale de horas máquina, lo discuto."*

**The Commercial Director (47, 15 years)**: *"Mis mejores vendedores viven del bono sobre Specialty. Si lo sacas, se van a la competencia."*

**The Grupo-Andino CFO (holding-company counterparty, 58)**: *"Queremos el ABC-cost-plus. Es más transparente. Pero no rompas la familia."*

**The Restrepo-family matriarch (founder's daughter, 68)**: *"Cincuenta y cinco años. Ni cincuenta y seis fracturados. Ni cuarenta y nueve tranquilos."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Restrepo-Cadavid had narrowed the managerial-accounting space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — ABC diagnostic + relevant-cost continue-making + ABC-cost-plus transfer pricing + variance-disaggregation-and-incentive-reset (Recommended).** Respects the Cooper-Kaplan ABC framework; identifies the cross-subsidy; preserves the Specialty-Solvent line under relevant-cost logic while repricing upward; frames the Grupo-Andino transfer pricing as governance-framed; disaggregates the IC cost overrun across three accountabilities; resets the sales-incentive structure. Projected twelve-month margin recovery to 5.8%.
- **Option B — Traditional-costing-kept + outsource-Specialty + negotiated transfer pricing + undisaggregated overrun absorbed + incentive-unchanged.** Rejected — reproduces the 2021–2025 pattern that produced the margin collapse; outsources the wrong product at a false-signal cost reading; accepts a negotiated-transfer-price that will fracture the Grupo-Andino governance; leaves the IC cost overrun unaddressed; and preserves the incentive structure that destroyed the margin.
- **Option C — ABC-diagnostic + discontinue-Specialty-immediately + market-transfer-pricing + aggressive variance-witch-hunt.** Defensible on margin-recovery grounds but risks a family-council-fracture in year fifty-five, an aggressive transfer-price reading that the Inmobiliaria-Andina refuses, and a variance-witch-hunt that depletes the production-and-commercial leadership.

Restrepo-Cadavid's private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the single decision most correlated with the twelve-month margin-recovery outcome was the Round 1 sales-incentive reset, because the 1987–2025 panel had shown that sales-incentive-structure mis-alignment correlated at 0.68 with contribution-margin-reversal outcomes, and because her 2016 São-Paulo experience had shown that the ABC diagnostic without the incentive reset produced a two-quarter margin recovery followed by a relapse. The *Costo de Decidir* frame sits at the LATAM family-firm pedagogical-primer tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from Andina ConsumerProducts CostLab (sim 209, cost-laboratory-sandbox frame) — 313 emphasises the ABC / relevant-cost / transfer-pricing / variance-analysis synthesis in a Colombian third-generation family firm.
