## Abstract

CERVECERÍA CARIBE was, in the case universe, a family-controlled Colombian national brewery with COP 3.8 trillion (≈USD 920M) in 2025 revenue, four brewing-and-bottling sites across Barranquilla, Medellín, Cali and Bogotá, and a flagship Barranquilla bottling line that had, across 2023–2025, drifted to a 4.8% outgoing-defect rate against a Colombian peer-benchmark of 2.3% and an internal target of 2.1%. The Q4 2025 operations-review had priced the aggregate defect cost — scrap, rework, complaint-driven returns, supply-chain-replacement expedites — at USD 4.6M annualised, and the Q1 2026 board of directors had approved a USD 240K DMAIC project envelope under the Lean-Six-Sigma deployment Cervecería Caribe had launched in mid-2024 after a 2023 competitive pressure from a regional Tier-1 brewer. The envelope committed the Black-Belt project lead to a five-phase cycle — Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control — against a Defect-Rate floor at or below 2.2%, a Sigma Level floor above 3.8σ, a Cpk above 1.33, and a composite Quality Score above 70. The case opens at 5:48 a.m. on Wednesday, April 1, 2026, at the Barranquilla plant as **Mateo Quiroga Bermúdez**, a 41-year-old Black-Belt, prepares the Define-phase commitment. Forty-nine days separate him from the May 20 steering-committee phase-gate review.

## The Protagonist

Mateo Quiroga Bermúdez, 41, had joined Cervecería Caribe in March 2024 from a regional Colombian consumer-goods operator where he had, across eight years, earned a Green-Belt and then a Black-Belt under a Master-Black-Belt coaching program, and had led three DMAIC cycles on filling-line variability, labelling defects and warehouse fulfilment accuracy. Before that, he had spent six years as a process engineer at a Barranquilla beverages-packaging plant. The Chief Operating Officer had recruited him specifically as the anchor Black-Belt for the 2024–2027 Lean-Six-Sigma deployment and had asked him to lead the first flagship DMAIC on the Barranquilla bottling line. His 2026 variable compensation tied 30% to the DMAIC project completion and 20% to the sustained Cpk floor at ninety-day post-Control close.

## The Decision

Quiroga had to commit, by the April 15 steering-committee Define-phase gate, the Define-phase commitment and the directional intent for Measure, Analyze, Improve and Control.

1. **Define — primary CTQ selection.** Three archetypes: single-CTQ-focus on bottle fill-level variance (62% of defect Pareto, project scope tight, team size 4, project duration 49 days, cost USD 210K), multi-CTQ (fill-level plus cork-seal, project scope wide, duration 82 days, cost USD 350K — outside envelope), or multi-CTQ-sequential (fill-level first then cork-seal as a follow-on DMAIC — Q3 2026 second cycle). Working envelope: single-CTQ-focus with sequential follow-on plan.
2. **Measure — process-capability diagnosis.** Three vectors: baseline-SPC-plus-capability (build R-chart, X-bar chart, capability indices — Cp 0.78, Cpk 0.61 reveal a combined centring-plus-variability problem), gauge-R&R-only (measurement-system adequacy confirmed but process-capability gap not surfaced), or short-run-SPC-only (variability assessed but centring missed). Working envelope: baseline-SPC-plus-capability.
3. **Analyze — URGENT filler-head root-cause event.** Five-why trail surfaces filler-head wear as the primary driver; cross-section analysis on sampled filler heads shows actual failure at day 58 against a scheduled 90-day replacement interval. Three vectors: condition-based-replacement based on filler-head-wear sensor readings (CAPEX sensor installation, OPEX reduction, forecast Cpk 1.38), fixed-60-day-replacement (OPEX up, Cpk ~1.05), or dual-track-maintenance-and-SPC-tightening (OPEX flat, Cpk ~0.95). Working envelope: condition-based-replacement.
4. **Improve — Poka-Yoke plus condition-based replacement.** Three vectors: condition-based-replacement plus Poka-Yoke line-stop sensor (USD 180K implementation, defect-rate forecast 2.1%, Cpk 1.38), pure-maintenance-tightening at fixed 60-day intervals (USD 80K implementation, defect-rate 3.2%, Cpk 1.05), or full-filler-head-replacement with new-generation design (USD 420K — outside envelope). Working envelope: condition-based-replacement plus Poka-Yoke.
5. **Control — SPC-plus-audit sustainment.** Three vectors: SPC-plus-quarterly-audit (USD 36K annualised audit plus maintained SPC charts, ninety-day Cpk retention rate 92% on 2020–2024 Caribbean-peer-sample), SPC-only (retention 68%), or audit-only-without-SPC (retention 42%). Working envelope: SPC-plus-quarterly-audit.

On Quiroga's working model, a Single-CTQ / SPC-Plus-Capability / Condition-Based-Replacement / Poka-Yoke / SPC-Plus-Audit envelope delivered Defect Rate 2.1%, Sigma Level 3.9σ, Cpk 1.38, Composite 78 — the trajectory that closed the DMAIC project inside the USD 240K envelope. A Multi-CTQ / Gauge-R&R-Only / Fixed-Replacement / Maintenance-Tightening / SPC-Only Envelope delivered Defect Rate 3.5%, Sigma Level 3.2σ, Cpk 1.05, Composite 48 — the trajectory that would forfeit the Cpk floor and the steering-committee sign-off.

## Plant Background

Cervecería Caribe had been founded in Barranquilla in 1931 as a family-controlled brewer and had, through four decades of consolidation, grown into a Colombian national operator with four brewing-and-bottling sites across Barranquilla, Medellín, Cali and Bogotá. The flagship Barranquilla bottling line had been re-commissioned in 2018 on a high-speed rotary filler rated at 48,000 bottles per hour across three fill-volume SKUs. The 2023 introduction of a competing Tier-1 regional brewer into the Colombian Caribbean-coast market had compressed Cervecería Caribe's shelf-presence margin and had, in the Q3 2023 board review, priced a quality-perception slip at 4.2 NPS points. The 2024 Lean-Six-Sigma deployment had committed the operations function to two Black-Belt cycles per year across the four sites.

The December 2025 steering-committee dossier had identified three specific pressures the five-phase envelope would inherit. First, the January 2026 in-market-complaint review had placed bottle fill-level variance at the centre of consumer-voice signals (62% of complaints referenced underfill). Second, the Q4 2025 filler-head maintenance log had suggested — but not confirmed — a mean-time-between-failure shift from the specified 90 days toward the 55–65-day range. Third, the Q1 2026 capex committee had pre-authorised up to USD 200K of condition-based-maintenance sensor capex against a documented root-cause diagnosis.

## Industry and Market Context

The Latin-American beverage Lean-Six-Sigma landscape in 2026 was characterised by three structural features the envelope sat inside. First, the 2019–2024 DMAIC-cycle evidence base had, across 58 documented beverage bottling-line DMAIC cycles, placed single-CTQ-focus projects at a phase-gate close rate of 84% against 38% for multi-CTQ projects inside a comparable budget envelope — a pattern that anchored the Define commitment. Second, the 2020–2024 filler-head-wear literature had, across 34 high-speed rotary-filler operations, documented actual mean-time-between-failure distributions 28–38% shorter than manufacturer-specification on filler-head-wear failure modes. Third, the 2018–2024 Lean-Six-Sigma sustainment evidence had, across 42 Control-phase hand-offs, placed SPC-plus-quarterly-audit sustainment at a ninety-day Cpk-retention rate of 89–94% against 58–72% for SPC-only sustainment.

Empirically, the post-2022 quality-management literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, DMAIC cycles that had surfaced a combined centring-and-variability process-capability reading inside Measure had, across a 2020–2024 sample of 48 beverage operations, identified a single-actionable root cause in 79% of Analyze phases against 34% for variability-only readings. Second, DMAIC cycles that had coupled a condition-based-replacement intervention with a Poka-Yoke mistake-proofing sensor had, across the same sample, held Cpk above 1.33 in 82% of ninety-day post-close audits against 31% for condition-based-replacement-only interventions.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Chief Operating Officer had, in the December 2025 steering-committee, approved the USD 240K DMAIC envelope and the single-CTQ-focus scope. The Master Black-Belt had, on January 19, coached the project charter onto the bottle fill-level variance CTQ. The Quality Director had, on January 26, published the 62-21-17 defect Pareto. The Maintenance Manager had, on February 9, confirmed the filler-head-wear hypothesis against the maintenance log. The Plant Controller had, on February 16, confirmed the USD 200K condition-based-maintenance capex pre-authorisation. The Chief Financial Officer had, on February 23, priced the aggregate defect cost at USD 4.6M annualised.

### What Quiroga knew

The team had modelled three five-phase envelopes. A Single-CTQ / SPC-Plus-Capability / Condition-Based-Replacement / Poka-Yoke / Audit Envelope projected Composite 78. A Multi-CTQ / Gauge-R&R-Only / Fixed-Replacement / Maintenance-Tightening / SPC-Only Envelope projected Composite 48. A Single-CTQ / SPC-Plus-Capability / Condition-Based-Replacement / Pure-Maintenance / Audit Envelope projected Composite 64 — trajectory that closed Cp but forfeited Cpk against the Poka-Yoke.

### What he did not know

Whether the filler-head-wear mean-time-between-failure distribution would hold on the full-population post-intervention measurement; whether the Poka-Yoke line-stop sensor would, at the 48,000-bottles-per-hour line speed, introduce a false-positive rate that forced a re-calibration inside Control; whether the cork-seal follow-on DMAIC would be approved at the Q3 2026 steering committee; and whether the January 2026 competitive-pressure trajectory from the Tier-1 regional brewer would require an accelerated DMAIC pipeline on the Medellín site.

### What others were saying

**The Master Black-Belt**: *"Single-CTQ. Fill-level variance. Do not chase the cork seal in this cycle."*

**The Quality Director**: *"Measure surfaces a centring-plus-variability problem. The Pareto alone misses the Cpk reading."*

**The Maintenance Manager**: *"Condition-based replacement. The filler-head fails at day 58. Scheduled 90-day replacement has never been the right interval."*

**The Plant Controller**: *"USD 180K inside the USD 200K pre-authorisation. The project clears the capex committee."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Quiroga had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Single-CTQ / SPC-Plus-Capability / Condition-Based-Replacement / Poka-Yoke / SPC-Plus-Audit:** the recommended envelope. Defect Rate 2.1%. Sigma Level 3.9σ. Cpk 1.38. Composite 78. Recommended.
- **Option B — Multi-CTQ / Gauge-R&R-Only / Fixed-Replacement / Maintenance-Tightening / SPC-Only:** the minimalist envelope. Composite 48. Rejected — fails Cpk and sustainment floors.
- **Option C — Single-CTQ / SPC-Plus-Capability / Condition-Based-Replacement / Pure-Maintenance / SPC-Plus-Audit:** the capex-light envelope. Composite 64. Rejected — clears Cp but forfeits Cpk-above-1.33 against the Poka-Yoke.

Quiroga's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single phase most correlated with the final Composite was Analyze — the condition-based-replacement root-cause commitment that, on the 2020–2024 evidence base, unlocked the Poka-Yoke Improve-phase intervention that closed Cpk above 1.33.
