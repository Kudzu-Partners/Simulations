## Abstract

Ciudad Valparaíso was, in the case universe, a Pacific-coast metropolitan area of 1.24M population across a 620-km² footprint and 12 constituent municipalities, with a 2025 Congestion Index reading of 1.80 on a 2019-rebased baseline of 1.30 (the Index captures peak-period travel-time relative to free-flow) against a published Mayor's Office target of 1.20, a 2025 Air Quality Index failing the WHO PM2.5 annual-mean threshold by 42%, a 2025 public-transit modal-share reading of 31% against a 2019 baseline of 38%, and a 2025 car-ownership intensity of 248 vehicles per thousand inhabitants against a 2019 baseline of 211. The 2019–2024 mobility-investment programme had committed USD 620M across four new road-capacity projects and had delivered a Congestion Index *worsening* from 1.42 to 1.78 — a trajectory the November 2025 internal review had attributed to induced-demand dynamics rather than to execution quality. The Q1 2026 Metropolitan Council had approved a USD 48M five-round systems-anchored mobility-intervention envelope — the SystemsMap programme — and had positioned it as the Mayor's Office response to three overlapping pressures: an air-quality-driven health-ministry advisory issued in January 2026, a February 2026 nine-day transit strike that had spiked Congestion to 2.4, and a March 2026 citizen-engagement diagnostic that had surfaced a 62% dissatisfaction reading against a 2019 baseline of 38%. The Q1 2026 Council had reaffirmed the USD 48M envelope and a Chief-Systems-Officer-led five-round commitment cycle against a Congestion floor below 1.20, an Air Quality-compliance floor, a Political-Durability floor above 58, and a Composite SystemsMap Score above 68. The case opens at 6:52 a.m. on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, at the Metropolitan Transport Authority office as **Tomás Reinoso-Vial**, a 49-year-old Chief Systems Officer, prepares the Round-1 causal-loop-mapping commitment. Sixty-seven days separate him from the June 28 Metropolitan Council review.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Round-1 causal-loop-mapping commitment (six-node reinforcing-loop envelope with traffic, housing density, transit capacity, commute time, car ownership and emissions vs. single-node intervention framing) and diagnose when a systems-mapping envelope dominates a lane-by-lane envelope on the Congestion and Durability composite.
2. Analyze the Round-2 leverage-point selection commitment (congestion-charge + upzoning combo envelope that targets a system leverage point vs. new-lane envelope that treats the symptom) and recommend the combo envelope on the induced-demand-avoidance basis.
3. Diagnose the Round-3 transit-strike stress-reading commitment (loop-confirmation envelope that reads the strike as confirmation of the reinforcing loop vs. shock-anecdote dismissal that writes the strike off as an exogenous event) and recommend the confirmation envelope on the evidence-base and political-narrative basis.
4. Respond to the Round-4 month-2-worse-month-5-better stewardship commitment (political-stewardship envelope that funds citizen-communication and service-responsiveness through the worse-before-better window vs. panic-reversal envelope) and recommend the stewardship envelope on the political-durability basis.
5. Design the Round-5 second-order embedding commitment that converts the systems-mapping exercise from a one-off diagnostic into a permanent urban-systems capability with codified leverage-point register and quarterly causal-loop refresh.

## The Protagonist

Tomás Reinoso-Vial, 49, had assumed the Chief-Systems-Officer role at the Metropolitan Transport Authority on August 15, 2024, after fourteen years across urban-planning, transport-policy and system-dynamics leadership at two Latin American metropolitan authorities. He had, in 2019–2023, designed and landed a congestion-pricing-plus-upzoning combo inside a mid-size Andean metropolitan area that had, across an 18-month window, reduced Congestion from 1.71 to 1.19 without producing the feared political-durability collapse (political-durability had closed the cycle at 62). The Mayor had recruited him in June 2024 specifically on two capabilities: his documented discipline of treating urban-mobility problems as causal-loop systems rather than as linear-capacity problems, and his willingness to hold the stewardship cadence through the month-2-worse-month-5-better window. His 2026 Council-reviewed objectives tied 40% to the Congestion floor and 25% to the Political-Durability floor.

## The Decision

Reinoso-Vial had to commit, by the May 12 Council pre-review, the Round-1 envelope and the directional intent for Rounds 2–5.

1. **Round 1 — Causal-loop mapping.** Three archetypes: six-node-reinforcing (traffic, housing density, transit capacity, commute time, car ownership, emissions with fully-annotated arrow inventory, Composite 76), single-node-intervention (treat congestion as isolated variable — Composite 28, induced-demand blindness), or three-node (reduced-systems view — Composite 50). Working envelope: six-node-reinforcing.
2. **Round 2 — Leverage-point selection.** Three archetypes: congestion-charge + upzoning combo (target induced-demand loop at its leverage point, Composite 76), new-lane (USD 320M capacity add — Composite 24, induced-demand confirmation), or BRT-only (bus-rapid-transit corridor — Composite 54). Working envelope: congestion-charge + upzoning combo.
3. **Round 3 — Transit-strike stress reading.** Three vectors: loop-confirmation (read the nine-day strike and 2.4 Congestion spike as confirmation of the reinforcing loop, convert the data into the political narrative, Composite 78), shock-anecdote-dismissal (treat the strike as exogenous — Composite 30, narrative loss), or partial (acknowledge without narrative integration — Composite 52). Working envelope: loop-confirmation.
4. **Round 4 — Month-2-worse-month-5-better stewardship.** Three vectors: political-stewardship (fund citizen-communication weekly, publish the expected worse-before-better curve, hold the intervention, Composite 78), panic-reversal (suspend the charge on month-2 pressure — Composite 26), or phased (partial communication envelope — Composite 52). Working envelope: political-stewardship.
5. **Round 5 — Second-order embedding.** Three vectors: integrated-architecture (standing urban-systems committee, leverage-point register, quarterly causal-loop refresh, data-infrastructure integration, Composite 78), one-off (treat SystemsMap as a single exercise — Composite 32), or phased (integrated on three loops only — Composite 54). Working envelope: integrated-architecture.

On Reinoso-Vial's working model, a Six-Node / Combo / Confirmation / Stewardship / Integrated envelope delivered Congestion 1.18, Air Quality passing the WHO line on a 24-month horizon, Political Durability 62, Composite 72.

## Company Background

Ciudad Valparaíso had, across a 1990–2025 window, doubled its population from 620,000 to 1,240,000 while tripling its vehicle stock and growing its housing-footprint by a factor of 1.8. The 2019–2024 mobility-investment cycle had executed USD 620M of road-capacity addition across four projects — a six-lane perimetral ring, two radial corridors and a tunnel — and had produced the Congestion-Index trajectory from 1.42 to 1.78 documented above. The November 2025 internal review had diagnosed the trajectory as a textbook induced-demand pattern: each new lane had shortened commute time on a one-to-six-month window, had raised car-ownership intensity on a twelve-to-eighteen-month window, and had returned Congestion to or above the pre-project baseline inside a twenty-four-month window, all while raising emissions.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the March 2026 Council dossier. First, the January 2026 Ministry of Health advisory on the metropolitan PM2.5 exposure had triggered a regulatory review calendar that intersected the June 28 Council review. Second, the February 2026 nine-day transit strike had spiked Congestion to 2.4 and had collapsed the modal-share reading to 24%, a stress-event that the March 2026 planning diagnostic had read as confirmation of the reinforcing loop. Third, the March 2026 citizen-engagement survey had surfaced a 62% dissatisfaction reading concentrated in the three municipalities through which the radial corridors and the perimetral ring had been built — a political signal that the USD 620M programme had produced.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2018–2025 urban-mobility literature had, across 34 documented metropolitan interventions, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, interventions that had added road capacity without an accompanying demand-side lever had, in 78% of cases, documented Congestion-Index readings at or above the pre-project baseline inside a 24-month window — the induced-demand pattern. Second, interventions anchored on a congestion-charge + upzoning combo had sustained Congestion readings below the pre-project baseline in 74% of cases against 22% for BRT-only or lane-addition interventions. Third, interventions that had funded citizen-communication through the worse-before-better window had preserved Political-Durability above 58 in 72% of cases against 26% for panic-reversal responses.

Empirically, the post-2020 systems-thinking literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, causal-loop maps with five-to-seven nodes had, across a 2018–2024 sample of 28 metropolitan programmes, produced leverage-point identification accuracy 2.3× higher than single-node framings. Second, integrated second-order embeddings anchored on a standing committee, a leverage-point register and a quarterly causal-loop refresh had sustained Congestion-Index reductions below the pre-project baseline at the five-year mark in 76% of cases against 24% for one-off exercises.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The Council had, in the February 2026 plenary, approved the USD 48M envelope and the June 28 review calendar. The Director of Urban Planning had, on March 5, delivered the induced-demand diagnostic. The Director of Citizen Engagement had, on March 12, delivered the political-durability pack. The Director of Air-Quality-and-Environment had, on March 19, delivered the WHO-compliance dossier. The Chief Financial Officer (Metropolitan Authority) had, on March 26, delivered the leverage-point-cost-benefit pack.

### What Reinoso-Vial knew

The team had modelled three five-round envelopes. A Six-Node / Combo / Confirmation / Stewardship / Integrated envelope projected Composite 72. A Single-Node / Lane / Dismissal / Reversal / One-Off envelope projected Composite 22. A Hybrid envelope projected Composite 48.

### What he did not know

Whether the Ministry of Health advisory would escalate into a compliance order inside the envelope window; whether the congestion-charge legislation would clear the Council on the May 11 pre-review without amendment-driven perimeter dilution; whether the upzoning approval would survive the municipal-council ratification step across the three affected municipalities; and whether the citizen-communication cadence would hold through the expected month-2 congestion-and-commute worsening.

### What others were saying

**The Director of Urban Planning**: *"Six-node loop. The induced-demand pattern is the diagnosis. Another lane confirms the pattern we're trying to break."*

**The Director of Citizen Engagement**: *"Stewardship. The month-2 curve is the political test, not the engineering test."*

**The Director of Air-Quality-and-Environment**: *"The WHO line closes the window. The combo is the only lever that crosses it."*

**The Mayor**: *"Solve the loop. Not the lane. Hold the charge through month two."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Reinoso-Vial had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Six-Node / Combo / Confirmation / Stewardship / Integrated:** the recommended envelope. Congestion 1.18. Air Quality passing WHO line on 24-month horizon. Political Durability 62. Composite 72. Recommended.
- **Option B — Single-Node / Lane / Dismissal / Reversal / One-Off:** the legacy envelope. Composite 22. Rejected — induced-demand probability above 78%.
- **Option C — Hybrid / Phased / Phased / Phased / Phased:** the partial envelope. Composite 48. Rejected — combo architecture fragments under partial gating.

Reinoso-Vial's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single round most correlated with the final Composite was Round 4 — the month-2-worse-month-5-better stewardship — because it was the round that decided whether the congestion-charge + upzoning combo survived the predictable political pressure of the early-cycle congestion-and-commute worsening before the leverage-point dynamics began to deliver the lower Congestion Index.
