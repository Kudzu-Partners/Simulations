## Abstract

Aura Cosmetics was, in the case universe, a Mexico City-headquartered Gen-Z beauty brand operating primarily across Mexico with early 2025 expansions into Colombia and Chile, with USD 45M in 2025 revenue, a 2025 Quarterly Creator-Marketing Budget of USD 500K against a USD 1.8M annual envelope, a 2025 Brand-Sentiment reading of 71 on a composite scale against a Mexican Gen-Z-beauty peer-benchmark of 74 (composite captures earned-media sentiment, TikTok comment polarity and creator-audience survey readings), a 2025 Compliance-Score reading of 62 against a peer-benchmark of 76 on a PROFECO-disclosure audit basis, a 2025 Creator-Efficiency reading of 58 on a cost-per-engaged-reach composite, and a 2024–2025 creator roster concentrated 48% in two macro creators and one lead creator who had produced 38% of 2025 engaged reach. The Q4 2025 board had approved a USD 4.2M eighteen-month creator-economy-rebuild envelope — the CreatorEngine programme — and had positioned it as the brand's response to three overlapping pressures: a Q4 2025 PROFECO audit that had flagged six posts missing the mandatory #publicidad disclosure tag and had opened a USD 60K fine-risk exposure, a Q1 2026 lead-creator content event that had produced a 23-point Brand-Sentiment drop inside a 72-hour window, and a Q1 2026 board paper mandating a shift toward community-led organic growth as paid-creator budgets compressed. The Q1 2026 board had reaffirmed the USD 4.2M envelope and a Chief-Marketing-Officer-led five-stage commitment cycle against a Brand-Sentiment floor above 72, a Compliance-Score floor above 84, a Creator-Efficiency floor above 68, and a Composite CreatorEngine Score above 72. The case opens at 6:44 a.m. on Thursday, April 23, 2026, at the Aura Cosmetics Mexico City head office as **Ximena Cortés-Bautista**, a 36-year-old Chief Marketing Officer, prepares the Stage-1 creator-tier-mix commitment. Sixty-six days separate her from the June 28 board marketing-review session.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Evaluate the Stage-1 creator-tier-mix commitment (micro-anchored four-tier envelope across Nano, Micro, Macro and Mega creators vs. mega-concentrated envelope) and diagnose when the four-tier envelope dominates the mega-concentrated envelope on the Creator-Efficiency and Brand-Safety composite.
2. Analyze the Stage-2 authenticity-dial commitment (creator-led envelope that protects creator voice and absorbs a 15% brand-message-consistency give-back against a 32% engagement uplift vs. scripted envelope) and recommend the creator-led envelope on the Gen-Z trust basis.
3. Diagnose the Stage-3 PROFECO-disclosure-compliance commitment (mandatory-label envelope that codifies the #publicidad disclosure on all paid creator posts with a monthly audit cadence vs. voluntary envelope) and recommend the mandatory envelope on the fine-risk basis.
4. Respond to the Stage-4 creator-crisis-distancing commitment (distance-and-reallocate envelope that distances the brand from the lead-creator content event, reallocates the lead-creator budget share to the micro-creator roster and sustains the Stage-2 authenticity posture vs. terminate-or-defend envelopes) and recommend the distance-and-reallocate envelope on the Brand-Sentiment-recovery basis.
5. Design the Stage-5 community-led organic-transition commitment that converts CreatorEngine from a paid-creator budget into a permanent community-anchored growth capability with a UGC contest, a community hashtag inventory and a 30% paid-spend reduction envelope.

## The Protagonist

Ximena Cortés-Bautista, 36, had assumed the Chief-Marketing-Officer role at Aura Cosmetics on May 1, 2024, after eight years across influencer, creator and brand leadership at two Mexican D2C beauty brands and a Los Angeles-based Gen-Z accessories label. She had, in 2022–2023, led a creator-crisis recovery at a Mexican brand that had, through a distance-and-reallocate protocol, rebuilt Brand-Sentiment from 52 to 74 inside a 7-month window following a comparable lead-creator event. The Chief Executive Officer had recruited her in March 2024 specifically on two capabilities: her documented discipline of building micro-anchored creator rosters rather than concentrating on macro-or-mega creators, and her willingness to codify disclosure compliance on every paid creator post against creator-preferred ambiguity. ## The Decision

Cortés-Bautista had to commit, by the May 14 board pre-review, the Stage-1 envelope and the directional intent for Stages 2–5.

1. **Stage 1 — Creator-tier mix.** Three archetypes: micro-anchored-four-tier (Nano 10% / Micro 45% / Macro 30% / Mega 15%, four platforms covered, Composite 76), mega-concentrated (Mega 60% concentrated in two creators — Composite 26, brand-safety concentration), or nano-only (95% Nano — Composite 52, reach deficit). Working envelope: micro-anchored-four-tier.
2. **Stage 2 — Authenticity dial.** Three archetypes: creator-led (protect creator voice, absorb 15% brand-message-consistency give-back against 32% engagement uplift, Composite 78), scripted (brand-controlled voice — Composite 30, authenticity collapse), or semi-scripted (partial scripting — Composite 54). Working envelope: creator-led.
3. **Stage 3 — PROFECO-disclosure compliance.** Three vectors: mandatory-label (#publicidad disclosure on all paid creator posts, monthly audit cadence, terminate-on-repeat clause, Composite 78), voluntary (creator-preference basis — Composite 22, fine-risk exposure), or phased (mandatory on Macro/Mega only — Composite 52). Working envelope: mandatory-label.
4. **Stage 4 — Creator-crisis distancing.** Three vectors: distance-and-reallocate (public-distancing statement without contract termination, reallocate lead-creator budget share to micro-creator roster, preserve Stage-2 authenticity posture, Composite 78), terminate (contract-termination and public statement — Composite 40, community-backlash risk), or defend (public defence of the creator — Composite 18, Brand-Sentiment collapse). Working envelope: distance-and-reallocate.
5. **Stage 5 — Community-led organic transition.** Three vectors: integrated-architecture (UGC contest, community hashtag inventory, 30% paid-spend reduction envelope, community-moderation protocol, Composite 78), paid-only (preserve as paid-creator programme — Composite 32), or phased (UGC contest without hashtag inventory — Composite 54). Working envelope: integrated-architecture.

On Cortés-Bautista's working model, a Four-Tier / Creator-Led / Mandatory / Distance / Integrated envelope delivered Brand Sentiment 74, Compliance-Score 86, Creator-Efficiency 72, Composite 73.

## Company Background

Aura Cosmetics had been founded in Mexico City in 2020 and had, across six years, built a USD 45M-revenue Gen-Z beauty brand with a 42-SKU assortment anchored on clean-formulation colour cosmetics and skincare products and a distribution footprint of DTC website, Mercado Libre and 220 brick-and-mortar doors across Mexico plus early Colombia and Chile expansions. The 2022–2025 creator-marketing cycle had scaled the creator-roster to 68 active creators across four tiers, with 2025 engaged-reach concentrated 48% in two macro creators and 38% of engaged reach produced by a single lead creator — a posture the November 2025 internal review had flagged as structurally exposed to creator-crisis concentration. The Q4 2025 board-approved USD 4.2M CreatorEngine envelope had been positioned explicitly as a five-stage rebuild of the creator-roster mix, authenticity posture, compliance architecture, crisis-response protocol and organic-transition pathway.

Three Q1 2026 events were named in the March 2026 marketing-review dossier. First, the November 2025 PROFECO audit had flagged six 2025-posted pieces of paid creator content across three creators as missing the mandatory #publicidad disclosure tag, with a USD 60K aggregate fine-risk exposure and a 60-day remediation window that had closed in February 2026 through a brand-issued relabelling and creator retraining protocol. Second, the January 2026 lead-creator content event had produced a 23-point Brand-Sentiment drop across a 72-hour window as a lead-creator-originated post generated a community backlash independent of Aura Cosmetics' brand positioning. Third, the Q1 2026 board paper had mandated a shift toward community-led organic growth with a 30% paid-spend reduction as paid-creator budgets compressed across the category.

## Industry and Market Context

The 2022–2025 LatAm creator-economy literature had, across 32 documented Gen-Z-beauty and fashion brand deployments, produced three findings the envelope had to respect. First, rosters anchored on micro-tier concentration had documented Brand-Safety readings 2.4× more durable than rosters concentrated in mega or macro tiers. Second, creator-led authenticity postures had delivered engagement-uplift readings 24–38 points higher than scripted postures in 76% of cases, with brand-message-consistency readings 12–18 points lower — a trade-off the Gen-Z audience had, across the 2022–2025 cycle, repeatedly priced in favour of authenticity. Third, brands that had codified mandatory disclosure-compliance architectures had, across the 2022–2025 PROFECO enforcement cycle, documented fine-event rates 4.2× lower than brands on voluntary-compliance postures.

Empirically, the post-2022 creator-crisis literature had two findings the envelope had to respect. First, brands executing distance-and-reallocate protocols had delivered Brand-Sentiment recoveries of 18–28 points inside 6–9 months in 74% of cases against 26% for terminate-or-defend protocols. Second, integrated community-led organic-transition architectures anchored on UGC contests and community-hashtag inventories had sustained engaged-reach trajectories through paid-budget compressions in 76% of cases against 24% for paid-only programmes.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The board had, in the December 2025 session, approved the USD 4.2M envelope and the June 28 review calendar. The Head of Creator Partnerships had, on March 5, delivered the creator-roster diagnostic. The Head of Social had, on March 12, delivered the authenticity-and-engagement pack. The Chief Legal Officer had, on March 19, delivered the PROFECO-compliance dossier. The Head of Community had, on March 26, delivered the organic-transition brief.

### What Cortés-Bautista knew

The team had modelled three five-stage envelopes. A Four-Tier / Creator-Led / Mandatory / Distance / Integrated envelope projected Composite 73. A Mega-Concentrated / Scripted / Voluntary / Terminate / Paid-Only envelope projected Composite 22. A Hybrid envelope projected Composite 46.

### What she did not know

Whether the PROFECO would escalate the November 2025 audit into a follow-up enforcement posture on the 2026 creator-roster; whether the lead-creator community would accept the distance-and-reallocate posture without a second-wave backlash; whether the 30% paid-spend reduction window would generate sufficient community-led organic reach to clear the revenue plan; and whether the Colombian and Chilean expansion markets would absorb the Mexico-calibrated authenticity and compliance frame.

### What others were saying

**The Head of Creator Partnerships**: *"Micro-anchored roster. Concentration in two macros and a lead is how we wrote this crisis into our own roadmap."*

**The Head of Social**: *"Creator-led authenticity. Scripted content is the single fastest way to lose Gen Z."*

**The Chief Legal Officer**: *"Mandatory disclosure. PROFECO will not let a missing tag slide twice."*

**The Chief Executive Officer**: *"Creator marketing isn't a budget. It's a trust account. Build the community architecture. Pay the paid-spend give-back."*

---

## Options Under Consideration

Cortés-Bautista had narrowed the space to three envelopes.

- **Option A — Four-Tier / Creator-Led / Mandatory / Distance / Integrated:** the recommended envelope. Brand Sentiment 74. Compliance-Score 86. Creator-Efficiency 72. Composite 73. Recommended.
- **Option B — Mega-Concentrated / Scripted / Voluntary / Terminate / Paid-Only:** the legacy envelope. Composite 22. Rejected — brand-safety and PROFECO-enforcement probability above 72%.
- **Option C — Hybrid / Phased / Phased / Phased / Phased:** the partial envelope. Composite 46. Rejected — roster architecture fragments under partial gating.

Cortés-Bautista's private view was that Option A was the right envelope and that the single stage most correlated with the final Composite was Stage 4 — the creator-crisis distancing — because it was the stage that decided whether Aura Cosmetics survived the lead-creator concentration it had built during 2022–2025 without collapsing the authenticity posture that Stage 2 had committed the brand to.
