## Abstract

The República Argentina was, in the case universe, a 45-million-inhabitant Southern Cone economy running, as of April 2026, headline inflation at 82%, GDP growth at 0.8%, unemployment at 11.4%, international reserves at 2.8 months of imports, public debt at 135% of GDP, a crawling peg depreciating at 3.5% per month against an underlying inflation running at 6.5% per month, and a Banco Central policy rate of 97% against a Taylor-prescribed 112.4% — a country, in the case narrative, one policy error away from the next crisis. On **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 8:04 a.m., **Dr. Matías Rossi-Fernández**, a 51-year-old incoming Director of the Consejo de Política Económica arriving on his fourteenth day in the role, opened the 4-KPI dashboard his chief economist had refreshed at 11:58 p.m. the previous evening and sat down to commit the four-round stabilization package in front of a 10:30 a.m. joint meeting with the Minister of Economy, the BCRA Governor and the IMF mission chief.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this case discussion, participants will be able to:

1. Apply Hicks's 1937 IS-LM framework to the Argentine −3.2% output-gap reading, articulate the asymmetric fiscal-tightening / monetary-loosening sequencing problem under high inflation, and articulate why the equilibrium is unstable when expectations are backward-looking.
2. Apply Phillips's 1958 and Friedman-Phelps's 1968 expectations-augmented Phillips-curve framework to Argentine inertial inflation, diagnose the wage-contract backward-indexation channel, and articulate why the inertia breaks only under a credible institutional anchor and not under a policy-mix adjustment alone.
3. Apply Mundell-Fleming's 1962 impossible-trinity framework to the crawling-peg-plus-capital-controls-plus-constrained-monetary-independence corner Argentina currently occupies, quantify the 3.0-percentage-point real-appreciation bleed, and articulate why the reserves drain is a trilemma consequence, not a speculative event.
4. Apply Taylor's 1993 monetary-policy rule to the Argentine inflation-gap and output-gap readings, quantify the 15-point accommodative-monetary-policy gap, and articulate why the BCRA's de-facto rule systematically under-prescribes relative to the Taylor benchmark and feeds the inflation it is nominally fighting.
5. Apply Blanchard-Leigh's 2013 state-contingent fiscal-multiplier finding to the −18% soy-export commodity-shock scenario, quantify the context-dependent multiplier under high debt and Ricardian expectations, commit the Round 4 IMF-program credibility fork between Path A (expectations-roll-down) and Path B (speculative-attack), and articulate why the macroeconomics frame sim 311 occupies is distinct from Macroeconomic Volatility (sim 064) and BoP Tracker (sim 251) — 311 emphasises the cabinet-council policymaker-lens synthesis across IS-LM, Phillips, Mundell-Fleming, Taylor and Blanchard-Leigh in an Argentine stabilization sandbox.

On the morning of **Thursday, April 23, 2026**, at 8:04 a.m., **Dr. Matías Rossi-Fernández** arrived at the Consejo-de-Política-Económica floor of the Ministerio de Economía building on Hipólito Yrigoyen and walked into the Director's office past the wall of four framed sepia-and-colour photographs (the 1989 hyperinflation stabilization programme; the 2001 convertibility collapse; the 2018 stand-by arrangement with the IMF; the 2024 Comprehensive Economic Programme letter of intent). The 4-KPI dashboard had opened on the laptop. Inflation 82.0%. Growth 0.8%. Unemployment 11.4%. Reserves 2.8 months. Debt 135% of GDP. Next to the dashboard sat a two-page brief from the chief economist: the 10:30 a.m. agenda, the 4:00 p.m. BCRA board vote, the 6:00 p.m. IMF mission-chief dinner, and the midnight cabinet sign-off window. Rossi-Fernández was on day 14 of 120 of the stabilization arc.

---

## The Protagonist

Rossi-Fernández, 51, had been Director of the Consejo de Política Económica since April 10, 2026 — fourteen days into role at the case opening — had completed a Licenciatura en Economía at the Universidad de Buenos Aires in 1998 and a PhD in Economics at MIT in 2006 under a monetary-economics dissertation supervised by the Phillips-curve-and-expectations-anchor school, had served as chief economist at a Buenos-Aires macroeconomic-research institute between 2007 and 2018, had been Sub-Secretary of Macroeconomic Coordination at the Ministerio de Economía between 2019 and 2023 through the peso-stabilization cycle of 2020, and had spent 2024 and 2025 as senior fellow at an international policy institute writing on LATAM stabilization sequencing. His policy instincts had been shaped by three formative exposures: the MIT monetary-economics cohort of 2003–2006; the 2020 peso-stabilization episode where the crawling-peg-plus-capital-controls configuration held because the fiscal anchor held; and the 2024 Argentine hyperinflation sequence where the fiscal anchor broke and the trilemma collapsed in under sixty days.

---

## The Decision

Rossi-Fernández had to commit, by the midnight cabinet sign-off window, the four-round stabilization package.

1. **Round 1 — IS-LM and Phillips.** Accept the −3.2% output gap. Commit the tight-fiscal / sequenced-monetary posture with the credible expectations anchor. Break the inertia.
2. **Round 2 — Mundell-Fleming and Taylor.** Commit the crawling-peg-plus-capital-controls-plus-constrained-monetary configuration. Close the 15-point Taylor gap. Align BCRA with the rule.
3. **Round 3 — Commodity shock.** Absorb the −18% soy-export shock under Blanchard-Leigh with a state-contingent multiplier. Keep the fiscal anchor visible.
4. **Round 4 — IMF program credibility.** Commit the Path-A package that produces the expectations-roll-down and the reserves recovery. Reject Path B.

Commitments: the Round 1 fiscal-monetary sequencing; the Round 2 trilemma-and-Taylor alignment; the Round 3 state-contingent multiplier posture; the Round 4 IMF-program credibility package; and a one-page cabinet memo that frames the four rounds as a single 120-day stabilization arc with the no-second-crisis operational constraint as the forcing function.

## Sectoral Background

The 1985–2025 Argentine stabilization record had, in the case universe, registered six documented stabilization cycles (the 1985 Austral plan; the 1991 Convertibility; the 2002 Lavagna stabilization; the 2016 gradualism; the 2018 IMF stand-by; the 2024 Comprehensive Economic Programme), with documented first-year success correlated at approximately 0.74 with a credible fiscal anchor, at approximately 0.66 with a sequenced monetary-tightening path, and at approximately 0.81 with an IMF-program credibility reading by the twelfth month. The 2024–2026 Argentine stabilization cycle had entered the programme with a 135%-debt-to-GDP starting point — the highest of any post-1985 Argentine stabilization — and with a 2.8-months-reserves starting point — the lowest.

## Industry and Market Context

The 1937–2025 macroeconomics literature produced four findings the simulation had to respect. First, Hicks's 1937 IS-LM synthesis, combined with Phillips's 1958 and Friedman-Phelps's 1968 expectations-augmented Phillips-curve framework, had produced documented stabilization-sequencing readings where a tight-fiscal / sequenced-monetary posture under a credible anchor produced first-year inflation declines of 45–65% in panel-match emerging-market episodes and 10–20% under anchor-broken episodes. Second, Mundell-Fleming's 1962 impossible-trinity framework, applied to the crawling-peg configuration, had documented real-exchange-rate-appreciation rates at approximately 3.0 percentage points per month of inflation-peg-speed gap. Third, Taylor's 1993 monetary-policy rule had produced documented central-bank-rule-alignment readings correlated at approximately 0.71 with inflation-expectations-stability readings twelve months forward. Fourth, Blanchard and Leigh's 2013 state-contingent fiscal-multiplier finding had documented multiplier readings ranging from 0.4 to 1.7 depending on debt-to-GDP starting level, Ricardian-household share, and import-leakage intensity.

## The Situation

### How we got here

The 2018 IMF stand-by arrangement; the 2020 peso-stabilization episode; the 2022 real-appreciation bleed; the 2024 hyperinflation sequence; the March 2026 Comprehensive Economic Programme letter of intent; the April 10 Rossi-Fernández arrival as incoming Director; the April 15–22 four-round framework briefing; and the April 23 cabinet commitment were the sequence.

### What Rossi-Fernández knew

The IS-LM output-gap reading at −3.2%; the Phillips-curve inertial-inflation decomposition with the backward-looking wage-indexation channel at approximately 68% of the headline; the Mundell-Fleming trilemma configuration with the crawling peg at 3.5% against 6.5% inflation; the Taylor-Rule 15-point accommodative-policy gap; the Blanchard-Leigh state-contingent multiplier reading at 0.7 under 135% debt; the −18% soy-shock scenario with approximately USD 4.8bn of annualised foreign-exchange-earnings loss; the IMF mission-chief positive-tone read; and the cabinet's appetite for a 120-day commitment with the no-second-crisis constraint.

### What he did not know

Whether the wage-contract counterparties (CGT, Asociación Empresaria Argentina) would accept the sequenced-anchor posture or would renegotiate indexation formulas before the signal cleared; whether the BCRA board would accept the 15-point Taylor-rate alignment in a single move or would phase it; whether the soy-export shock would absorb at the projected Blanchard-Leigh multiplier reading or would trigger a wider demand shortfall; whether the IMF mission chief would sign on Path A or would condition on additional fiscal adjustments that the cabinet would not absorb; whether the peso would hold the crawling-peg corridor or would break to a free-float; and whether the 120-day arc would deliver on month-four inflation reading below 4% per month.

### What others were saying

**The Minister of Economy (58)**: *"Matías, ciento veinte días. La ventana no se reabre. Si rompemos el ancla fiscal, rompemos el programa."*

**The BCRA Governor (54)**: *"La regla de Taylor prescribe ciento doce cuatro. El directorio vota ciento diez esta tarde. El mercado lee la diferencia y acierta."*

**The IMF mission chief (47)**: *"The programme signs on Path A. We need the credibility anchor in writing by June 15. No anchor, no disbursement, no reserves."*

**The CGT secretary-general (63)**: *"Cada punto de inflación que cae sin salario real que sube, lo pagamos en la calle."*

**The soy-producer association (representing ~USD 26bn export complex)**: *"Minus dieciocho por ciento de precio. Si la política cambia la paridad, dejamos de embarcar."*

**The 45m Argentine public (aggregate signal through the inflation-expectations survey)**: *"El dólar es lo que medimos. Lo que se aparta del dólar no lo creemos."*

## Options Under Consideration

Rossi-Fernández had narrowed the stabilization-policy space to three commitment postures.

- **Option A — Tight-fiscal anchor + sequenced-monetary tightening + crawling-peg corridor + Blanchard-Leigh-calibrated soy absorption + Path-A IMF program (Recommended).** Respects the IS-LM sequencing; breaks the Phillips-curve inertia through the credibility anchor; closes the Taylor gap over 60 days; absorbs the soy shock at a 0.7 state-contingent multiplier; commits Path A with a June-15 anchor milestone. Projected first-year inflation reading at 38%, reserves recovery to 5.5 months by month 12, debt-to-GDP stabilisation at 128%.
- **Option B — Loose-fiscal + aggressive-monetary-only + free-float peso + fiscal stimulus under soy shock + delayed IMF program.** Rejected — violates the IS-LM sequencing; the Phillips-curve inertia does not break under a monetary-only anchor; the Taylor-gap closure is undone by the fiscal expansion; the Blanchard-Leigh multiplier under 135% debt reads sub-unity and the stimulus is absorbed by Ricardian households; Path-B risk at approximately 68% probability and reserves bleed to sub-one-month.
- **Option C — Gradualist-on-both-fronts + crawling-peg-accelerated + no-shock-adjustment + IMF-program-kept-alive.** Defensible on political-economy grounds but under-delivers the credibility anchor; the Phillips-curve inertia persists; the Taylor gap widens in the soy-shock aftermath; Path-B risk at approximately 41% probability.

Rossi-Fernández's private view was that Option A was the right commitment and that the single decision most correlated with the twelve-month stabilization outcome was the Round 1 credibility-anchor-in-writing by June 15, because the 1985–2025 panel had shown that first-year inflation declines of 45–65% correlated at 0.81 with an anchor-credibility reading at month six, and because his 2024 policy experience had shown that a missed anchor-credibility window regenerated the inertial-inflation channel within ninety days. The *País en Tus Manos* frame sits at the cabinet-council policymaker-lens synthesis tier in the 2026 Express catalogue, distinct from Macroeconomic Volatility (sim 064, volatility-management frame) and BoP Tracker (sim 251, central-bank-balance-of-payments frame) — 311 emphasises the four-framework synthesis (IS-LM, Phillips, Mundell-Fleming, Taylor, Blanchard-Leigh) inside a single stabilization-sequencing decision.
